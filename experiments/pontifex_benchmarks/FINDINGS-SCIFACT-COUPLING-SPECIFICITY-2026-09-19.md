# Pontifex SciFact coupling-specificity null findings

## Status

**Completed prospective mechanism follow-up.** Protocol: `PROTOCOL-SCIFACT-COUPLING-SPECIFICITY-2026-09-19.md`.

Run: <https://github.com/franklinbaldo/papers/actions/runs/35449589119>

Artifact: <https://github.com/franklinbaldo/papers/actions/runs/35449589119/artifacts/10586639067>

This test was motivated by the earlier residual-side ablation and is therefore not an independent confirmation of the original Pontifex hypothesis.

## Information boundary

**Leakage audit: PASS.** K was fixed at 256, with 31 pre-generated null permutations and seed `20260919`. The map and null bank were frozen before test relevance grades were loaded. No SciFact task labels were used for fit or selection, and target-space B coordinates were not generated for test queries or corpus documents.

## Result

| Condition | nDCG@10 |
|---|---:|
| TRUE correspondence | 0.645311 |
| COUPLED-NULL median | 0.642651 |
| COUPLED-NULL mean | 0.643434 |
| INDEPENDENT-NULL median | 0.634310 |
| INDEPENDENT-NULL mean | 0.633493 |

The two planned questions separate cleanly.

### Does the correct semantic residual-to-anchor correspondence beat a shared scrambled warp?

Not reliably in this run.

- `TRUE - COUPLED-NULL median = +0.002661` nDCG@10;
- finite-bank upper-tail p = `0.3125`;
- paired-query bootstrap mean `TRUE - COUPLED = +0.001878`;
- 95% interval `[-0.007845, +0.011360]`;
- bootstrap probability mean > 0 = `0.652`.

The correct correspondence point estimate is positive, but the evidence is insufficient to claim semantic-specificity of the local residual mapping at K=256.

### Does applying the same warp to both sides matter even when correspondence is wrong?

Yes, more clearly.

- `COUPLED-NULL median - INDEPENDENT-NULL median = +0.008340` nDCG@10;
- paired-query bootstrap mean `COUPLED - INDEPENDENT = +0.009941`;
- 95% interval `[+0.001884, +0.018939]`;
- bootstrap probability mean > 0 = `0.993`.

This supports a **shared-warp coherence** effect: keeping query and document transformations coupled preserves retrieval structure substantially better than independently scrambling the two sides.

The `TRUE - INDEPENDENT` bootstrap mean is `+0.011819`, with interval `[-0.000112, +0.024133]`, which is suggestive but still barely includes zero.

## Interpretation

This is evidence **against a strong claim that the K=256 residual-side interaction is specifically caused by learning the correct local A→B semantic transport**. A large part of the interaction can be generated by applying the same nonlinear deformation coherently to both sides of retrieval, even when the anchor-to-residual identities are scrambled.

That does not make the result useless. It identifies a real mechanism boundary:

- two-sided coupling matters;
- correct semantic correspondence has not yet earned a distinct downstream contribution at K=256;
- therefore the next benchmark work should prioritize external utility and stronger information-matched baselines rather than elaborate the local-warp story from this result.

No physical torus, causal semantic locality, or universal transport superiority follows from this null.
