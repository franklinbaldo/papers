# Pontifex NFCorpus local shared-warp replication K=256

## Status

**Completed preregistered independent-dataset replication; primary result negative.**

Preregistration commit: `aa33ff96f18e363247977604e3491d281f00104d`  
Run: <https://github.com/franklinbaldo/papers/actions/runs/35466980084>

The protocol, implementation, split roles, nuisance construction, seeds, locality threshold, and primary decision rule were committed before the first NFCorpus `D_test` relevance-grade read. NFCorpus changes the held-out dataset while retaining the same frozen MiniLM -> MPNet representation pair used in the SciFact mechanism series; this is therefore an independent **dataset** replication, not an independent representation-pair replication.

## Information boundary

- `D_assembly`: canonical BEIR NFCorpus archive, corpus/query text, split membership, and frozen encoder revisions. Relevance grades were not used for fitting or selection.
- `D_student`: deterministic 80% of exact-test-text-overlap-filtered train queries. It supplied A/B coordinates for the K=256 anchors, Procrustes fit, residual vectors, eight residual-L2 strata, and both local nuisance banks.
- `D_val`: remaining 20% of filtered train queries; A/B coordinates used only for coordinate-space `tau` / `lambda` selection.
- `D_test`: 323 official NFCorpus test queries. Relevance grades were loaded only after map, selector output, strata, nuisance banks, locality calibration, manifests, and A-side mapped-coordinate ingredients were frozen.
- B coordinates for test queries and corpus documents were never encoded.
- Task labels/qrel grades used for fitting or selection: zero.

Frozen selector: `tau=0.1`, `lambda=1.5`, with `D_val` coordinate loss `0.4450762`.

## Local nuisance calibration

Both 31-member nuisance banks destroy exact residual identity within eight residual-norm strata. Candidate perfect derangements were accepted only if they captured at least 50% of the random-to-minimum-cost locality improvement in normalized A-space.

Observed calibration was much tighter than the minimum requirement:

| quantity | query bank | document bank |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| minimum-cost mean A-cosine distance | 0.652204 | 0.652204 |
| random-within-stratum expectation | 0.855995 | 0.855995 |
| bank mean A-cosine distance | 0.652270 | 0.652275 |
| bank max A-cosine distance | 0.652413 | 0.652373 |
| mean locality capture | 0.999673 | 0.999649 |
| minimum locality capture | 0.998973 | 0.999168 |

The query and document banks were disjoint and frozen under separate RNG streams. Every accepted assignment came from the first preregistered jitter level (`0.01`). Thus the primary test compares shared versus independent deformations under a nuisance family extremely close to the strongest feasible local derangement, rather than under a global shuffle.

## Held-out result

| condition | NFCorpus nDCG@10 |
| --- | ---: |
| TRUE | 0.318457 |
| LOCAL_COUPLED mean | 0.314782 |
| LOCAL_INDEPENDENT mean | 0.314777 |

The preregistered primary contrast was:

`LOCAL_COUPLED - LOCAL_INDEPENDENT = +0.0000053` nDCG@10.

The 5,000-resample paired-query bootstrap over 323 held-out test queries gave 95% CI `[-0.000329,+0.000356]`, with bootstrap probability of positive mean `0.5056`. The interval crosses zero essentially symmetrically. Therefore the predeclared replication criterion **fails**: the SciFact shared-warp advantage did not replicate on NFCorpus under this highly local nuisance design.

The secondary exact-identity contrast was `TRUE - LOCAL_COUPLED = +0.003674`, with paired-query 95% CI `[-0.001637,+0.009323]` and bootstrap probability of positive mean `0.9076`. This also does not support exact residual identity under an interval-above-zero rule, but it was not the preregistered replication target.

## Interpretation boundary

This result is **falsification pressure on cross-dataset robustness of the SciFact shared-warp mechanism claim**. It does not erase the four positive SciFact coupled-versus-independent results; instead, it narrows them to that dataset/null regime until another independent replication succeeds.

The particularly tight NFCorpus banks also expose a useful mechanistic possibility: once null residuals are constrained to nearly minimum-cost local substitutions, coupled and independent banks may implement almost indistinguishable regional fields. That is a hypothesis generated by this result, not an explanation established by it. A future discriminant should vary locality strength prospectively and test for a coupling-by-locality interaction rather than choosing a threshold after observing these test labels.

This run is not evidence for or against an intrinsic/physical torus, causal semantic locality, universal transport superiority, native-B superiority, low-budget dominance, broad cross-representation generalization, or `D_assembly -> D_student` generalization.
