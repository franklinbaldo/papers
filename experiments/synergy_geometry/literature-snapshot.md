# Synergy–Geometry literature snapshot

This is a compact working source list, not an exhaustive bibliography. The submission-time novelty audit must be refreshed.

- Smolensky (1990), *Tensor Product Variable Binding and the Representation of Symbolic Structures in Connectionist Systems* — explicit higher-order role/filler binding.
- Plate (1995), *Holographic Reduced Representations* — fixed-dimensional binding/superposition precedent.
- Fauconnier & Turner (2002), *The Way We Think* — conceptual blending and emergent structure in blends.
- Williams & Beer (2010), *Nonnegative Decomposition of Multivariate Information* — PID foundation; target-relative synergy is prior art. https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.2515
- Bertschinger et al. (2014), *Quantifying Unique Information* — alternative PID formalization; reinforces measure-dependence.
- Lengerich et al. (2020), *Purifying Interaction Effects with the Functional ANOVA* — interaction identification is distribution-relative. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/lengerich20a.html
- Roeder, Metz & Kingma (2021), *On Linear Identifiability of Learned Representations* — representation claims require explicit transformation/gauge discipline.
- Guo et al. (2025), *Quantifying Compositionality of Classic and State-of-the-Art Embeddings* — additive compositionality and unseen combinations across layers. https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1206/
- Aljaafari, Carvalho & Freitas (2026), *Where Do LLMs Compose Meaning?* — composition is distributed across transformer depth and can be studied interventionally. https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.214/
- Aljaafari, Carvalho & Freitas (2026), *Bridging Linguistic Structure and Mechanistic Interpretability for Conceptual Interpretation in Language Models* — causal tracing linked to semantic-role structure. https://aclanthology.org/2026.conll-main.44/
- Dai, Heinzerling & Inui (2026), *Cell-Based Representation of Relational Binding in Language Models* — strongest direct mechanistic collision: low-dimensional relation/binding geometry, cross-context transfer, two model families, activation patching. https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2194/
- Fang, Zhang, Tao & Chan (2026), *Towards Understanding Modality Interaction in Multimodal Language Models via Partial Information Decomposition* — PID synergy used in contemporary multimodal transformers with layerwise/intervention analyses. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00959

The programme is not novel if it merely reproduces any one of these established phenomena under the name “interaction geometry.”
