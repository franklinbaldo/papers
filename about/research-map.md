# Locality rule

Experiments, formalizations and prior-art audits belong beside the research
line they support. Global folders are reserved for genuinely transversal
concerns.

# Archived apparatus

The former `yesindeed/`, `otherwise/` and `synthesis/` trees and the
embedding-seeded tournament are preserved under
[Adversarial Paper Development](/archive/adversarial-paper-development/).
Historical identifiers such as `paper1g`, `phase3`, session and round
numbers remain inside that archive because there they are part of the record,
not canonical names for current research.
