# Experiment

This archive preserves the apparatus that iteratively attacked, defended and
synthesized drafts through `otherwise`, `yesindeed` and `synthesis`.
It produced useful stress tests and vocabulary corrections, but its marginal
returns declined and it is no longer treated as an active research line.

# Historical naming

Names such as `paper1b`, `paper1g`, `phase3`, session numbers and round
numbers are intentionally retained inside the archive. They are contemporaneous
experimental identifiers. Active papers now use semantic paths.
