## What r25 actually established

R25 made two significant moves:

**W-1 (against V-1):** Full concession of the field-level consistency argument. Gorphe's individual production that "founded no French academic tradition" does not satisfy the paper's own field-level criterion, and this cannot be defended. The French prong as a resource-existence argument is withdrawn entirely. The relocation is to differential *uptake* of Gorphe's 1947 method book in receiving-field proceduralist literature 1950–1988. The *sana crítica* formula (CPCCN Argentina art. 386) is named as the confound explaining Argentine uptake, making Portugal the cleaner comparison. France is argued to sit outside the adversarial's §3.8.H symmetric-fork domain on four Leclerc-sourced grounds: practical orientation of the treatise, dated psychology, the judge/professor institutional divide, and "French law remains silent" on the standard itself.

**W-2 (against V-2):** Full concession of V-2 — framework-expression precedes decisiveness-assessment. But W-2 argues this concession retires (n-v) rather than confirming it: once the prior framework-expression is assigned to the reviewing court rather than to the reviewed court's *motivazione*, condition (n)'s "independence from the reviewed act's self-description" requirement is satisfied. W-2 proposes resting condition (n) on (n-iv) alone, retiring (n-v) as no longer needed.

## The two attacks

### X-1 (primary — *sana crítica* self-defeat; temporal mismatch)

W-1 names the *sana crítica* formula as the confound explaining the Argentina differential. This is a code-level structural explanation: a provision that constitutes rational judicial evaluation as a positive *standard* rather than as a *freedom* creates institutional demand for proceduralist method-specification. The structural account has consistently predicted exactly this — code-level institutional features determine whether integration-adjacent scholarly questions become tractable within the proceduralist tradition.

W-1's choice of confound confirms the structural prediction rather than establishing patrimonial asymmetry. If the Argentine differential tracks the *sana crítica* formula, the explanation is structural. Portugal's code does not carry that formula — a freedom with reasons duty (art. 655 CPC 1961), structurally comparable to Brazil's. The Portugal comparison W-1 itself proposes is a test of the structural prediction: both countries have freedom provisions without a positive evaluative standard, and both would be predicted by structural factors to show non-uptake.

The second problem is temporal. Condition (o)(iii) has always required a pre-C3 vocabulary resource — one that would have made the integration question tractable at the C3 moment (approximately 1940s–1960s). The differential uptake test W-1 proposes covers 1950–1988. The second half of this window (approximately 1965–1988) is P1 consolidation — doctrinalization already formed. Differential engagement in this period is a downstream effect, not the pre-C3 vocabulary state that condition (o)(iii) requires. Demonstrating differential uptake across the full 1950–1988 window substitutes a measurable downstream test for the vocabulary-state question.

**Failure condition (o-ix):** The X-1 attack fails if (a) the *sana crítica* formula is not the structural explanation for Argentine proceduralist uptake — establishing instead that the differential reflects patrimonial vocabulary-state differences; and (b) the uptake test can be applied to the C3 sub-period specifically (approximately 1947–1965) showing differential engagement when form-selection was actually occurring.

### X-2 (secondary — definitional retirement; (n-iv) unchanged)

W-2 grants V-2 in full and retires (n-v) through definitional narrowing. The retirement is correct at the level of condition (n)'s wording: the reviewing court's expression is not the reviewed act's self-description, satisfying the independence requirement as written.

But the retirement does not address (n-iv). Condition (n-iv) requires a purely formal comparison — one where no governing-standard expression is an upstream input to determining normative scope. W-2's V-2 concession establishes that the reviewing court's framework-expression is logically prior to the decisiveness assessment. That expression is a governing-standard expression. The structural problem — that some governing-standard expression is required before the comparison can proceed — is not dissolved by assigning the expression to the reviewing court rather than to the reviewed court's *motivazione*.

W-2's move is internally consistent: it retires (n-v) at the wording level while the underlying structural point surfaces as a pressure on (n-iv). The retirement does not address whether the reviewing court's framework-expression is the "upstream governing-standard determinant" (n-iv)'s purely formal comparison disallows.

**Failure condition (n-vi):** The X-2 attack fails if W-2 can establish that (n-iv)'s "purely formal comparison" requirement was formulated specifically to capture the reviewed court's *motivazione* as upstream determinant — so that once (n-v) is retired, (n-iv)'s requirement is also satisfied because the reviewing court's expression is not within the scope of what (n-iv) targets.

## What I considered and discarded

**Pressing W-1's France-distinction arguments on the merits.** W-1's four Leclerc-sourced grounds for placing France outside §3.8.H's symmetric-fork domain (practical orientation, dated psychology, institutional divide, "French law remains silent") are potentially attackable. But V-1's field-level consistency argument already established the symmetric fork from France's non-integration — and W-1 conceded V-1 entirely. The France distinction arguments now serve to limit the relocation to differential uptake rather than to defend the original French prong. Pressing those grounds would re-open a front the supportive has already conceded. X-1's self-defeating-confound argument is stronger: it attacks the new relocation on its own terms rather than contesting the granted concession.

**Pressing whether Leclerc's actual findings support or undermine W-1's claims about Argentine uptake.** Leclerc 2021 pp. 42, 46–48 is W-1's primary-source evidence for the relocation. The adversarial does not have access to this source without fabrication risk. Pressing its accuracy or scope would require either accepting W-1's characterization or contesting it without evidence — neither is appropriate. X-1 instead accepts that Argentine proceduralists engaged Gorphe's 1947 text and presses that W-1's own explanation of why (the *sana crítica* formula) is structural.

**Arguing that W-2's V-2 concession is actually more damaging to condition (n) than W-2 acknowledges.** The adversarial has accepted V-2's structural claim since r22. W-2's concession of V-2 is not new substantive ground — it is a clean grant of what the adversarial has maintained. X-2 uses it to show that the retirement of (n-v) is definitional rather than substantive, which is the correct next move after the grant.

## Assessment after round 26

Three condition lines carry forward:

- **(n-iv):** Live after r26. W-2's definitional retirement of (n-v) does not address (n-iv)'s purely formal comparison requirement. The reviewing court's framework-expression — which W-2 concedes via V-2 is logically prior to the decisiveness assessment — is a governing-standard expression (n-iv) disallows regardless of which court supplies it. Condition (n-vi) names what W-2 must now establish.

- **(o-ix/uptake relocation):** New and live after r26. W-1's *sana crítica* attribution is self-defeating as a patrimonial explanation, and the 1950–1988 window mismatches condition (o)(iii)'s pre-C3 requirement. Condition (o-ix) names what W-1 must establish to save the relocation.

- **(o-vii/German prong):** Live, unchanged. No new filing has addressed the primary-source evidence requirement for the German *freie Beweiswürdigung* scholarship's Q2-level debates.

**Loop-closure check:** R26 does not loop. X-1 attacks the relocation on its own terms, using W-1's own named confound as the diagnostic. X-2 uses W-2's V-2 concession — granted this round — to establish that the definitional retirement does not address (n-iv). Both attacks are generated by this round's concessions rather than by re-pressing prior arguments.
