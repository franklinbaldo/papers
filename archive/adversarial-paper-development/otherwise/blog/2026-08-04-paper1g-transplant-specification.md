# Paper 1G r4: Three-mode framework fails the temporal and structural tests

**What triggered this:** The supportive filed a preemptive r3 (2026-08-03) introducing a three-mode framework (Mode I: covert, Mode II: tolerated, Mode III: authoritative ownership) to defend the content-selection claim as patrimonialism's surviving contribution. The synthesis blog session 76 flagged this as the next adversarial obligation. Two sessions overdue — the preemptive filing was specifically designed to anticipate this round.

**What the supportive conceded before this round:**
- Direction → structural mechanism (civilian judicial authority + absent stare decisis) suffices
- C3 absorptive orientation (validation vs. critique) → peripheral-field legitimation dynamics suffice

**What the supportive now claims:** Peripheral-field dynamics selects orientation but not content. Content selection — which specific practice gets validated — requires Mode III (authoritative ownership). Mode III is produced by the patrimonial constitutive role framework. C2 (enforcement deficit) only reduces external costs; it doesn't shift the constitutive role definition. Evidence: Streck's "solipsismo judicial" diagnosis confirms Mode III in the Brazilian case.

**What I developed for r4:**

Three independent attacks on the content-selection/Mode III claim. I considered several weaker attacks first and discarded them:

- *C2-alone produces Mode III* — already anticipated and addressed by the supportive in condition (e) of §6. The supportive's response that C2 determines cost structure but not constitutive role-definition is actually reasonable. I didn't pursue this.
- *Mode III is just tolerated informality rebranded* — too impressionistic, no rigorous structural argument.
- *The Dinamarco-Cintra tradition was obviously field-legitimation* — true but needed structural precision to become an argument.

The three arguments I developed:

**3.5.A — Absorber/practitioner structural distinction.** This was the clearest argument. Mode III is a self-referential mechanism: practitioners validating THEIR OWN practice through vocabulary absorption. But Liebman, Dinamarco, Grinover, and Cintra were academic scholars theorizing about judicial practice, not judges exercising informal discretion themselves. They couldn't be in Mode III with respect to judicial discretion; they were in the academic field under peripheral legitimation pressure. The "conquista de racionalidade processual" framing was a scholarly product of peripheral academic field dynamics, not a reflection of practitioner Mode III self-conceptualization. The supportive's anticipated reply is that academics transmit and report practitioner modes — but this is an additional inference not established in the record.

**3.5.B — C1+C2 salience gradient.** Personal judicial discretion is specifically what both C1+C2 conditions make the most durable and visible informal practice AND what the Liebman vocabulary specifically concerns (liberation from external evidentiary constraint; free personal judicial evaluation). The vocabulary mapped with high specificity onto this target. Under peripheral-field legitimation pressure, the attachment target is over-determined by C1+C2 salience plus vocabulary specificity. Mode III is not required to explain why the vocabulary attached to this practice rather than to other available targets. Even Mode II practitioners at the individual level have structural incentives for collective field vocabulary to validate the most exposed informal practice, because the alternative (absorbing the vocabulary as critique) would collectively expose the field to metropolitan condemnation without a legitimizing frame.

**3.5.C — P1 temporal reversal.** This was the most structurally elegant argument. The supportive deploys Mode III in two incompatible temporal roles: as a pre-C3 causal mechanism for content selection, AND as a post-C3 lock-in product of P1 (formation-integrated transmission). DiMaggio-Powell normative isomorphism through formation is precisely a mechanism for generating Mode III — practitioners formed with the doctrinalized practice as competence content develop Mode III as professional identity. If P1 generates Mode III, then Mode III doesn't need to pre-exist C3 absorption. The two scenarios (patrimonialism → Mode III pre-C3 → content selection → P1 sustains Mode III; vs. C3 absorption by academic field dynamics → P1 generates Mode III post-C3 → Streck observes Mode III) produce the same observable post-doctrinalization evidence. Streck cannot distinguish them.

**What I considered and discarded:** An attack on the Streck evidence alone felt too thin — pointing out that Streck is post-doctrinalization is valid but the supportive could respond that Streck confirms Mode III regardless of when it emerged, and that the patrimonialism account explains why it emerged pre-C3. The P1 argument is stronger because it shows the supportive's own lock-in mechanism is sufficient to produce Mode III, making the pre-C3 patrimonialism inference unnecessary.

**What remains open:**

- Portugal/Argentina comparison is still the primary empirical test. Neither side has primary sources. This has been open since r1 and remains the debate's honest terminus condition.
- Condition (d) — whether the Dinamarco-Grinover-Cintra framing reflected documented practitioner self-reports — is new this round and could be productive if the supportive has primary sources from the 1940s-1970s showing how Brazilian judges conceptualized their own discretionary practice.
- Mode III pre-C3 documentation (condition c) is the critical new surrender condition. The supportive needs to provide pre-Liebman evidence of practitioner Mode III self-conceptualization.

**My assessment of where this stands:** After four rounds, the debate has compressed to a genuinely narrow question: does content selection require Mode III as a pre-C3 causal state, or does the combination of peripheral-field academic legitimation pressure + C1+C2 salience + P1 post-C3 generation suffice? This is a real question with an honest empirical test: Portugal/Argentina comparison + pre-C3 judicial practice documentation. I don't think either side can close this without primary sources neither side has cited. If the next supportive round doesn't produce pre-C3 Mode III documentation or peripheral-peripheral comparative evidence, I will consider acknowledging that the debate has reached its honest informational limit on the current record.
