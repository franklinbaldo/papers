## What I found in the supportive round 12

Supportive round 12 delivered on four distinct fronts. First, posture-type stripping: a third preprocessing layer targeting phrases appearing in greater than ninety percent of decisions of each procedural type across doctrinal clusters, addressing habeas corpus admissibility characterizations, recurso especial admissibility templates, and analogous procedural-posture-specific language that the cross-cluster-convention step cannot reach because the distribution is posture-specific rather than cross-cluster. Second, type (b) shared infrastructure: the argument that type (b) extensions are structurally analogous to C1 calibration annotation across clusters — the same expert legal knowledge required for doctrinal reference-answer construction already handles contested-boundary extensions — converting the type (b) incremental annotation burden from an expansion requiring new infrastructure to a complexity increment within existing infrastructure. Third, Jurisprudência Temática/Teses operationalization: official STF and STJ thematic jurisprudence compilations provide structured database lookup for established application areas, replacing the inferential ementa-as-authoritative-ratio move with explicit institutional documentation. Fourth, secretariat authorship convention: asserted as the basis for why explicitly structured ementas in high-profile constitutional decisions make the implicit-structure class manageable.

The posture-type stripping step is well-specified: the ninety-percent threshold operationalizes the structural intuition about procedural-type universality across doctrinal domains. The type (b) shared-infrastructure framing correctly identifies the genuine structural overlap between type (b) contested-boundary annotation and C1 cross-cluster calibration. Both advances are real.

The Jurisprudência Temática/Teses operationalization is the most significant new claim. It is the right move to make — it addresses the ementa-as-authoritative-ratio vulnerability by replacing inferential reconstruction with official institutional documentation. But it introduces two new vulnerabilities that were not present in the ementa-as-ratio move.

## What I decided to argue

**Accept posture-type stripping.** The ninety-percent threshold operationalizes the structural requirement correctly: phrases universal within a procedural posture across doctrinal clusters are institutional-procedural formulas by the same logic as phrases universal across three or more doctrinal clusters. The step closes the residual the cross-cluster-convention stripping left open. §6 surrender condition (g) is updated accordingly: the condition is now met for four categories, subject to implementation validation of the threshold calibration.

**Accept type (b) shared infrastructure.** The structural argument is correct. Type (b) contested-boundary extension annotation requires the same expert doctrinal knowledge as C1 cross-cluster reference-answer construction — the expert legal reviewer already handling C1 annotation can handle type (b) extensions at the same infrastructure level. The incremental cost is complexity within existing infrastructure, not new infrastructure. This is accepted without reservation.

**Contest the Jurisprudência Temática/Teses operationalization on two grounds.** The granularity gap: official compilations are organized at doctrinal-category level — "proporcionalidade em matéria penal," "due process in administrative proceedings." A tese entry names the governing principle and the established application category but does not identify which specific doctrinal constructions within a given proceeding constitute the fundamentos determinantes. The annotation task is not to identify whether proporcionalidade applies to a case; it is to identify which specific proporcionalidade formulation, under which specific constitutional dimension analysis, was the operative ground. Thematic-level classification does not resolve this. The granularity problem is structural: it was present under ementa-as-ratio (ementa principle-level abstraction), and Jurisprudência Temática entries inherit it — they document the existence of an established application area without identifying the specific doctrinal construction that functions as the fundamento determinante in individual decisions.

The currency limitation: Jurisprudência Temática compilations document settled doctrine. They are updated when STF/STJ formally approves new thematic entries, which occurs after sufficient precedent has accumulated. Contested-boundary developments — the doctrinal extensions that constitute the type (b) class and concentrate in the high-adversarial-record arm — are by definition not yet settled and therefore not yet compiled. The arm where annotation tractability is most disputed is precisely the arm where the compilation is least useful: the high-adversarial-record cases raising genuinely contested applications are the cases that will not yet have a Jurisprudência Temática entry. The database-lookup framing may work for the type (a) settled arm; it fails structurally for the type (b) contested arm.

**Contest the secretariat authorship convention as undocumented.** The claim that STF secretariat conventions favor explicit logical structure for high-profile constitutional ementas has been asserted in prior supportive rounds and re-asserted in round 12 as the basis for implicit-structure class manageability. It has not been supported by reference to STF institutional documentation: drafting guidelines, secretariat practice manuals, or empirical evidence from the ementa corpus. An undocumented convention cannot function as an empirical premise for an implicit-structure manageable-class claim. The round 12 filing named the secretariat authorship convention as the basis for treating the implicit-structure class as tractable; without documentary evidence, the convention's reliability as a predictor of explicit logical structure in contested-constitutional ementas is an assertion rather than an established institutional fact.

---

## What I considered and discarded

**Contesting posture-type stripping on threshold-calibration grounds alone.** The ninety-percent threshold is a design choice, not a theoretically motivated universal. One could contest whether ninety percent is the right threshold for all procedural types — some formulas may be near-universal without reaching ninety percent; others may be domain-specific boilerplate that happens to appear across procedural types. But threshold calibration is an implementation question, not a structural objection. Named it as the implementation validation requirement in the surrender condition update rather than pressing it as a substantive contest. The structural advance is genuine; the threshold calibration is an empirical validation question. Discarded the threshold-calibration contest as the primary argument.

**Contesting type (b) shared infrastructure on magnitude grounds.** One could argue that even if type (b) annotation uses the same infrastructure as C1 cross-cluster annotation, the volume of type (b) cases in the high-adversarial-record arm may substantially increase the expert annotation burden in the contested arm specifically. This is the combined class size argument addressed in the fourth requirement added to surrender condition (f). Named it as the empirical requirement — the joint type (b) rate and implicit-structure flagging rate from pre-calibration pilot data — rather than contesting the structural claim. The structural acceptance is correct; the magnitude question is what the pilot must answer. Discarded the magnitude-based contest of the structural framing.

**Pressing granularity gap as the dominant objection.** The granularity gap is the more fundamental objection because it is structural rather than temporal: even if compilations were perfectly current, they would still not resolve which specific doctrinal construction was the fundamento determinante. The currency limitation is real but could in principle be addressed by maintaining a current supplementary database. The granularity problem cannot be addressed by better currency — it requires a different type of documentation. Gave the granularity gap primary position in the round 12 text, with currency limitation as the second objection.

**Accepting the secretariat authorship convention conditionally.** The convention could be accepted conditionally — as a working assumption pending documentary verification — while noting the empirical gap. Chose instead to contest it as undocumented, since the convention is being used as an empirical premise for a tractability claim in a paper that otherwise requires empirical evidence for its assertions. Accepting an undocumented convention conditionally would be more concessive than the structural position warrants.

## Assessment

The round 12 adversarial position is structurally honest. The posture-type stripping and type (b) shared-infrastructure acceptances reflect genuine advances the supportive earned. Accepting both removes the C3 procedural-type residual from the list of outstanding concerns and clarifies that the remaining annotation tractability dispute is the granularity-currency-convention cluster, not the preprocessing architecture.

The most important structural observation is that the Jurisprudência Temática/Teses operationalization inherits the granularity problem it was introduced to solve. The ementa-as-ratio move failed because ementas abstract from the doctrinal specificity of individual vote threads; the Jurisprudência Temática move is subject to the same critique because thematic entries abstract from the doctrinal specificity of individual decisions. The supportive has moved from one abstraction-level document to another abstraction-level database without closing the gap between thematic categorization and fundamento-determinante identification.

The currency limitation argument targets the specific arm where the operationalization matters most. The type (a) settled cases where compilations are current are the cases where annotation is uncontested regardless. The type (b) contested-boundary cases where annotation is genuinely hard are exactly the cases where compilations will be absent or incomplete. The operationalization is most credible where it is least needed and least credible where it is most needed.

The secretariat authorship convention challenge forces the supportive to produce documentation or concede that the implicit-structure class manageable-class claim rests on an unverified assumption. If the supportive produces documentation (secretariat drafting guidelines, institutional practice evidence), the challenge is answered. If no documentation is produced, the convention remains an assertion and the manageable-class claim is not established.

The adversarial's primary failure condition for round 13: the supportive produces empirical evidence from the Jurisprudência Temática corpus showing that type (b) entries exist — that compilations include entries for contested-boundary extensions — resolving both the currency and granularity objections for at least a significant portion of the type (b) class. If compilations are more current and more granular than the adversarial claims, the operationalization may work for a larger portion of the type (b) arm than the currency and granularity arguments allow.
