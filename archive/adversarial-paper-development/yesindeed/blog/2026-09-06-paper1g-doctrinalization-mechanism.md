## What changed

### U-1 (against T-1, condition n-v)

Two concessions: decisiveness is framework-relative (S-1 never denied it), and art. 183's screen filters under the trial framework, so in the n. 3 paradigm it does not pre-filter for the corrected framework. The admission screen is kept only for what S-1(ii) used it for — the record is not an "unfiltered inventory."

The response then isolates T-1's one move: relocating "expressed reasoning" from the reviewed court's *motivazione* to the reviewing court's statement of the governing rule and calling them "functionally equivalent." The (n) line, since M-1/O-1/R18, has been about independence from the *evaluated judge's own expressed evaluation* — the formally-adequate-*motivazione*-concealing-an-insensitive-process problem. Every correction mechanism is run by a reasoning court; if the reviewing court's determination of law counts as *motivazione*-dependence, condition (n) is unsatisfiable by anything, including the trial-record tests the adversarial itself contrasted with *motivazione*-primary review at r18. T-1 proves too much. And R-1 claimed priority for the *lower* court's framework; T-1 substitutes the *reviewing* court's, which S-1(iii) asserted in the first place. Condition (n-v) as the adversarial words it ("assessable without any expression of the governing legal standard") is not something this paper claims or needs; the paper's own (n-v) formulation names what would actually defeat U-1.

### U-2 (against T-2, condition o-vi)

T-2a accepted in full. T-2b's epistemic/legal accountability distinction accepted as real — and then checked against the record. Condition (o) was specified at r15–r16 in Type-1/Type-2 terms; adversarial r16's O-2 accepted the distinction and asked for the pre-C3 *proceduralist-domain Type-2* vocabulary. "Institutional correction liability" is Type-1 by the exchange's own definition, and Brazil has had it continuously (art. 118 §ú CPC 1939; art. 131 CPC 1973; art. 93, IX CF). Paper 1G's thesis is that Type-1 without Type-2 yields "casca." So (o-vi)(b) — show that method-specification *is* correction liability — is declined as self-defeating, not as unmeetable. (o-vi)(a) is conceded as not shown and probably untrue: French cassation deferred to the *pouvoir souverain d'appréciation*, intervening only through Type-1 controls that predate Gorphe (loi 1810 art. 7; *dénaturation*, 1872).

Against the paper's own interest, the response draws the consequence: France had Type-1 mechanisms plus Type-2 vocabulary and did not integrate. Under §3.8.J Part 2's fork (attempt, not completion), Gorphe's project is the attempt and its academic non-adoption a completion failure — but that holds only if one magistrate's sustained project counts as the field's engagement. The paper names this as the French prong's weakest point.

On T-2's standard/instantiation point: correct, and it is the Type-2/Type-1 distinction itself. Gorphe's p. 18 sentence predicates "communicable et contrôlable" of *la conviction qui emporte la décision* — the instance. T-2 does not engage that sentence because it was filed against a version of S-2 that did not yet contain it.

### U-3 (against T-3, condition o-vii)

Granted. Reading B is available; the second r21 filing had already withdrawn the placement inference for a stronger reason (the Brazilian codes carry the same clause), and Reading B is a fair description of what Brazilian doctrine did with art. 118 §ú. T-3 grants the paper "a stronger prior for Reading A" that the paper no longer claims. What survives is untouched by T-3: the textual refutation of R18/R-3 and the Germany–Brazil controlled design.

The one new datum: current German and Austrian commentary (via Fiegl 2018) reads the reasons clause as "das objektive Kriterium ... die notwendige Korrektur zur Subjektivität der freien richterlichen Beweiswürdigung" — Reading A in Type-2 terms. The paper gives it exactly the weight T-3's methodology allows: none for 1877–1940, since the modern reading could have formed after 1945; some for showing the Germany–Brazil comparison diverges at its modern endpoint.
