## What changed

### S-2: Gorphe's own formulation, and a further concession on the French prong

Leclerc's epigraph quotes Gorphe 1947, p. 18: in a rational conception of justice, the conviction that carries the decision "passe ainsi de l'état de simple croyance subjective à celui d'une véritable connaissance objective, communicable et contrôlable." This is the sentence r21's first filing said it recalled as the thrust of Gorphe's work but would not assert without verification. It is now verified through Leclerc and placed under S-2 with its source. It does the work (o-iv) asked for as the adversarial worded that condition: the rational character of the conviction is made to depend on its being communicable and controllable, not merely accurate. Two further passages from Leclerc frame the project against R-2's "epistemic virtue" reading rather than within it: the 1924 thesis opens on the judge left to his "flair" and "divination instinct" (1924, p. 11), and the 1947 book names the danger of "emotional intuition" after the abolition of legal proof (1947, p. 17). The paper now says plainly that R-2's virtue picture is the position Gorphe wrote against.

What this run does *not* claim is unchanged from the first filing: no form-a institutional-review obligation is shown from Gorphe, because his statements on *motivation* and appellate control have still not been read, and the p. 18 passage is known only through Leclerc.

The French prong also lost ground, and the paper says so. Leclerc's conclusion is that Gorphe "received very limited attention within French universities," that after his death "the path he had opened up was not followed by others," and that continued doctrinal interest in his work was Spanish-language, where *sana crítica* rules gave dogmatic scholarship a hook. So there was no French field-level tradition that integrated this vocabulary. The French prong now supports vocabulary *existence* before C3 (the 1924 thesis; the 1931–1938 articles on judicial conviction that Leclerc lists; the 1947 book's pre-war composition, stated in the book itself) and *accessibility* to Argentine scholars from 1950 — and nothing more. The Gorphe-noticed affinity with *sana crítica* is recorded as a lead for the Portugal/Argentina comparison, not a finding.

### S-3: the Brazilian codes have the same sentence, so code placement is withdrawn as a prior

r21's S-3 argued that § 259 CPO 1877's second sentence — a reasons duty specific to the conviction, inside the free-evaluation norm — is, by the code-structure standard both sides have used for Italy, evidence that the German tradition conceived the reasons duty as part of the free-evaluation concept, so that "the German prior differs from the Italian one, in the direction of integration." Checked against the Brazilian codes, that inference fails on the supportive's own home case. Art. 118 §ú CPC 1939 ("O juiz indicará na sentença ou despacho os fatos e circunstâncias que motivaram o seu convencimento") and art. 131 CPC 1973 ("mas deverá indicar, na sentença, os motivos que lhe formaram o convencimento") place the same clause in the same position, with a general *fundamentação* duty elsewhere (art. 458, II CPC 1973) — the German three-part structure, not the Italian two-part one. Paper 1G §3.2 documents that Brazilian doctrine read the clause as "casca — não núcleo." If placement were evidence of conceptual integration, it would be evidence of that for Brazil, which the thesis denies. The paper cannot use for Germany an inference it must deny for Brazil.

S-3 is therefore narrowed. What survives: (a) R18's "structurally identical" premise is false on the text; (b) R-3's parity argument has no purchase, because it compared Art. 132 with § 313 and omitted the provision without an Italian counterpart. What is withdrawn: the German prior in the direction of integration, and the unsourced §3.8.O scholarship claim with it. What is gained: the Germany–Brazil pair holds code text constant, so condition (o-v) now discriminates between the accounts in both directions — German Possibility B with Brazilian form-only reading isolates field formation as the variable (the thesis's territory); German Possibility A makes non-integration the default even where the code integrates and satisfies (o-ii)(a) for the German prong. The same discipline is applied to the supportive's own use of the Italian two-institution structure: consistent with separation, not proof of it.

The clause is Type-1 in all three codes ("sind die Gründe anzugeben"; "indicará"; "deverá indicar"). This is noted to pre-empt a reception-selectivity reading (adversarial §3.8.B–C): the vocabulary-absence claim was always about Type-2 constitution in the scholarship, and the code text answers that question for none of the three traditions.
