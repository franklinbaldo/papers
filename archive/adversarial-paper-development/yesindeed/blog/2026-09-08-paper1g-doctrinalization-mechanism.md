## What changed

### W-1 (against V-1, condition o-viii)

**Two concessions.** V-1 is right that the field-level criterion applies symmetrically and that under it Gorphe's project is not French field-level Type-2 vocabulary — the second r21 filing had already reduced the French prong to "existence and accessibility," and U-2 had named the field-level question as the soft spot. The paper now draws the consequence V-1 did not draw: accessibility was symmetric as well. A published text is not absent from any field that can read it; Gorphe circulated in Spanish from 1933, Brazilian federal libraries hold Spanish editions, and Brazilian doctrine cites him. Condition (o)(iii) — a resource "absent from Brazil's equivalent pre-C3 formation" — cannot be satisfied by existence or access. **The French prong as a resource-existence argument is withdrawn**, and condition (o) no longer rests on it.

**The relocation.** What (o)(iii) can still mean is uptake — the field-level criterion applied to the receiving fields, which keeps the consistency V-1 demands. On uptake the record is asymmetric in a specific way: the two Argentine translations of the 1947 method book (the text with the p. 18 definition) were made by proceduralists, Alcalá-Zamora y Castillo (1950) and Sentís Melendo (1955); what is verified for Brazil is uptake of the 1924 witness-critique in the psychology-of-testimony register. That gives the comparative test a concrete tracer (reception of the 1947 book in each receiving field's proceduralist literature, 1950–1988). The confound is named against the paper's interest: Leclerc explains the Spanish-language uptake by the *sana crítica* rule, which made evaluation of evidence a dogmatic topic. Argentina's code names a standard; Brazil's and Portugal's name a freedom. So the Argentina–Brazil pair is confounded by the code formula, and the Portugal–Brazil pair (art. 655 CPC 1961) is the cleaner one. The comparative test is now Portugal-first.

**Against "France confirms the symmetric fork."** The adversarial's §3.8.H barrier is defined for a peripheral receiving field with a received concept, a Form (i) framing to protect, and P1-consolidated non-integration. France has none of these; it cannot confirm a mechanism whose preconditions it does not meet. And Leclerc's mechanism is a different one: practical orientation, dated psychology, the judge/professor institutional divide, and — the one that matters — a dogmatic legal culture with no textual object, because "French law does not impose on judges what form evidential reasoning should take ... a domain where French law remains silent." That explanation is unavailable for Brazil, whose codes carry a conviction-specific reasons clause inside the free-evaluation norm — the textual object the French field lacked — which Brazilian doctrine, on Paper 1G's account, read as form. Condition (o-viii)(b) is met on that evidence. The design becomes a four-way table on one variable (the code hook): France (none; no uptake), Germany (freedom + reasons clause; Reading A today; 1877–1940 open), Brazil (same hook; Type-1 reading), Argentina (standard named; proceduralist uptake).

**What V-1 establishes and the paper accepts.** Field-level Type-2 absence is multiply realizable; §3.7 wrote as if the patrimonial route were the only one. The claim is narrowed to what Paper 1G needs: in Brazil, with the textual object present and read as form, the absence took the patrimonial route rather than the dogmatic-object route.

**Engagement threshold.** V-1's demand for "citations in mainstream treatises, adoption in legal education" is a demand for completion, which the fork never predicted. L-2's actor-heterogeneity mechanism says engagement comes from actors outside the mainstream; Gorphe is that. But France is a source field, so the paper takes only the threshold from it and states it symmetrically — and names the Brazilian check that would meet condition (f): Amaral Santos's *Prova judiciária no cível e comercial* (1949–1955), where Gorphe is a standard citation on testimony, read for whether it engaged the 1947 definition of rational conviction.

### W-2 (against V-2, condition n-v)

V-2 concedes T-1's core (no functional equivalence; the prior expression is the reviewing court's) and retains only that framework-expression precedes decisiveness-assessment. Granted in full; S-1(iii) asserted it first. But once the prior expression is the reviewing court's own determination of law, the priority claim is a feature of every review mechanism and is compatible with complete independence from the reviewed act's self-description, which is what condition (n) asks about. The claim and the condition no longer touch. (n-v) in the adversarial's wording is proposed for retirement; (n-iv) and the paper's own (n-v) remain the live tests.

## Discarded alternatives

- **Defending the French prong as a resource argument** ("condition (o) asks for a text, and a text existed"). It fails on the paper's own (o)(iii) wording once access is shown symmetric, and defending it would have required pretending Brazilian jurists could not read French. A concession beats that.
- **Claiming that Amaral Santos cites Gorphe.** Every indication (Brazilian secondary literature; Gorphe's tripartite analysis of testimony appearing in Brazilian treatments that cite Amaral Santos alongside him) suggests he does, but the treatise itself was not reached and no snippet was obtained. Named as the check, not asserted.
- **Using France as positive evidence for the fork** (one heterogeneous actor engaged, as L-2 predicts). France is a source field; the fork is about receiving fields. Only the threshold is drawn from it.
- **Reopening the classification of Portugal as a non-patrimonial comparator**, given Paper 1G's own attribution of the disposition to *Iberian* patrimonialism. Outside this round's one move; flagged in the paper in one sentence and below.
- **Dating Argentina's art. 386 to 1967.** Ley 17.454 is the 1967 code, but the mirror consulted gives the 1981 consolidation date and the original wording was not separately verified; the paper says "text as consolidated in 1981."

## Concessions made

1. The field-level criterion applies symmetrically; Gorphe's project is not French field-level Type-2 vocabulary.
2. Accessibility to Gorphe's texts was symmetric between Brazil and the comparators; existence and access cannot satisfy condition (o)(iii). The French prong as a resource-existence argument is withdrawn; condition (o) now rests on the German prong (o-v) and on the comparative uptake test.
3. Argentine uptake has a code-formula explanation (*sana crítica*) available to the adversarial; the Argentina–Brazil pair is confounded and the comparison is Portugal-first.
4. Field-level Type-2 absence is multiply realizable (patrimonial formation; dogmatic culture without a textual object); §3.7's claim is narrowed to the patrimonial route where a textual object was present and read as form.
5. On (n-v): framework-expression precedes decisiveness-assessment, in full.

## Remaining uncertainty

- **Brazilian proceduralist uptake of the 1947 book, 1950–1988.** Unverified either way; Amaral Santos is the first check. If uptake was symmetric with Argentina, the tracer yields nothing.
- **Portuguese uptake and the 1939 Portuguese code text.** Neither reached. The Portugal–Brazil pair is proposed as the cleaner comparison on the strength of the 1961/1967 text.
- **Portugal as a non-patrimonial comparator.** The exchange has classified it so since r1; Paper 1G's thesis traces the Brazilian disposition to Iberian patrimonialism, and the tension is not examined here.
- **Leclerc's dogmatic-object explanation** is his; the paper's (o-viii) formulation names the possibility that French proceduralist scholarship did have a textual object and declined it.
- **Hahn's *Materialien*** remain the decisive unread source for the German prong.
