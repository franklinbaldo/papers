## What I decided to argue

**The three-mode framework for self-conceptualization under C2.** C2 (enforcement deficit) reduces external costs on informal practice. But the self-conceptualization of informal practice is independent of external cost structure. Three modes are available under C2:

- *Mode I (covert)*: practitioners know the practice is non-compliant and do not publicly claim it. Enforcement deficit permits it; internal assessment still flags it as formal-standard-exceeding.
- *Mode II (tolerated)*: practitioners treat the practice as accepted institutional reality without positively endorsing it as correct.
- *Mode III (authoritative ownership)*: practitioners conceive of the practice as legitimate exercise of their institutional authority — not deviation from a formal standard, but the role's natural expression.

C2 determines that the external cost of modes I and II is reduced. It does not determine whether practitioners adopt modes I, II, or III. What determines mode III is the practitioners' constitutive framework of their institutional role.

In a rational-legal framework: the judge's role is applying formal norms. Informal personal evaluative exercise is, by the role definition, excess relative to the standard. The internal assessment of this excess persists under C2. Enforcement deficit does not eliminate the awareness that "this is not what the formal obligation specifies."

In the patrimonial framework: personal evaluative judgment is the natural form of judicial authority. There is no internal reference to a formal-standard-exceeding — the informal practice IS the role as constitutively understood. C2 creates the environment where mode III is exercised openly, but mode III is not produced by C2; it is produced by the constitutive framework that defines personal judgment as authority.

**Why mode III is required for retrospective-validation C3 absorption.** Absorbing C3 as retrospective validation ("we have been practicing what the Europeans call *libre conviction*") requires that the practice be publicly claimed as correct. In mode I, public claiming exposes the covert. In mode II, the vocabulary would be absorbed as prospective aspiration, not retrospective confirmation. Mode III is the only available state for retrospective-validation absorption. Streck's "solipsismo judicial" diagnosis is documentary evidence of mode III: what he identifies is practitioners who genuinely believe deciding "according to conscience" is how judicial authority legitimately operates — mode III, not mode I or II.

**The three-level structure.** Patrimonialism produces mode III (the constitutive role-framework that defines personal judgment as authority). C2 produces the survival of mode III under field conditions (external enforcement would force mode III back toward modes I or II). Peripheral-field dynamics produce the orientation of C3 absorption when mode III practice is available (validation rather than critique). The three mechanisms are not redundant; they operate at different levels of the same process.

**Update to §6 condition (e).** Added the mode III specificity: for the C2-alone variant of condition (e) to succeed, the adversarial must show that sustained enforcement deficit produces the constitutive role-definition shift (from formal-standard-applying to personal-authority-as-role), not merely that it permits the practice to persist unchallenged.

## What I considered and discarded

**Attempting the Portugal/Argentina comparison.** Both sides and the synthesis have identified this as the primary empirical frontier. I considered researching documentary evidence about the Argentine and Portuguese reception of the Liebman tradition. The synthesis says "if the supportive can make it, it should." However, the specific documentary evidence required — how the processualismo vocabulary was received in Argentine and Portuguese civil procedure scholarship — requires primary-source access to those fields' doctrinal literature that this session cannot supply without risking fabrication. The three-mode framework is a genuine structural contribution that does not require empirical comparison; it is available now and provides a response to the predicted r4 argument that the empirical comparison cannot substitute for. Fabricated comparative evidence would violate the invariant rule; absent fabrication, the comparison remains open for a future session with primary-source access.

**Arguing that the adversarial's own Reply 2 confirms the mode framework.** The adversarial paper's §4, Reply 2, already contains a version of the individual/collective distinction: "the peripheral-field dynamic operates at the collective level regardless of whether individual practitioners are individually patrimonially disposed." I considered whether to engage this directly. Decided against explicitly naming it, since the mode framework operates at the level of the practitioner's constitutive role-understanding — which is distinct from both individual habitual disposition (the adversarial frames its Reply 2 response against) and from collective structural incentive. The mode framework is a structural point about role-constitution, not about individual disposition or collective incentive. It does not engage the adversarial Reply 2 directly; it addresses the C2-alone sufficiency variant that the synthesis predicts for r4.

**Pressing P2 as specifically patrimonial.** The synthesis session 75 did not identify this as the most urgent obligation. The mode framework is more targeted to the predicted r4 argument. Deferred.

## Assessment

The three-mode framework is a genuine structural development, not restatement of what the paper already said. The existing paper used "self-conceptualized as a natural expression of judicial authority rather than as deviation" correctly as the content-selection variable, but did not specify the mechanism by which the patrimonial framework produces that self-conceptualization mode (III) while C2 alone does not. The three-mode analysis fills this gap precisely at the level the predicted adversarial r4 argument targets.

The framework is not dependent on empirical evidence the session cannot supply. It does not require the Portugal/Argentina comparison (which remains open). It operates at the level of role-constitution, which can be analyzed from the materials already in play — the patrimonial framework's constitutive characterization in Faoro and Holanda, and the documentary evidence of mode III in the Brazilian case (Streck's "solipsismo judicial" and the Dinamarco-Grinover-Cintra tradition's positive framing of *livre convencimento*).

The main vulnerability introduced is that the adversarial could argue that the rational-legal/patrimonial constitutive framework distinction is itself a cultural-historical claim that collapses into the patrimonialism thesis rather than providing independent support for it — making the mode framework circular rather than structural. This is a legitimate objection the adversarial r4 might press. The response would be that the constitutive role framework distinction is the operationalization of the patrimonial habitus at the level relevant for content selection; circularity is not established by the fact that an operationalization and the theoretical account it operationalizes refer to the same underlying variable.
