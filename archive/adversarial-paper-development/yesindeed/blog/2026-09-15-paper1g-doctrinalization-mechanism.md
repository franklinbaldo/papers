## What changed

### Y-1 (against X-1) — and what the tracer returned

**X-1(a).** Accepted as to what it says about W-1's confound: it is code-level as Leclerc states it, W-1 named it against interest and set Argentina–Brazil aside, and — this paper's addition — the Portugal test is one-directional (non-uptake is predicted by both accounts; only uptake would refute the code-formula mechanism). Rejected as to the inference. The formula is not a code feature exogenous to formation: it is a Spanish doctrinal vocabulary (1846 Reglamento; LEC 1855 art. 317; LEC 1881 art. 659), "netamente hispánico" in Sentís Melendo's own words, given its Latin American canonical form by Couture; and the Argentine uptake instances W-1 relied on were made by Spanish-formed exiles — Luis Alcalá-Zamora y Castillo (1950) and Sentís Melendo (1955) — twelve and seventeen years before the code X-1 names (CPCCN art. 386, Ley 17.454, 1967). The confound relocates from the code to the actors' formation. A narrowing follows against the paper's interest: the formation that carried it is Spanish, so Paper 1G's "patrimonialismo ibérico" must be read as Luso-Brazilian or Argentina becomes an in-category counterexample.

**X-1(b).** Accepted. The window is narrowed to 1947–1965 at no cost: both Argentine translations and both Brazilian candidates (Amaral Santos 1949–1955; Pontes de Miranda's 1939-code commentary) fall inside it.

**The tracer.** Run on the Brazilian side through Oliveira's page-cited quotations, it returned the result that decides the round. Amaral Santos names the 1939 code's system the *sistema da persuasão racional* (p. 332; denomination attributed to Máximo Castro, Furno and Costa Carvalho), conditions the conviction to four things (pp. 333–338) — the facts, the evidence taken, legal rules and maxims of experience, and *motivation* — and derives the fourth from the conditioning: "Exatamente porque a convicção do juiz está condicionada, é-lhe imposta a obrigação de motivá-la ... Na motivação está a única garantia possível ... É pela motivação que se apura qual o trabalho intelectual desenvolvido pelo juiz na apreciação da prova" (pp. 337–338). At p. 343 the requisites are what distinguish the system from *livre convicção*; at p. 352 it is "da tradição brasileira." Under the exchange's Type-2 criterion (N-2, accepted at O-2) that is the integrated conception, in the field's principal C3 evidence treatise, claimed as the field's tradition.

**Consequences drawn.** Condition (o) fails from the Brazilian side (requirement (c), absence from Brazil's formation, cannot be met) and the (o) line closes by concession; the differential-uptake relocation, the German prong's role in (o) and the Hahn *Materialien* target are released. Condition (f) is met on the record as it stands; §3.7's vocabulary-absence mechanism is withdrawn. The same fact refutes X-1(a)'s mechanism (a freedom-specified code adopted the standard-vocabulary wholesale), which does the paper no good.

**What survives.** Paper 1G's own §3.2 claim is practice-level ("a motivação funcionando como condição de forma, não de substância"; "casca — não núcleo") and describes a *gradual reinterpretation*; it never claimed the doctrine lacked the words. Amaral Santos's fourth condition is Paper 1G's own description of the "versão europeia continental." The supportive claim narrows to what §5 stated from the start — C1–C3 and P1–P2 — and to a new within-field test: the de-integration trajectory of the fourth condition in Brazilian doctrine 1952–1988, on which the two accounts predict different intermediate texts (condition dropped under the disposition; kept and neutralized under the "conquista" framing). On form selection at C3 the adversarial's legitimation account is at least as well supported as patrimonialism, and the paper says so.

## Discarded alternatives

- **Defending X-1(a) on the "confound is formation-level" point alone and leaving the Brazilian tracer for a later round.** The narrowed window X-1(b) asked for made the Brazilian check the obvious next step, and the paper had named it. Running it and reporting an adverse result was the only honest course.
- **Reading Amaral Santos's motivation as Type-1** ("é-lhe imposta a obrigação" is obligation language). The obligation is listed as a *condition of the conviction*, inside the system's definition, derived from the conditioning, and described as the means of reviewing the intellectual work. On the exchange's own definition that is Type-2. Arguing otherwise would have been the "dishonest defense" the loop forbids.
- **Waiting for the primary text before withdrawing.** The quotations are page-cited, extensive and internally consistent, and Google Books confirms the term's presence in the treatise. The withdrawal is made now with the primary-text check named as the one condition on which it is reconsidered.
- **Treating the Spanish formation of the Argentine carriers as evidence for the fork** (formation supplies the vocabulary). It is evidence for the *level* at which Paper 1G's mechanism operates, but the content of that formation is Iberian, which cuts against the paper's label; recorded as a narrowing, not claimed as support.
- **Verifying the 1880 Capital code's testimonial rule.** Not reachable; stated as unverified, with the observation that if the rule was there it was the Spanish rule received.
