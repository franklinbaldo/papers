## What I decided to argue

**Accept the per-ementa/per-pair distinction.** The adversarial is correct that the three criteria are per-ementa operations and that (A)/(B) determination is per-pair. This logical distinction is sound. The criteria classify ementa vocabulary; (A)/(B) determination involves a specific thread T compared against the ementa. These are different operations at different granularity.

**Contest the reliability proposition through secretariat authorship decoupling.** The reliability proposition — that principle-level-classified ementas reliably correspond to entanglement-class thread populations — requires that ementa vocabulary level reliably tracks vote-thread specificity level. STF secretariat authorship practice breaks this correspondence through three institutional mechanisms:

*Mechanism (a) — upward abstraction.* Doctrinal-specificity vote threads → abstract principle vocabulary in the ementa. The secretariat abstracts upward from threads addressing specific CTN provisions, statutory constructions, or constitutional dimensions to governing principle vocabulary ("razoabilidade e proporcionalidade"). Criterion 1 correctly classifies this ementa as principle-level, and the thread population is genuinely entanglement-class. Mechanism (a) is the paradigm where the criteria work: criteria correctly classify ementa vocabulary AND the thread population is entanglement-class.

*Mechanism (b) — institutional specificity override.* Abstract-principle vote threads → specific-provision ementa citations for institutional precision. A justice argues about what proporcionalidade requires without analyzing specific provisions; the secretariat inserts "art. 5°, LV, CF" for institutional anchoring. Criterion 2 classifies this ementa as concrete-specificity. The triply-modified scheme applies (B). But a thread reasoning at abstract-principle level against a specific-provision ementa may not have a tractable textual-designative (A) determination: whether the abstract-principle formulation is "textually stated in" the specific-provision ementa may require assessing whether the cited provisions implement the abstract argument — conceptual-coverage territory. The criteria classify the ementa as concrete-specificity (correctly by vocabulary) but the thread-ementa relationship generates a determination that (B) forecloses without establishing. This is a reliability failure in the direction of under-classifying entanglement: concrete-specificity classification applies (B) for a thread population where the actual determination requires entanglement analysis.

*Mechanism (c) — cross-case precedential abstraction.* The secretariat abstracts ementas above the specificity of any individual vote thread for broad cross-case applicability. Criterion 1 classifies this ementa as principle-level. But the thread population is heterogeneous: some threads reason at doctrinal specificity (mechanism (a) configuration within this ementa, generating genuine entanglement); others reason near the ementa's abstraction level (tractable determinations); others at intermediate specificity (indeterminate). The per-ementa criterion 1 classification applies (pendente) uniformly to all absent threads regardless of which thread-population configuration applies. The criteria cannot distinguish, within the principle-level-classified ementa class, which thread-population configurations apply to which ementas.

**The circularity survives in refined form.** The criteria are logically distinct from the (A)/(B) determination. But their empirical reliability for per-thread entanglement prediction requires knowing the thread-population configuration — which requires examining individual vote threads for each classified ementa. This is a per-(T,E)-pair examination: the verification procedure the adversarial proposes as establishing reliability requires the thread-level comparison the criteria were designed to avoid. The circularity holds at the level of the reliability verification, not at the level of the criteria themselves: the criteria can classify ementa vocabulary without thread comparand; they cannot establish their own reliability for per-thread entanglement prediction without thread examination.

## What I considered and discarded

**Contesting the per-ementa/per-pair distinction itself.** The adversarial correctly identifies the granularity difference. The criteria are per-ementa; (A)/(B) is per-pair. This is a sound logical observation. Contesting it would be incorrect. Discarded.

**Arguing mechanism (a) dominates empirically without evidence.** I could have argued that upward abstraction from doctrinal-specificity threads is the typical secretariat function, making mechanism (a) dominant and the criteria reliable in practice. This might be true but requires empirical basis I do not have. Named mechanism (a) as the paradigm where the criteria work, and named empirical verification of mechanism dominance as the failure condition (§6). The honest position is that all three mechanisms are structurally available under secretariat practice; which dominates in STF constitutional adjudication is an empirical question.

**Pressing mechanism (b) as the primary argument.** Mechanism (b) is the clearest reliability failure: it identifies a direction where the criteria mis-classify the entanglement prediction (classifying as concrete-specificity when the thread population may require entanglement analysis). But mechanism (b) requires accepting that abstract-principle threads against specific-provision ementas generate entanglement-class determinations — which the adversarial may contest by arguing the textual-designative determination is tractable even for abstract-against-specific configurations. Named mechanism (b) alongside mechanisms (a) and (c) rather than pressing it exclusively.

**Accepting the reliability proposition conditionally.** I could have accepted that the criteria are reliable when mechanism (a) dominates and contested only that mechanism (a) always dominates. This would have been a more concessive move that focuses the dispute on mechanism dominance as an empirical question. I chose instead to show the full structural case for why secretariat authorship decouples vocabulary from thread specificity, leaving the empirical mechanism-dominance question as a failure condition rather than the primary dispute. This is stronger: it shows the structural reason reliability fails, not merely that reliability is empirically uncertain.

**Developing the reliability verification requirement as a formal circularity argument.** The strongest version of the argument is that the reliability verification procedure cannot be conducted from ementa text alone — it requires per-pair examination. I developed this as the circularity's refined form but kept the emphasis on the three mechanisms as the substantive reason the criteria fail, with the verification structure as the consequence that confirms the circularity. This ordering is correct: the mechanisms explain why ementa vocabulary decouples from thread specificity; the verification structure confirms that establishing reliability requires precisely the thread examination the criteria were designed to avoid.

## Assessment

The round 9 response is structurally honest. Accepting cost types I and II and the per-ementa/per-pair distinction confirms the concessions the adversarial earned and avoids retreating from them. The secretariat authorship argument is the correct substantive response: it shows why ementa vocabulary level cannot reliably predict thread-population specificity level, and names three distinct mechanisms through which the decoupling occurs.

The most important structural observation in the response is that the adversarial's reliability verification procedure — the empirical check the adversarial itself proposes as the resolution — cannot be conducted from ementa text alone. The verification requires examining individual vote threads for each principle-level-classified ementa. This is a per-(T,E)-pair examination, which is precisely what the criteria were designed to avoid. The circularity does not survive unchanged (the criteria are logically distinct from (A)/(B) determination); it survives in refined form (establishing the criteria's reliability for per-thread entanglement prediction requires the per-pair examination the criteria were designed to bypass).

The adversarial's round 9 blog entry named path (c) — operational equivalence concession — as the correct adversarial next step if the secretariat authorship argument lands. If the secretariat authorship decoupling argument is accepted, the triply-modified scheme does not achieve epistemic accuracy through a genuinely independent mechanism; it converges on the same result through the same analysis under different vocabulary. The concession path would acknowledge this. Whether the adversarial pursues path (c) or contests the secretariat authorship argument empirically (by showing mechanism (a) dominance) is the open question after round 9.

The adversarial's most available round 10 path: press that mechanism (a) (upward abstraction) is empirically dominant for STF constitutional adjudication — that secretariat authorship practice systematically abstracts from doctrinal-specificity threads in exactly the paradigm configuration the criteria correctly predict. If this can be shown from evidence (secretariat authorship guidelines, ementa drafting practice), the reliability proposition holds for the dominant configuration and the mechanism (b)/(c) reliability failures are residual rather than systematic. Named this as the primary failure condition.
