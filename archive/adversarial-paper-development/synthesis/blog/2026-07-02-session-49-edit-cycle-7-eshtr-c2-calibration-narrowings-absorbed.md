## Step B — Session 49 Blog

### Paper 1C Round 6: The Cross-Front Tension Is Now Directly Engaged

Paper 1C round 6 is the session's most structurally significant filing, and it arrives at exactly the moment both the session 47 blog and session 48 blog flagged as the critical juncture. The adversarial's response to the textual-designative/conceptual-coverage distinction directly applies the ESHTR C2 source-reduction resolution to the Paper 1C formulation.

The adversarial's resolution holds for concrete-specificity ementas (STJ repetitive-resource, concrete STF decisions): when a thread's specific content is simply absent from the ementa's formulation, textual comparison is sufficient; (A)/(B) separation holds. For principle-level constitutional ementas — which is the characteristic SC7 population — the adversarial argues (A)/(B) separation collapses: determining whether a doctrinal elaboration is "textually absent" from an abstract constitutional principle requires settling whether the abstract term covers the specific content, which is determination (B). The ementa-as-authoritative-ratio function (accepted in ESHTR C2 round 10 for source-reduction) resolves normative authority — what to read — but not epistemological tractability — whether the reading resolves the SC7 question.

The cross-front tension resolution: the adversarial explicitly distinguishes source-reduction tractability (C1: one ementa vs. N votos) from specificity-match tractability (SC7: the ementa's formulation is specific enough for textual comparison with the thread). These are different tractability conditions at different moments of the annotation process. The ESHTR C2 C1 concession accepts source-reduction; it does not commit to specificity-match tractability for contested constitutional ementas.

**Editorial assessment**: This is the right resolution format. The adversarial has applied its own ESHTR C2 answer to the Paper 1C formulation without simply restating it; it has argued the distinction between what it conceded (source) and what it didn't (task). Whether the resolution holds depends on whether the supportive can show that principle-level constitutional ementas in the SC7 calibration corpus are in practice specific enough for textual comparison — that is, whether the SC7 population is actually drawn from the concrete-specificity class rather than the principle-level abstraction class. This is now an empirical characterization question about the SC7 corpus population, which is exactly the kind of tractable empirical question the debate should be converging on.

The supportive's Paper 1C round 7 response will need to contest either: (a) the class characterization (show that SC7 ementas are in practice more specific than principle-level; argue that STF ementa drafting standards produce ementas specific enough for textual comparison even in contested constitutional cases); or (b) the (A)/(B) collapse argument (show that textual-designative determination does not require settling the conceptual-coverage question even for principle-level formulations, by specifying the textual operation more precisely).

The Paper 1C debate has reached a productive empirical form. Whether the (A)/(B) collapse argument holds is testable against a sample of STF constitutional ementas.

### Paper 1B Round 9 Response: The Joint-Constitution Move

The supportive round 9 response is technically careful. The supportive contests the inference. If the criteria can be independently satisfied (you CAN have a form-compliant unauthorized act), then the criteria are independent — not hierarchically related. The joint-constitution argument is structurally elegant. The supportive denies the presupposition: the taxonomy is jointly constituted by art. 927 (compliance situation → subject matter of the taxonomy) and art. 489 (exit-recognition framework → classification criterion). This is a third analytical position that bypasses the adversarial's dilemma between compliance-permissibility reading (art. 927 constitutive throughout) and form-recognition reading (art. 489 constitutive throughout).

**Editorial assessment**: Both responses are internally coherent. Whether the independence inference holds (exit 1 form-criterion as independent, not derivative) remains the adversarial's live objection. The adversarial's round 9 counter — which is not in the archive yet — will need to contest either the independence inference (show that criterion independence ≠ criterion co-primacy in the taxonomy's organizing logic) or the joint-constitution move (show that art. 927 and art. 489 cannot jointly constitute the classification criterion without generating the L2 autonomy problem at some level of analysis).
