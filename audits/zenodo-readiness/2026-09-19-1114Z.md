## Material findings

### 1. The dogmatic summary is behind the archival child-paper state

The synthesis still describes Paper 1B's fifth exit as `abstenção da invocação` and describes lower-court `superação racional` too generically. The now-remediated dogmatic umbrella explicitly rejects citation silence as a way to escape art. 927 and replaces the fifth situation with an independent issue that genuinely makes the precedent non-controlling. It also makes disagreement/revision competence-sensitive: reasoned criticism, distinguishing, anticipatory overruling or another legally available nonapplication mechanism must be separated from formal revision/cancellation by the competent/source court.

Paper 1G and the 1E/1F institutional mechanism summaries likewise need to inherit their current audit boundaries: generic cost/reputation mechanisms are not enough to establish the Brazil-specific causal story, and the patrimonialist chronology/causal burden cannot be narrated as settled historical origin without the corrections identified by the child audit.

### 2. The technical-method summary resurrects a known acyclicity error

`sintese_programa.md` says compilation is superior to acyclicity because legal systems have a Kelsenian circular structure that acyclicity requirements cannot accommodate. The current readiness record for `pipeline_lean_argdown.md` (#533) establishes that this comparison is not freezeable as written: TAIR was misdescribed, argumentation frameworks can contain cycles, and Lean mutual recursion does not license arbitrary circular proof justification.

The synthesis should instead preserve the narrower distinction among audit surfaces: dialectical topology/semantics in Argdown/argumentation frameworks, formal consequence under explicit assumptions in Lean (including `#print axioms`), and independent legal review of whether the assumptions faithfully encode law and record.

### 3. The synthesis calls a preregistration an empirical evaluation

The synthesis repeatedly describes `empirical_evaluation.md` as supplying/equipping an empirical evaluation in a real TJRO corpus. The current child paper states explicitly that it is a **pre-registered design with no results yet**, that no data has been collected, and that its numeric settings are design parameters rather than measurements. The synthesis must call it a protocol/preregistration until evidence exists.

### 4. ESHTR changed materially after #639 was opened

The newly merged `audits/prior-art/embedding-seeded-tournament-falsification-2026-09-19.md` adds a truth-status correction that materially affects the synthesis:

- local total orders plus an ordering of cluster winners do **not** identify a full global ranking; additional cross-cluster bridges or an explicit score-linking model are required;
- for `n=1000, k=20`, the stated all-pairs-within-cluster protocol requires 24,690 comparisons, not ~1,250, corresponding to about **95.06%** reduction from global all-pairs rather than ~99.7%;
- the Semantic Proximity Hypothesis remains testable but is confounded by comparison difficulty/quality margin and needs a crossed design plus expert ground truth;
- heterogeneous LLM panels do not automatically cancel shared style/presentation bias, and pairwise judging requires pointwise/reference-based and perturbation controls.

Therefore the synthesis should not present ESHTR as an already established reliability mechanism. It may present it as a defined evaluation architecture with corrected output semantics and an explicit validation programme.

### 5. The central institutional bridge is still a programme hypothesis

The sentence that the technical papers `efetivamente reduzem` argumentative cost and thereby change incentives is stronger than the frozen evidence. The defensible synthesis-level statement is a programme hypothesis: if the audited pipeline lowers the measured cost of producing legally valid arguments without degrading quality, then 1E/1F predict specific incentive/reputational consequences that require separate empirical evaluation. That preserves the useful bridge without converting implementation into institutional evidence.
