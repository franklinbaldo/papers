## 4. Findings and actionable issues

### 4.1 Paper 1A — Embargos de Declaração

Issue: #574 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:paper1a-embargos-declaracao`.

The paper remains valuable as a dogmatic synthesis and drafting intervention, but its current originality and doctrinal map are materially out of sync with `audits/prior-art/paper1a-embargos-declaracao-2026-09-18.md`. The audit finds direct pre-cutoff doctrine and case law for the two broad propositions that (a) genuine declaratory defects may concern merits and (b) modification can arise as a necessary consequence of curing the defect rather than as an autonomous principal request.

The strongest concrete blocker is not merely priority language. Current §5.2 presents Nery Jr./Nery as representing a line that requires modification as a separate express request, whereas the publicly verifiable Nery formulation located by the audit states the core opposite: infringement may be a consequence of granting the declaratory motion and should not be the principal request. This attribution must be corrected and the exact edition verified before freeze.

The defensible residual is narrower: the paper's exact replacement drafting prescription and the later explicit `unique determination` boundary for recognitive/integrative cognition, both presented as bounded synthesis/refinement rather than exhaustive firstness. Metadata, current license policy and packaging can be added mechanically after the scientific/doctrinal correction.

### 4.2 Paper 1B — Cinco Saídas

Issue: #575 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:paper1b-cinco-saidas-precedentes`.

The later audit materially changes the novelty map. Broad lower-court rational supersession/nonapplication under art. 489, §1º, VI and the distinction between concrete-case nonapplication and formal source-court revision were already explicit parts of Brazilian doctrinal debate before the paper's cutoff. They cannot carry a broad originality claim in the deposited version.

The exact five-exit organization remains a plausible synthesis contribution. `Aplicação parcial silenciosa` is the strongest narrower candidate for an autonomous category/name, but a close March-2026 partial-application argument exists with unresolved first-public-date provenance. The safe publication boundary is therefore an exact taxonomy/synthesis claim with bounded novelty language, not a first-discovery claim for Exit 4 itself.

### 4.3 Paper 1D — Vinculação Racional e Diálogo Institucional

Issue: #576 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:paper1d-vinculacao-racional`.

This paper has the strongest domain-level blocker in this batch. The audit identifies prior art for generic complaint-as-dialogue, two-way reasoning burdens and lower-court signaling, but more importantly it finds two propositions that are too strong independently of novelty: (1) reason-giving alone is not shown to make every contrary result to an otherwise applicable binding súmula legitimate; and (2) a persuasive lower-court challenge does not automatically impose a duty to formally revise the súmula within the complaint proceeding.

The residual thesis becomes stronger when competence-sensitive: binding effect remains real; the lower court can supply an argumentative signal through legally available distinction/challenge channels; the procedurally available complaint transmits the conflict; the STF owes reasoned engagement with material arguments; preservation, distinction or formal evolution then occurs within the competent mechanism. The initial archival claim should remain scoped to the STF/binding-súmula complaint channel unless separate authority supports generalization to other qualified-precedent regimes.
