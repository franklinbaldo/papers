### Material audit findings now available to the manuscript

The audit establishes that several generic ingredients are prior art or close antecedents and therefore cannot carry component-level novelty:

- Gromov Dynamic Time Warping already aligns ordered trajectories in incomparable spaces using intra-relational geometry;
- Canonical Time Warping and Generalized Time Warping already provide multimodal monotone temporal alignment;
- Kawakita et al. provide a close pre-cutoff example of unlabeled Gromov-Wasserstein alignment between human and LLM similarity structures;
- causal-abstraction literature already treats intervention mappings as part of the model contract;
- narrative/event boundaries are established objects of cognitive/neural study.

The defensible bounded statement is narrower: the audit did not locate a pre-cutoff work containing the full text↔MaleCNS ordered-proxy-intervention conjunction after the recorded searches. This is a bounded negative-search result, not a firstness claim.

The adversarial pass also found material interpretation/control requirements:

1. a positive alignment supports **predictive structural correspondence under the declared interventions**, not by itself shared mechanism, ontology, or computation;
2. GDTW/GWOT and CTW/GTW-style methods must enter the conventional baseline ladder rather than using time-only DTW as the strongest generic control;
3. boundary narrative content must be separated experimentally from endpoint-anchor status;
4. cumulative-prefix gains must survive lexical/participant-overlap and shuffled-order controls, including incremental response-vector ablations;
5. proxy validity should use independently specified/multiple renderers, negative proxy pairs, and no tuning after cross-space response inspection;
6. map/warp capacity should be preregistered, with random/untrained-observer and shuffled-response nulls.
