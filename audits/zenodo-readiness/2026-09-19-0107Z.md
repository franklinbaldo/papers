## 2. Gate vocabulary

`pass` means the present manuscript clears the gate for its declared artifact type. `warn` means a bounded/disclosed limitation that does not alone block archival publication. `fail` means a concrete blocker before the next Zenodo freeze. `n-a` means a gate is not applicable to the declared artifact type/version. Compound values record mixed evidence without false precision.

A missing positive result is not automatically a blocker. Position papers, mathematical notes and experimental programmes can be legitimate Zenodo deposits when claims, literature boundaries, provenance, metadata and limitations match what the frozen version actually establishes.

## 4. Paper findings and created work

### 4.1 Semantic Atlas

Issue: #565 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:semantic-atlas`.

The manuscript already behaves like a responsible programme paper: it marks efficiency, cross-model invariance, controllability and token savings as hypotheses unless backed by a named completed experiment; it separates six claims; and it contains falsification gates. The later planning/control audit nevertheless materially narrows its novelty surface. Meaning-space reachability/controllability, continuous semantic trajectories under control, closed-loop activation steering, latent planning separated from verbalization and token reduction by latent reasoning all have substantial pre-cutoff antecedents.

The defensible contribution is therefore the tighter **full conjunction**: a frozen externally calibrated semantic frame + reusable empirical atlas + explicit transition/reachability/control-cost structure + route planning + closed-loop realization by the original language model + end-to-end efficiency accounting. The base paper must integrate that boundary and pin which later companion results, if any, belong to the frozen archival version rather than silently inheriting them.

### 4.2 Pontifex

Issue: #566 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:pontifex`.

The current root manuscript is still the original July position paper centered on byte-level occlusion, bilateral comparison and a multi-space convergence head. It explicitly reports no empirical results and already warns that its old originality scan is preliminary and not independently verified. The repository now contains multiple later Pontifex/Torus claim-specific audits, including separate cutoffs for occlusion/cartography and Torus Assembly.

The readiness problem is therefore partly **version identity**. The original paper can be archived as a position paper, but its applicable prior-art corrections must be integrated and any broad “no published architecture combines...” language narrowed. Later Torus Assembly claims and evidence must not be backdated into the July paper. If the evolved Torus architecture is incorporated, that should be an explicitly later version with its own claim history.

### 4.3 Claim Provenance in Legal Documents

Issue: #567 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:proveniencia-claims`.

The paper's computational object remains interesting, but the later audit shows that several components are well established: essential versus secondary factual allegations, legal argument graphs, generic backward provenance, proof/source provenance, and first-class unresolved/undecided states. The surviving contribution is narrower: **source-document necessity status + cross-document claim provenance + explicit detection of contingent-in-source becoming load-bearing downstream + pending uncertainty remaining visible in formal dependencies**.

The legal-domain gate also needs work. The manuscript currently states consequences involving devolutive effect, res judicata, reasoning defects and embargos in categorical language. Before freeze those propositions and the Marilene demonstration should be checked against primary/source material and rewritten where the consequence is conditional, contested or case-specific. `pendente` is an acceptable final state when the record does not support a stronger conclusion.

### 4.4 RL Relay Transducers

Issue: #568 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:rl-relay-transducers`.

The position paper is explicit that it reports no implementation/results and contains substantial safety and evaluation controls. A later claim-specific audit of §4.2, however, establishes that the broad semantic-recall → learned-utility-selection idea is prior art (notably MemRL) and that broad delayed credit to retrieved memories is also occupied (notably MemQ), with REPLUG/PRCA/Memento/Memory-R1 providing additional antecedents around frozen/black-box language models and reward-aware retrieval.

The narrower unresolved retrieval mechanism is the conjunction of frozen semantic keys, separate trainable functional vector keys, signed downstream-advantage movement, contextual utility/exploration/cost, relay-level bind/reject/commit control, and frozen/versioned learned retrieval state during held-out evaluation. Because only this subsection has received the strongest temporal audit, the whole relay architecture should receive its own claim-specific pass before Zenodo freeze rather than extrapolating §4.2's result to discrete editing, multi-hop communication, associative memory and co-adaptation claims.

### 4.5 Structural Identification from Restricted Truths

Issue: #569 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:structural-identification`.

This is the closest new candidate in the batch. The manuscript already disclaims novelty for categoricity, Scott sentences, teaching dimension, distinguishing formulas and version spaces, and frames its value as a compact bridge with machine-checked core results. The later audit nevertheless finds that generic version-space isolation, observational-equivalence limits, finite competitor-cover/set-cover geometry and cost-sensitive identification are established machinery, and it identifies a particularly close pre-cutoff August 2026 partial-order paper combining structural objects, version spaces, teaching cost and hitting sets.

The defensible residual is the domain-independent `(M, ~, H, Q, c)` interface with an explicit structural quotient/invariant-query contract, plus its compact Lean formalization and conjunction-collapse warning in that abstraction. The close antecedent should be integrated, and the exact Lean files/revision should be pinned and compiled before the deposit candidate is marked ready.
