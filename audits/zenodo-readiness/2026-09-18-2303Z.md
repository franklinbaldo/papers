## 4. Paper findings and created work

### 4.1 Contract-aware driver rehosting

Issue: #542 — `paper:contract-aware-driver-rehosting`.

The manuscript is already careful to distinguish the demonstrated LiteBox substrate from proposed `.sys` rehosting, synthetic NT ABI, shadow checking and third-party evaluation. That supports a methodology/position-paper deposit without pretending the implementation is complete.

The blocker is claim/literature alignment. `audits/prior-art/contract-aware-driver-rehosting-2026-09-17.md` establishes older antecedents for both broad ingredients: logical bounds inside a physically valid allocation (ASan container-overflow, EffectiveSan/sub-object bounds) and Windows driver execution/emulation outside the normal kernel (x64dbg/driver_unpacking, Speakeasy). The narrower candidate contribution is the Windows `METHOD_BUFFERED` dual-bound detector plus ambiguity-aware promotion and calibrated evidence discipline. Those findings need to be absorbed before freeze.

Metadata, license and packaging remain downstream mechanical blockers: ordered Author refs, explicit Zenodo license/config and a successful `zenodo_prepare.py` bundle from one exact SHA.

### 4.2 Dynamic Quasar Reference Frames

Issue: #543 — `paper:dynamic-quasar-reference-frames`.

The paper is unusually explicit that its vortical/Navier–Stokes language is a hypothesis-generating coordinate choice, not a physical ontology claim. Its central DGCT question is falsifiable, matched against controls, and can be archived before a positive result if the artifact is consistently labeled as a proposal/preprint.

The blocker is the unabsorbed prior-art audit. The generic principles “choose a representation that simplifies dynamics”, “shared dynamics across systems”, and “fixed/shared field plus system-specific residual” have strong antecedents, and SemRF is a close domain-specific pre-cutoff neighbor. The DQRF-specific contribution should be stated around the external-freeze requirement, preregistered low-capacity amplitude and residual-complexity/transfer battery rather than those occupied components.

The freeze must also pin exactly which DGCT protocol/results, if any, belong to the deposited version; absence of a positive result is not itself a blocker.

### 4.3 Embedding-Seeded Hierarchical Tournament Ranking

Issue: #544 — `paper:embedding-seeded-tournament`.

ESHTR remains depositable in principle as a position/methodology paper before empirical validation. Its current broad novelty surface is not ready to freeze, however. The claim-specific audit shows direct/strong partial antecedents for pairwise LLM ranking, divide-and-conquer local ranking plus cross-group representatives (CrowDC), representation-informed pre-ordering (EZ-Sort/Dodgersort), and LLM-judge non-transitivity.

The defensible residual is narrower: semantic *type* clustering changes the comparison graph of a heterogeneous LLM jury because semantic distance is hypothesized to increase cycle inconsistency, followed by a cross-type championship in judicial evaluation. In addition, the main paper needs synchronization with material protocol/limitation refinements already developed in the repository's later ESHTR debate files, without pretending open doctrinal disputes have been resolved.

### 4.4 Forbidden Relay

Issue: #545 — `paper:forbidden-relay`.

The paper's “pre-registered design; no results yet” status is compatible with Zenodo. Its safety and interpretation boundaries are strong: benign targets, synthetic literal bans, independent-observer decoding, side-channel controls and an explicit refusal to infer universal jailbreak capability.

The blocker is literature integration before the preregistration freeze. The dedicated audit shows that RL-learned communication, hidden/recoverable information through LLM text transformations, word/literal blocking robustness, and receiver-relative decodability all have substantial antecedents. The contribution should therefore be attached to the full constrained repeated-relay benchmark conjunction and its transfer/control battery, not to those component phenomena.

Once the literature boundary is corrected, decision-bearing protocol choices must be frozen before data collection; later changes should be prospective amendments rather than silent edits.

### 4.5 Generative Machine Teaching

Issue: #546 — `paper:generative-machine-teaching`.

The manuscript is already explicit that it does not invent generated teaching data and reports no training result. That makes it a legitimate conceptual/pre-empirical Zenodo candidate in principle.

The current contribution boundary nevertheless needs a material correction. The prior-art audit shows that online growing dictionaries with stable references and recursive expansions (LZ78), induced reusable grammars (SEQUITUR/SLPs), learned abstraction libraries/predicate invention, verified cumulative invented predicates (ADVENT), and dynamic/self-tokenizing raw-byte models all predate the paper. The term “self-tokenizing” and generic online vocabulary growth cannot carry component-level novelty.

The defensible candidate is the conjunction of teacher-controlled deterministic procedural demonstrations, primitive-stream external scoring, persistent proof-indexed registry growth, learner-relative pedagogical cost and executable algorithm acquisition.
