## Issue attempted

### #593 — `paper:semantic-tokenization-transformers`

The paper is explicitly a position paper, so absence of empirical results was not treated as a Zenodo blocker. The concrete blocker was that the manuscript still overstated the novelty of semantic-level modeling and offline semantic quantization despite pre-cutoff antecedents, and its evaluation protocol did not yet force the remaining STT-specific composition to beat those closer controls.

Changes merged by PR #615:

1. integrated the dedicated prior-art boundary directly into the manuscript;
2. treated Ippolito et al. (2020) as prior art for sentence-level language modeling over pre-trained embeddings;
3. treated Large Concept Models / Quant-LCM (2024) as the principal pre-cutoff comparator for `pre-trained semantic representation + RVQ + autoregressive higher-level language modeling`, rather than claiming that mechanism as STT novelty;
4. added H-Net and SemToken as prior art for content-aware/semantic token reduction, Kaiser et al. for autoregressive shorter discrete latent sequences, and DReSD as adjacent retrieval-grounded decoding prior art;
5. narrowed the candidate contribution to the reconstruction-oriented conjunction `overlapping semantic chunks -> fixed semantic representation / quantization -> real corpus medoids -> coherent path selection -> overlap stitching -> constrained normalization`;
6. replaced broad architectural novelty language with a matched baseline ladder including continuous sentence/concept modeling, Quant-LCM/RVQ, semantic-token reduction, a conventional learned decoder, nearest-representative retrieval, medoid/path, stitching, normalization, and long-context baselines;
7. added explicit stage-wise error decomposition: E1 semantic-code prediction, E2 representative retrieval, E3 path selection, E4 overlap stitching, and E5 normalization additions/deletions or meaning changes;
8. clarified that a gain attributable only to a more powerful final normalizer must not be credited to semantic tokenization;
9. softened remaining overstrong prospective language: decoding properties are measurement targets, training/hyperparameter choices are proposed, and computational-budget figures are explicitly illustrative planning estimates rather than measurements;
10. added ordered canonical author metadata using `/authors/franklin-silveira-baldo.md` with byline `Franklin Silveira Baldo`;
11. set `publication.status: ready`, target `zenodo`, `publication_type: preprint`, version `0.1`, `access_right: open`, and `license: cc-by-nc-4.0` under the settled repository policy.
