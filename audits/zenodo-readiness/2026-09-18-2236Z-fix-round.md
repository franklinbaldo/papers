### Why automation stopped

Selecting a public-content license is a legal/copyright grant, not a clerical inference. The repository currently contains a canonical author identity and the Zenodo packager's self-test uses `cc-by-4.0`, but that fixture is not an author instruction or repository-wide license policy. Likewise, deciding whether the present uncalibrated v0.1 is the intended archival release is an authorial publication decision.

The routine therefore did **not** invent either choice, did **not** mark `publication.status: ready`, and did **not** close #531. Once the two decisions are recorded on the issue, the remaining author metadata, Zenodo frontmatter and packaging check are mechanical and can return to automation.
