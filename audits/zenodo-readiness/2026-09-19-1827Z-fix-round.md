## Issue attempted: #574 — `paper:paper1a-embargos-declaracao`

Paper: `paper1A_embargos_declaracao.md`.

The live manuscript still contained all of the blockers recorded by #574, including an objectively incorrect statutory citation: it treated CPC art. 1.024 §3 as the provision governing modification after Embargos de Declaração, although current §3 governs conversion to agravo interno and §4 governs the situation in which granting the embargos modifies the challenged decision.

### Corrections applied

The candidate was rewritten as a bounded dogmatic preprint rather than preserving superseded originality claims.

1. corrected every modification-related reference from CPC art. 1.024 §3 to **§4**, while separately identifying §3 as the conversion-to-agravo-interno rule;
2. added art. 1.023 §2 as the pre-judgment adversarial-hearing safeguard when eventual grant may modify the decision;
3. removed the historical claim that CPC/2015 originated the consequence-not-autonomous-request architecture;
4. corrected the attribution to Nelson Nery Jr. / Rosa Maria de Andrade Nery: their publicly reproduced pre-CPC/2015 formulation is treated as antecedent support for infringement as a consequence rather than the main request;
5. integrated the claim-specific audit's boundary: `mode, not object` and modification-as-consequence are treated as established doctrine/case-law lineage rather than discoveries;
6. retained only the narrower practical drafting prescription and the later `unique determination` cognitive boundary as bounded residual proposals, expressly without exhaustive-firstness claims;
7. distinguished `unique determination` from direction-without-uniqueness and stated that the latter may require genuinely generative merits cognition;
8. retained the paper as dogmatic/analytical work and explicitly disclaimed empirical frequency claims;
9. added direct source-level references to the CPC, Mattos, Monteiro Neto, Talamini, Lima and representative STJ records;
10. added canonical ordered author metadata via `/authors/franklin-silveira-baldo.md`;
11. added repository-standard Zenodo metadata: `publication.status: ready`, `publication_type: preprint`, version `0.1`, `access_right: open`, `license: cc-by-nc-4.0`.

### Source verification

The statutory correction was rechecked against the current compiled CPC. The current rule is unambiguous:

- art. 1.024 §3: conversion of declaratory motions to internal appeal when that is the proper remedy, after opportunity to supplement the reasons;
- art. 1.024 §4: rights of the opposing party when granting declaratory motions modifies the original decision.

The Nery boundary was also rechecked against publicly accessible pre-CPC/2015 reproductions, including Carlos Alberto Barbosa de Mattos (2006), which places the infringement as a consequence of granting the declaratory motion rather than its principal request.
