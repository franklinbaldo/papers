## 3. Claims and prior-art boundary

The manuscript already avoids the most serious overclaim. It derives that, for the motivating forced incompressible Navier–Stokes scaling, core kinetic energy tends to zero and a constant-density areal-density proxy also tends to zero even as characteristic velocity and shear diverge. It therefore explicitly rejects the naive interpretation that the singular core is itself a finite-energy fusion hotspot.

The dated audit `audits/prior-art/vortex-cumulation-fusion-2026-09-18.md` reconstructs the earliest public cutoff for the audited claim family as PR #430 at **2026-09-09 00:20:37 UTC**. It materially narrows the novelty boundary:

- the motivating core-energy scaling is source-derived from the 8 September OpenAI result;
- generic self-similar magnetic cumulation predates the paper by decades;
- specific-energy cumulation for fusion ignition already appears in cumulation-jet work;
- deliberate rotation/shear as a stabilizing variable in fusion implosions is established prior art;
- magnetized plasma vortices enhancing viscous/Ohmic heating and vortex-to-thermal conversion are established phenomena;
- Sam et al. already provide a fusion-specific MagLIF bridge in which magnetized viscosity damps vortical structures and converts their kinetic energy into heat;
- Beresnyak et al.'s Mag Noh family is a particularly close structural antecedent because it combines self-similarity, rotation, magnetized implosion/stagnation, stability analysis and hotspot motivation.

Accordingly, the archival candidate contribution should not be “vortex/shear heating for fusion”, “rotating self-similar MHD implosion”, “specific-energy cumulation for ignition”, or “rotation can stabilize an implosion”. The unresolved synthesis is narrower: use the **specific 8 September 2026 anisotropic inward-spiral/axial-stretching forced-Navier–Stokes scaling** as a candidate drive trajectory; continue it into compressible resistive MHD; require density and magnetic-confinement buildup before deliberately accelerated viscous/Ohmic thermalization; and test incremental hotspot coupling at matched driver work against ordinary compression, ordinary rotation/shear, a Mag-Noh-like self-similar rotating-MHD control, and a vortical but non-cumulating control.

The prior-art audit's bounded post-cutoff search did not locate a work reproducing that full conjunction. That remains a negative-search result, not an exhaustive novelty proof.

## 5. Adversarial review, contrary evidence and limitations

This manuscript already contains substantial evidence **against** its motivating intuition, rather than hiding it. The vanishing core-energy result and unfavorable constant-density areal-density trend directly refute the strongest naive “blowup as energy concentration” reading. Its nine falsifiers include vanishing useful mass, shock preemption, premature viscous damping, wrong thermal partition, magnetic back-reaction, instability dominance, no coupling advantage, extreme forcing sensitivity and early continuum breakdown.

The prior-art layer adds important boundary evidence rather than simple support. Vortex-to-thermal conversion is stage-dependent rather than automatically favorable, and older similarity/implosion work warns that exact solutions may be fragile or non-attracting. These findings do not falsify the narrow C7 research programme, but they constrain its truth status: a positive result requires a robust finite pre-singular window with an incremental matched-work advantage, not merely strong shear or a visually similar vortex.

For Publication Readiness this is a strength. An exploratory paper can be archived before the experiment succeeds when it states exactly what would make the hypothesis wrong. The present blocker is therefore literature integration and claim freezing, not scientific tier or absence of a positive result.
