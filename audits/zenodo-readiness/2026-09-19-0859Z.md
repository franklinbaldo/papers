## 3. Claims and contribution boundary

The manuscript is already conservative in several important ways. It does not equate local order, predictability or compressibility with agency; it makes the maximum-entropy null observer-relative rather than a literal equilibrium cosmology; it keeps a structured non-agent law as a first-class comparator; and it requires prospective prediction/intervention rather than retrospective fit alone.

The repository's claim-specific audit, `audits/prior-art/informational-time-critical-recognition-2026-09-18.md`, reconstructed a separate public cutoff for the companion's stronger four-family criterion: **2026-07-31 03:28:13 UTC**. The audit finds substantial pre-cutoff occupation of the component machinery:

- intentional/agent descriptions justified by predictive usefulness;
- Bayesian inverse planning and hidden-goal inference;
- agency attribution from non-random structure;
- observer-relative predictive agency detection;
- first-threshold-crossing sequential evidence;
- prequential prediction and MDL model selection;
- active recognition as a way to accelerate recognition.

Accordingly, the manuscript must not imply novelty for those components in isolation.

The narrower candidate for which the audit did **not** locate a material pre-cutoff antecedent is the conjunction: compare `M_agent` against observer-relative maximum entropy, self-generation and the strongest admissible structured non-agent law; charge the full model/registry/search/parameter cost; require prospective held-out or intervention-sensitive advantage; and, when tied back to Informational Time, measure the first stable crossing in observer-relative accumulated recoverable causal cost.

That is a bounded negative-search result, not a priority proof. The usefulness of the conjunction remains unvalidated until direct baseline testing.

## 5. Domain and adversarial review

Domain review is `pass/warn`. The maximum-entropy null is explicitly conditional on the observer's current information; the rare-pattern argument is qualified by nonzero probability and approximately independent opportunities; and the note correctly separates evidence for an external continuing source from evidence for agency.

The principal remaining conceptual sensitivity is model-class fairness. A richer `M_agent` family can beat a narrower `M_law` family merely because it has more expressive capacity. The current note already charges model/registry/search/parameter cost and warns against winning by flexibility alone, which is strong. The archival version should make capacity matching/model-class sensitivity an explicit benchmark limitation.

Adversarial review is therefore `pass/warn`: the four-family null structure, finite-transcript underdetermination, prospective testing, interventions and causal-scale uncertainty are meaningful controls, but the eventual empirical programme must compare against strong structured non-agent models rather than random/stationary strawmen.

## 6. Reproducibility, evidence and limitations

No empirical result is claimed in this note. Therefore a Zenodo deposit as a conceptual companion/preprint does not require completed agent-recognition experiments. The empirical-evidence portion of this gate is `n-a`; reproducibility is `warn` only because the exact criterion, model classes, thresholds and eventual baselines should be frozen/versioned before they are treated as a preregistered benchmark.

The dedicated prior-art audit already proposes the right direct controls:

1. WCD-like prefix recognition assuming agency;
2. Bayesian inverse-planning posterior threshold;
3. SPRT likelihood-ratio stopping time;
4. prequential-MDL model selection;
5. complexity-matched structured-law control;
6. the full retrospective-cost + prospective-test criterion measured in `tau_I` rather than raw observation count;
7. passive versus active recognition under equalized budgets.

The limitations gate is `pass/warn`. The note explicitly disclaims metaphysical certainty, fixes no privileged biological ontology, and allows the recognition scale to be organismal, collective, institutional or otherwise distributed. Before freeze it should additionally say plainly that the component machinery is inherited and that the full four-family criterion has not yet demonstrated better calibration, false-positive control or informational efficiency than the established baselines above.
