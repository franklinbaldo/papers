## 4. Contrary evidence and truth status

The new audit deliberately separates priority from truth status and materially lowers confidence in a generic MaleCNS-topology advantage:

1. this paper's own v0.1 comparison is a direct null for higher-order topology under the tested regime: MaleCNS `0.2753 ± 0.0682` F1 versus degree-preserving shuffle `0.2782 ± 0.0610`, paired difference `-0.00292 ± 0.02840`;
2. Suárez et al. / `conn2res` report that the fruit-fly empirical connectome does not significantly outperform 500 rewired nulls at peak memory capacity (`p = 0.11`) in their cross-species reservoir experiment;
3. *Flies Are All You Need* reports a parameter-matched direct-input control slightly better than its MaleCNS route in all three fitted seeds, so graph participation does not establish architectural advantage;
4. Costi et al. show that biological-versus-classical reservoir ordering can reverse as recurrent spectral radius changes, making dynamics a material boundary condition;
5. McAllister et al. provide an alternative mechanism in which biological wiring may primarily improve robustness/stability under sparsity or perturbation rather than mean task score;
6. nanoFLY's later TinyStories checkpoint points in the opposite direction — real MaleCNS wiring beating a degree-matched shuffle — but with only one seed per condition, supporting task/dynamics/regime dependence rather than a universal conclusion.

This evidence does **not** require abandoning the paper's hypothesis or rewriting the frozen negative result. It does require lower prior confidence in topology advantage and makes “undertraining” only one diagnostic hypothesis among several rather than the privileged explanation for the null.

## 5. Citations and domain review

The current Related Work already credits `conn2res`, Morra et al., Costi et al., reservoir NLP sequence labeling, and *Flies Are All You Need*. It does not yet incorporate the closer pre-cutoff nanoFLY design or the new truth-status evidence as explicit limitations/boundary conditions.

Before Zenodo freeze, the paper should therefore:

- credit nanoFLY as `partial_prior_art` for MaleCNS-language plus degree-matched shuffled-wiring control logic;
- state that `conn2res` supplies a relevant fruit-fly rewired-null non-advantage result rather than citing it only as generic connectome-reservoir background;
- use *Flies Are All You Need* not only as a component antecedent but also as contrary evidence for a broad MaleCNS-language architectural-advantage expectation;
- state explicitly that Costi et al. make the conclusion recurrence-regime dependent;
- avoid implying that longer training is the default causal explanation for the v0.1 null.

The domain contract remains sound: the manuscript distinguishes engineered leaky-`tanh` dynamics from Drosophila physiology and makes the MaleCNS-versus-shuffle comparison the topology test. The new evidence strengthens the need to scope any future positive topology claim to the frozen dynamics unless a predeclared gain/leak sensitivity analysis shows robustness.

## 6. Adversarial review and future discriminating test

The paper already passes an important adversarial-quality threshold because it reports the negative/indeterminate result as a first-class result rather than optimizing it away. The new audit adds a stronger discriminating rule for any future confirmatory version:

- freeze the selected training regime;
- evaluate on untouched `test.jsonl`;
- require a predeclared practically meaningful paired improvement over **both** the degree-preserving shuffled recurrent null and a conventional sequential baseline such as a GRU or temporal CNN;
- if MaleCNS fails that hurdle, retire or materially downgrade the topology-advantage claim for this task/version instead of escalating reservoir size as the next explanation;
- if the claim is intended to generalize beyond v1 dynamics, include a small predeclared recurrent-gain/leak sensitivity check.

These future controls are not prerequisites for depositing the completed v0.1 negative baseline. They are constraints on what a later positive biological-topology claim would need to establish.

## 8. Limitations

The manuscript already discloses the tiny validation set, developmental validation use, untouched test split and non-biological engineered dynamics. The new audit adds limitations that should be made explicit in the archived text:

- generic MaleCNS topology advantage now has materially lower prior plausibility than the first readiness snapshot implied;
- the v0.1 null is consistent with genuine no-advantage, inadequate training, task mismatch, dynamical-regime mismatch, or topology benefits appearing in robustness rather than mean F1;
- no one of those explanations should be privileged without a discriminating control;
- later positive nanoFLY evidence and negative/simpler-control evidence elsewhere currently point to regime dependence rather than a stable universal effect.
