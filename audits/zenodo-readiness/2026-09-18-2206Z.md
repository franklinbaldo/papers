## 4. Findings by paper

### 4.1 `schmidhuber_meter.md`

**Readiness:** blocked.

The paper is appropriately scoped as a methodology and repeatedly separates observable citation deficit from plagiarism, intent, negligence, and causal derivation. It also has an explicit limitations section and a deterministic v0.1 specification. Those are strengths for a citable exploratory artifact.

The scientific blocker is that the metric itself has no claim-specific prior-art audit yet. The current bibliography covers sleeping beauties, the Matthew effect, obliteration by incorporation, and reviewer-identified missing citations, but a deposit freeze should first compare the method against the broader missing-citation/recommendation, citation-context, scientific-credit, priority, and claim-overlap literature. The aggregation and interpretation bands are also explicitly uncalibrated; that is acceptable for a methodology preprint only if the status remains conspicuous.

Mechanical blockers are ordered author refs, explicit Zenodo license/config, `publication.status: ready`, and a successful `zenodo_prepare.py` bundle.

**Issue:** #531 — `[paper:schmidhuber-meter] Zenodo readiness — prior art and deposit metadata`.

### 4.2 `semantic_atlas_static_geometry.md`

**Readiness:** blocked, but closest reviewed candidate to a defensible deposit.

Claims, preregistration discipline, reproducibility, negative boundaries, and limitations are strong. The manuscript identifies its frozen protocol, terminal findings record, corpus hash, code/results/manifests and durable data release. It explicitly limits the result to static partial local alignment plus observer-specific neighborhood structure.

The blocker is unusually clean: the repository already has a strong prior-art audit (`audits/prior-art/semantic-atlas-static-geometry-2026-09-18.md`), but the manuscript itself does not yet credit the direct methodological antecedents identified there. In particular, nearest-neighbor overlap, mKNN representation alignment, shared local geometry, observer-dependent semantic neighborhoods, and permutation/null calibration cannot be left outside the deposited manuscript while the empirical conjunction is presented as the paper's contribution.

After a concise Related Work integration, the remaining work is mostly Zenodo metadata/license/packaging.

**Issue:** #532 — `[paper:semantic-atlas-static-geometry] Zenodo readiness — integrate prior art and freeze deposit metadata`.

### 4.3 `pipeline_lean_argdown.md`

**Readiness:** blocked on a substantive correctness revision.

The repository's own audit (`audits/prior-art/pipeline-lean-argdown-2026-09-18.md`) establishes a material correction that has not yet been absorbed: TAIR is misdescribed as enforcing an acyclicity gate; Dung-style argumentation frameworks are not generally acyclic; and Lean mutual recursion does not justify arbitrary circular proof dependency. The current comparison therefore cannot be externally frozen as written.

The surviving contribution is narrower and stronger: traceability across Argdown dialectical structure, Lean consequence under explicit assumptions / `#print axioms`, a separate legal adequacy review of those assumptions, defeat synthesis, and forensic translation. The manuscript also contains unresolved `[CITE ...]` placeholders.

Metadata/license/packaging remain downstream blockers but should not be fixed before the substantive correction.

**Issue:** #533 — `[paper:pipeline-lean-argdown] Zenodo readiness — correct acyclicity framing and resolve citations`.

### 4.4 `affordance_restriction.md`

**Readiness:** blocked.

A position-paper deposit does not require the PINK validation roadmap to be completed. The blocker is epistemic, not lack of experiments. The existing audit (`audits/prior-art/affordance-restriction-2026-09-17.md`) finds direct antecedents for deterministic action shielding/runtime authority, parameterized action schemas, approval/execution integrity, and cryptographic capability/provenance binding. The manuscript's candidate contribution should therefore live in the specific composition of those pieces, especially the human-readable content-addressed scenario canon plus doctrine/procedure governance asymmetry.

Explicit `[CITATION NEEDED]` markers remain in the manuscript. Those are hard freeze blockers. Prospective deployment/validation statements may remain if clearly labeled and provenance-backed.

**Issue:** #534 — `[paper:affordance-restriction] Zenodo readiness — absorb prior-art audit and close citation debt`.

### 4.5 `empirical_evaluation.md`

**Readiness:** blocked today, but lack of results is **not** itself a Zenodo blocker.

The manuscript already states that no data have been collected and that numerical values are design parameters. This makes it a legitimate candidate for a timestamped protocol/preregistration deposit. The wording must be harmonized so title/abstract/introduction cannot be mistaken for a completed empirical-results paper, and all decision-bearing design choices must be frozen prospectively.

Issue #349 already records the missing external preregistration link, so this round did not duplicate that task. The new readiness issue treats #349 as a dependency and focuses on protocol identity, metadata, license and packaging.

**Issue:** #536 — `[paper:empirical-evaluation] Zenodo readiness — freeze as preregistration, not results paper`.

## 7. Cross-cutting observation

The current packager already provides a useful hard mechanical contract: ordered author refs, `publication.status: ready`, Zenodo target/config, explicit license, and provenance-hashed output. Most current papers predate that frontmatter adoption, so metadata/packaging will recur as a mechanical blocker.

That should **not** lead to dozens of identical metadata-only issues. The fix routine should preferably add the common publication metadata when it is already touching a paper for a substantive readiness issue, while future audit rounds should open new issues only when there is a paper-specific blocker or a genuinely actionable delta.
