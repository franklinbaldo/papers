## 2. Gate vocabulary

`pass` means the current frozen text clears the gate for its declared artifact type. `warn` means a disclosed or bounded limitation that does not by itself forbid archival publication. `fail` means a concrete blocker for the next Zenodo freeze. `n-a` means the gate is not applicable to the declared artifact type/version. Compound states such as `warn/fail` indicate that the paper has good discipline in part of the gate but still contains a concrete freeze blocker.

A missing positive result is not automatically a failure. Position papers, protocols, preregistrations and negative-result empirical papers can be valid Zenodo deposits if their claims, provenance, limitations and metadata match what was actually established.

## 4. Paper findings and created work

### 4.1 Informational Time

Issue: #553 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:informational-time`.

The paper is already unusually careful about category mistakes. It explicitly denies that informational time replaces physical time, distinguishes causal history from symbolic compression, separates work from depth, and marks recursive-tokenization / recognition claims as hypotheses. Those features make a position-paper deposit scientifically legitimate in principle even without a completed benchmark.

The freeze blocker is that the later claim-specific audit materially narrows the seven listed contributions. Pre-cutoff work already occupies causal-depth-as-time-like structure, actual-path versus endpoint/minimal-depth distinctions, work/span, prefix-length recognition, active recognition, intentional/inverse-planning interpretations of agency, sequential first-threshold-crossing and complexity/predictive model-selection machinery. In particular, the audit identifies a July 2026 causal-depth preprint that predates the public cutoff by several days.

The surviving candidate contribution is narrower: a registry-relative path quantity that accumulates coded recoverable causal distinctions, coupled to a directional agent-recognition criterion that charges representation/model/search cost and requires prospective held-out advantage over strong structured non-agent alternatives. The stronger four-family refinement in `informational_time_negentropy_clarifications.md` has its own later cutoff and must not be backdated into the original paper's priority history.

Before freeze, the audit must be integrated into Related Foundations and contribution wording, ordered Author metadata / explicit license / Zenodo configuration must be added, and the exact candidate SHA must successfully pass `zenodo_prepare.py`.

### 4.2 The Interstitial Agent

Issue: #554 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:interstitial-agent`.

The paper's strongest current assets are its boundaries: it separates surface from functional persistence, direct generation from downstream reconstruction, local from end-to-end policy scope, and contains an explicit `What This Is Not` plus substantial failure-mode and staged-disclosure discussion. Lack of a completed relay implementation is therefore not a publication blocker for a position-paper version.

The claim boundary is not yet ready to freeze. The repository prior-art audit finds strong pre-cutoff antecedents for learned communication protocols, receiver-relative hidden information, encoded reasoning, generative-agent collusion/steganography, system/path-level monitoring, and — most importantly — local-benign/global-unsafe composition. `Semantic Intent Fragmentation` already uses the term `compositional safety gap`, and a July 2026 multi-agent mediation paper is a close pre-cutoff relay/transformation antecedent.

The defensible residual is the conjunction in which a learned **external discrete relay** between otherwise black-box LLM calls, trained from end-to-end reward, is itself treated as a safety-relevant policy locus and is evaluated jointly with topology, memory and receiver capabilities. The manuscript needs to credit the direct lineage and stop assigning novelty to the generic composition/covert-channel ingredients before Zenodo freeze.

### 4.3 Machine Discovery

Issue: #555 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:machine-discovery`.

The manuscript already decomposes correctness, novelty, provenance, autonomy, significance and downstream usefulness rather than collapsing them into a single “AI discovery” label. Its public-epistemic-state formalism and extensional-versus-resource-bounded distinction make it a coherent framework artifact even before a full benchmark is run.

The dedicated audit nevertheless shows that many broad components are occupied: automated science itself, scientific progress as enlargement of knowledge, external institutional gates for AI-generated research, discovery as a verified state/regime transition, time-relative/frozen-snapshot novelty, and machine-created reusable theorem libraries that affect later proof search. The negative re-evaluation of LEGO-Prover is especially important because it demonstrates why mere library growth plus aggregate performance improvement is insufficient evidence that learned artifacts caused recursive productivity.

The surviving candidate synthesis is therefore public-state admission + frozen snapshot/equivalence audit + provenance/machine-contribution accounting + explicit separation of correctness/novelty/significance + **resource-charged held-out downstream fertility**. The current paper needs to make that residual explicit, integrate the closest audit findings and package one exact version with author/licensing metadata.

### 4.4 MaleCNS frozen-reservoir legal sequence tagging

Issue: #556 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:malecns-connectome-reservoir-tagging`.

This is the closest paper in this batch to an archiveable empirical object. The manuscript reports the completed five-seed baseline with a matched directed degree-preserving shuffled recurrent null and byte-only baseline. MaleCNS does **not** beat the shuffled control on mean validation F1 (`0.2753` versus `0.2782`; paired difference `-0.0029 ± 0.0284`), and the paper says so directly rather than hiding the negative result. It also records the legal-data commit, connectome hashes, seeds, workflow run and artifact digest, while reserving the test split for later confirmation.

The main scientific blocker is literature synchronization rather than a missing positive result. The prior-art audit finds that connectome-as-reservoir computation, Drosophila reservoirs, topology/weight ablations and reservoir NLP sequence labeling all predate the study. More importantly, `Flies Are All You Need` publicly used MaleCNS v1.0 with language input, a frozen anatomical reservoir, trained readout and a direct no-graph control on 2026-09-11 — two days before this paper's claim-specific cutoff. The manuscript currently cites Costi et al. but does not yet incorporate this closest antecedent.

The narrow experimental contribution that remains defensible is the **byte-level Portuguese legal span-tagging package** with the degree-preserving topology-destroying recurrent null plus the non-recurrent byte baseline under paired conditions. Zenodo can legitimately preserve the present negative baseline as a versioned empirical result; a later diagnostic or untouched-test confirmatory run should become a subsequent version rather than retroactively rewriting this result.

### 4.5 Structured Irregularity / Pedagogical Signal Extraction

Issue: #557 — labels `zenodo-readiness`, `paper:pedagogical-signal-extraction`.

The paper explicitly reports no empirical results and labels progressive decodability / optimal pedagogy as hypotheses. The teacher-channel-learner model, primitive next-bit scoring contract and distinction between exogenous noise, endogenous opacity, accidental pattern and concealment are conceptually clear enough for a position-paper deposit in principle.

The prior-art audit narrows the novelty surface substantially. Learner-relative optimal teaching is established machine-teaching machinery; sequential curricula and learning-progress objectives are old; teaching under uncertainty/noise is established; retrospective revaluation shows that later evidence can change the significance of earlier cues; and minimal predictive representations have deep computational-mechanics / predictive-information antecedents.

The narrower unresolved contribution is the combination of **learner-relative temporary opacity** with a concrete post-unlock ablation: after later decoding structure becomes available, remove a specific earlier observation from the completed history and ask whether held-out future/task loss worsens (`R_{i,t}`). The manuscript should center this operational distinction rather than generic curriculum, revaluation or predictive-invariant claims, while keeping the four-way taxonomy explicitly provisional where the literature search only establishes bounded negative evidence.
