## What I found in the supportive round 9

The supportive accepted the per-ementa/per-pair granularity distinction as logically correct — the round 9 adversarial's central claim was not contested on logical grounds. The supportive then pressed reliability through three institutional mechanisms of STF secretariat authorship that decouple ementa vocabulary level from vote-thread specificity level:

**Mechanism (a) — upward abstraction:** Doctrinal-specificity vote threads are abstracted upward to abstract principle vocabulary in the ementa. This is the paradigm where the criteria work: principle-level ementa classification correctly predicts genuine entanglement-class thread populations. Named as the mechanism where the adversarial's criteria succeed.

**Mechanism (b) — institutional specificity override:** Abstract-principle vote reasoning produces specific-provision ementa citations inserted by the secretariat for institutional anchoring. The criteria classify these ementas as concrete-specificity (correctly by vocabulary), but the thread population reasons at abstract-principle level. The adversarial had not identified this as a reliability failure direction — the criteria under-classify entanglement for mechanism (b), classifying as concrete-specificity when the actual thread-ementa relationship may involve abstract threads against specific-provision ementas.

**Mechanism (c) — cross-case precedential abstraction:** Ementas abstracted above the specificity of any individual thread for cross-case applicability. Criteria classify as principle-level, but the thread population is heterogeneous — mechanism (a)-type threads, tractable threads, and intermediate threads coexist under the same per-ementa classification.

The reliability argument: establishing whether principle-level-classified ementas correspond to entanglement-class thread populations (mechanism (a)) requires examining individual vote threads — per-pair examination — to determine which mechanism generated the ementa's vocabulary level. This is the thread-level comparison the criteria were designed to avoid. The circularity survives in refined form: the criteria are logically distinct from the (A)/(B) determination but cannot substitute for it in per-thread entanglement prediction without per-pair calibration.

## What I assessed

**Mechanism (a) dominance: not documentable.**

Mechanism (a) is the most mechanistically predicted explanation for why contested constitutional ementas operate at a higher abstraction level than individual vote threads. The structural logic of STF colegiado decisions — justices writing individual votes at doctrinal specificity, secretariat synthesizing at principle-level — points to upward abstraction as the standard secretariat function.

This structural prediction does not constitute empirical documentation. I have no access to STF secretariat drafting guidelines, ementa/vote comparative specificity studies, or scholarship specifically establishing mechanism (a) as the dominant mechanism for STF constitutional adjudication. The synthesis prescribed honesty on this question; the honest answer is that I cannot document mechanism (a) dominance.

Mechanisms (b) and (c) are asserted by the supportive as systematic features of secretariat practice, not exceptional cases. The structural prediction for mechanism (a) dominance cannot override the supportive's structural claim about mechanisms (b) and (c) without empirical evidence on relative mechanism frequency.

**Consequence: path (c) is the honest terminal form for §3.9.**

## What I decided to argue

Path (c) — operational equivalence concession — is accepted for the §3.9 abstraction-level check claim. The corpus-level criteria cannot establish their reliability for per-thread entanglement prediction without per-pair calibration examination:
- Confirming which principle-level-classified ementas correspond to mechanism (a)-type thread populations requires examining individual vote threads for those ementas
- This calibration is the per-pair comparison the criteria were designed to avoid
- The circularity therefore survives in refined form at the calibration level

The consequence: the triply-modified scheme's abstraction-level check converges on the taxonomy's (A)/(B) determination through the same analysis for the SC7 entanglement subclass. The schemes reach the same epistemic outcome through the same path, not through independent mechanisms. The taxonomy's necessary/contingent approach for this subclass is confirmed rather than replaced.

The §3.9 debate terminates with this acceptance. The "refinement artifact" characterization was the adversarial's claim that the triply-modified scheme could achieve the taxonomy's epistemic accuracy through a genuinely independent corpus preprocessing mechanism; that claim is now retracted.

## What I considered and discarded

**Contesting the three mechanisms on frequency grounds without empirical evidence.** I could have argued that mechanisms (b) and (c) are institutional possibilities but not dominant patterns — that the structural prediction for mechanism (a) is strong enough to support the criteria's reliability as a working assumption pending empirical verification. This argument is structurally available but requires establishing that the criteria are reliable enough for practical use even if not validated against a calibration corpus. The synthesis's prescribed path was explicit: mechanism (a) dominance must be documentable for the criteria to survive, and I cannot document it. Pressing the criteria on structural-prediction grounds alone, without empirical documentation, would not constitute genuine adversarial argument — it would be contesting an empirical claim with a structural prediction.

**Accepting mechanism (a) as dominant and contesting (b) and (c) as exceptional cases.** The synthesis warned explicitly: "Filing a round 10 that contests mechanisms (b) and (c) without engaging mechanism (a) dominance will not advance the debate." Contesting (b) and (c) without addressing the dominance question was prescribed as the wrong move.

**Partial path (c): accepting the concession for mechanism (c) while contesting (b).** Mechanism (b) is the most specific reliability failure direction — under-classifying entanglement. I could have accepted path (c) for mechanism (c) while pressing that mechanism (b) is also an under-classification case (concrete-specificity criteria for abstract-thread ementas) rather than an over-classification case, making it a reliability failure in a different direction than the adversarial's original claim about entanglement prediction. This precision is real but doesn't change the bottom line: both mechanisms require per-pair calibration to assess frequency and impact, and that calibration is the thread-level examination the criteria were designed to avoid. The full path (c) concession is cleaner.

**Pressing a new front: the criteria's reliability failure for mechanism (c) is systematically in the over-classification direction.** Under mechanism (c), the criteria classify as principle-level ementas whose thread populations are heterogeneous — some mechanism (a)-type (genuine entanglement), some tractable, some intermediate. The criteria systematically over-apply (pendente) to the tractable subset of the mechanism (c) thread population. This is a direction-specific reliability failure the adversarial could press. But this argument requires showing mechanism (c) has non-trivial frequency and that the tractable subset within mechanism (c) populations is significant — both empirical questions without available evidence. The full path (c) concession is the honest move.
