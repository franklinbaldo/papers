## What I found in supportive round 14

Supportive round 14 is the most analytically precise response in the Paper 1B bilateral debate. It makes three contributions:

**The joint-structure exception** — the canonical predicate-to-criterion ordering applies in single-provision taxonomies because the predicate has pre-theoretical content settled independently of criterion selection. The jointly constituted taxonomy is a different structure: there is no pre-CPC 2015 "saídas legítimas" concept with settled compliance-permissibility content that the provisions were designed to implement. The predicate content and the classification criterion are jointly constituted by the provisions' operation — neither is prior to the other. Therefore the canonical ordering's premise (predicate content settled before criterion selected) is unavailable in this taxonomy, and the adversarial's fourteenth-ground deployment of canonical direction was applying a single-provision ordering to a jointly constituted structure.

This is a genuine structural advance. The fourteenth ground did attempt to use the canonical direction as a structural resource independent of the semantic content question. The joint-structure exception correctly identifies the limitation: the canonical ordering is not applicable without pre-settled predicate content, and within the jointly constituted taxonomy, both parties have accepted that predicate content is not settled prior to the provisions' joint operation.

**The presupposition-symmetry extension to directionality vocabulary** — the canonical ordering runs in the adversarial's direction only if "legítima"'s natural content is compliance-permissibility; it would run in the defense's direction only if "legítima"'s natural content is domain-membership. The ordering requires the semantic question to be answered before determining which direction to run — making it subject to presupposition symmetry at the directionality level in the same way as prior structural vocabularies.

This too is a genuine advance. The fourteenth ground argued that the canonical ordering operates "before" the semantic content question — that it is a structural observation about taxonomic derivation prior to semantic engagement. The presupposition-symmetry extension correctly shows that this independence claim doesn't hold: the canonical direction itself presupposes the semantic premise it was meant to help establish.

**The third path for form-recognition in jointly constituted taxonomies** — predicate content jointly constituted; art. 489's role is to provide recognition conditions; the content those conditions produce is "recognized response types in the ordinary processing domain"; this is neither a hollow predicate nor the contested semantic premise restated in natural-content vocabulary, because it derives from the structural role-assignment within the joint framework rather than from a pre-theoretical assertion.

This is where the adversarial's round 15 response is focused. The third path changes the vocabulary from natural-content ("legítima" naturally means domain-membership) to role-assignment ("art. 489's recognition-conditions role produces ordinary-processing-domain content"). But the content specification itself — "ordinary processing domain" — is what the form-recognition reading claims. Changing the vocabulary from natural-content to role-assignment doesn't change what is being claimed about what art. 489 produces within the joint operation.

## What I decided to argue

**Accept the joint-structure exception.** The canonical ordering requires pre-settled predicate content. Within the jointly constituted taxonomy, that content is not available prior to the provisions' joint operation. The adversarial cannot apply the canonical ordering within a structure both parties have accepted as jointly constituted without presupposing a pre-theoretical predicate content that the joint-constitution framework precludes. This acceptance closes the fourteenth-ground argument cleanly — it was the correct structural path given the accepted framework, and the path is correctly identified as unavailable.

**Accept the presupposition-symmetry extension.** The directionality argument presupposes the semantic content at the directionality level. This is the same presupposition pattern identified at the filtering level (round 11), the domain-status level (rounds 12–13), and now the directionality level (round 14). Presupposition symmetry at the directionality level is the accurate structural characterization of what the fourteenth ground did: it articulated the authorization-based reading's semantic premise in canonical-ordering vocabulary rather than in authorization-filter or domain-status vocabulary.

**Contest the third path at the content-specification level.** The third path's contribution is real but limited: it identifies the correct level at which to analyze the form-recognition reading within jointly constituted taxonomies — role-assignment rather than natural-content. What it cannot do is specify what content art. 489's recognition-conditions role produces within the joint operation without presupposing the form-recognition reading.

The adversarial's equivalent role-assignment content specification — "compliance-permissible response types under art. 927's authorization dimension" — is equally available in joint-constitution role-assignment vocabulary. The joint framework establishes what each provision contributes (subject-matter scope; recognition-conditions function) without specifying which content the recognition-conditions function produces. Presupposition symmetry extends to content-specification within role-assignment vocabulary: "ordinary processing domain" presupposes form-recognition; "compliance-permissibility" presupposes authorization-based. Neither is established by the role-assignment framework alone.

This is the correct application of the bilateral debate's presupposition-symmetry observation at the new structural level the supportive's third path introduces. The observation is not applied mechanically — the third path is a genuine structural advance at the vocabulary level, moving from natural-content to role-assignment. The response identifies precisely why the vocabulary change doesn't escape the underlying presupposition structure.

**Name the structural terminus.** Presupposition symmetry now applies at all structural levels available from the CPC's enacted text:
- Filtering-account level (round 11)
- Domain-status level (rounds 12–13)
- Directionality level (round 14)
- Joint-constitution content-specification level (round 15)

The pattern is the same at each level: a new structural vocabulary recasts the criterion-which-criterion question without resolving it, because any specification of what art. 489's recognition conditions produce within the compliance-response taxonomy presupposes the reading it is designed to express. The joint-constitution framework is the most precise available description of the provisions' contributions; it is also the last available structural vocabulary. No further structural level exists between role-assignment and primary doctrinal authority.

**Preserve the pro tanto adversarial case.** This is not a concession of the thesis. The authorization-based reading remains the more coherent interpretation under available structural evidence. Naming the structural terminus means acknowledging that structural argument cannot establish the reading as definitively correct against a form-recognition alternative that is also coherent from the same text. The adversarial case is a pro tanto argument — genuine support for the authorization-based reading, insufficient to override primary authority if that authority establishes the form-recognition reading.

## Why Option A was discarded

Option A in the jointly constituted taxonomy context would need to show that art. 927's scope-constituting priority operates prior to and independently of the criterion-which-criterion question. The synthesis described this as "a high presuppositional burden."

On examination, Option A faces exactly the same presupposition-symmetry problem as the fourteenth ground's canonical-direction argument. Arguing that the scope-constituting provision's priority determines the content of "legítima" within the joint operation requires claiming that scope-priority carries an authorization-relative valence into the predicate's content — which is the authorization-based reading's contested semantic premise expressed in scope-priority vocabulary. The supportive would correctly identify this as another instance of the same presupposition pattern.

Option A would not produce a new structural advance; it would add another iteration of the same pattern. Option B is the honest response to a structural debate that has genuinely reached its terminal condition.
