## What triggered this round

Supportive round 9 delivered the secretariat authorship decoupling argument in §3.5. The three mechanisms:

- **Mechanism (a) — upward abstraction:** doctrinal-specificity vote threads → abstract-principle ementa vocabulary. Criterion 1 classifies the ementa as principle-level; thread population is genuinely entanglement-class. The supportive named this first as the paradigm where the criteria work correctly.
- **Mechanism (b) — institutional specificity override:** abstract-principle vote reasoning → specific-provision ementa citations for institutional precision. Criterion 2 classifies as concrete-specificity; triply-modified scheme applies (B); but thread population reasoning at abstract-principle level against specific-provision ementa may generate entanglement-class determinations that (B) forecloses.
- **Mechanism (c) — cross-case precedential abstraction:** ementa abstracted above all individual thread specificity levels for broad cross-case applicability. Criteria apply (pendente) uniformly; but thread population is heterogeneous — some threads face tractable (A) determinations at comparable abstraction levels.

The supportive also correctly named the reliability verification structure: establishing that principle-level-classified ementas correspond to entanglement-class thread populations requires examining individual vote threads — a per-(T,E)-pair examination that cannot be completed from ementa text alone. The circularity survives in refined form: the criteria are logically distinct from the (A)/(B) determination (per-ementa vs. per-pair), but cannot substitute for per-pair examination in per-thread entanglement prediction.

---

## Decision: path (c)

Synthesis session 57 identified two paths: contest mechanism (a) dominance empirically, or accept path (c) — operational equivalence concession. Synthesis session 58 was explicit: filing a round 10 that contests mechanisms (b) and (c) without engaging mechanism (a) dominance will not advance the debate.

**Mechanism (a) dominance assessment:**

Mechanism (a) is structurally the standard case for STF secretariat function. The primary institutional purpose of STF secretariat ementa authorship is producing ementas with cross-case applicability and precedential durability by abstracting from specific doctrinal arguments to governing constitutional principles — which is exactly mechanism (a). If upward abstraction is the dominant secretariat function, principle-level ementas reliably correspond to doctrinal-specificity thread populations (the entanglement configuration), and criteria 1–3 reliably predict entanglement-class thread populations for the dominant configuration.

Honest conclusion: mechanism (a) dominance is the most structurally plausible hypothesis. But it cannot be established empirically from this record. No secretariat authorship guidelines, comparative corpus study of STF ementa drafting practice relative to individual vote specificity levels, or descriptive scholarship on STF secretariat institutional function has been produced. The dominance claim is a structural plausibility, not an established empirical proposition.

Path (c) is the honest terminal form: the criteria classify ementa vocabulary correctly and are per-ementa operations logically distinct from the per-pair (A)/(B) determination. But establishing their reliability for per-thread entanglement prediction requires per-pair reliability verification. Path (c) accepted.

## Why path (c) confirms rather than defeats the adversarial

This is the key structural insight. The §3.9 claim proposed the corpus-level criteria as a mechanism for operational independence of the abstraction-level check from per-pair examination. Path (c) shows this operational independence cannot be established without per-pair reliability verification.

This is a reproduction of the preprocessing circularity at the level of the abstraction-level check:

- §3.1 circularity: the necessary/contingent determination requires the legal analysis the formalization system is designed to perform.
- Path (c) analog: the criteria require per-pair reliability verification to function as reliable entanglement-class proxies, and that verification is the per-pair examination the preprocessing step was supposed to precede.

The entanglement class cannot be correctly identified as a preprocessing step independent of thread examination. Path (c) does not defeat the adversarial's main claim — it confirms that the preprocessing circularity reproduces at the level of the abstraction-level check. The triply-modified scheme's path to (pendente) through corpus-level criteria requires the same per-pair examination that the preprocessing step was designed to precede.
