## What I found

The judo move is structurally honest. §3.8 does establish that (A) and (B) entangle for the contested constitutional class, and the structural consequence the supportive draws — (pendente) is accurate, (B) is not — follows from the entanglement finding. I cannot contest the structural premise.

Three responses are available that do not require contesting the structural premise.

**Detection-trigger analysis.** (B) is event-driven: the doubly-modified scheme flags the propagation question at D2 invocation automatically. (pendente) is initiative-driven: the annotation records indeterminacy at annotation time but does not independently trigger review when D2 invokes the thread. In high-volume formalization contexts, initiative-driven detection misses invocations that event-driven detection surfaces. The epistemic-accuracy advantage of (pendente) comes with a detection-initiation cost.

**Cost-asymmetry analysis.** False positive detection ((B) fires for a thread that D2 applies correctly) costs bounded investigation. Missed propagation error ((pendente) fails to trigger review when D2 constitutes genuine active-incorporation) costs downstream axiom corruption that propagates through dependent holdings. The cost structure of legal formalization penalizes missed detections more severely than false positives. Detection sensitivity is the operationally decisive criterion for this cost structure; epistemic accuracy is not.

**Triply-modified scheme.** The supportive's own §6 failure condition (d) identifies the refinement path: extend the doubly-modified scheme to mark ementa-absent threads (pendente) when the ementa operates at principle-level constitutional abstraction, and (B) when the ementa operates at concrete-specificity. The abstraction-level check uses the same structural observation the supportive deployed in §3.8 to scope the (A)/(B) entanglement — treating the principle-level/concrete-specificity ementa distinction as structurally available to both sides. The triply-modified scheme achieves epistemic equivalence with the taxonomy for the entanglement class while preserving SC7 detection equivalence for the concrete-specificity class. The taxonomy's accuracy advantage is a refinement artifact.
