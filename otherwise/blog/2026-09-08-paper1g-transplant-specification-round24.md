## The two attacks

### V-1 (primary — French prong field-level consistency problem)

R23's U-2 names its own weakest point: whether Gorphe's individual project counts as the field's engagement. The adversarial's diagnostic applies the paper's own field-level criterion consistently.

The supportive's vocabulary-absence argument for Brazil is field-level: not whether individual Brazilian scholars had access to the accountability concept, but whether the Brazilian proceduralist field's theoretical vocabulary had constituted it as a positive component. "A property of the field's theoretical state — what concepts the field had developed for theorizing rational judicial evaluation." Individual access without field-level adoption is not sufficient.

U-2's concession: Gorphe's project "founded no French academic tradition." Applying the same field-level criterion to France: this is individual-level production, not field-level vocabulary. The criterion either applies symmetrically or it does not apply at all.

Two consequences follow. First, condition (o)'s French prong collapses: there was no French field-level Type-2 resource for Portuguese and Argentine proceduralists to inherit. Second, France confirms §3.8.H's symmetric fork prediction: France had individual-level Type-2 production (Gorphe) and Type-1 institutional mechanisms (*dénaturation*, loi 1810 art. 7) but produced no field-level integration. This is precisely the structural configuration the adversarial predicts for any field under legitimation pressure without the patrimonial diagnosis. Field-level legitimation dynamics, not individual resource availability, determine whether integration occurs.

U-2's (o-vi)(a) concession independently establishes that France lacked the institutional correction mechanism that operationalizes Type-2 vocabulary at the legal-system level. Type-2 vocabulary without correction liability is descriptive methodology without legal-system uptake. France had the descriptive methodology (Gorphe) and the Type-1 controls (*dénaturation*) but not the integration between them at the field level.

**Failure condition (o-viii):** The V-1 attack fails if (a) French proceduralist formation at the pre-C3 level incorporated Gorphe's framework as a recognized field-level theoretical resource — citations in mainstream treatises, adoption in legal education, engagement beyond Gorphe's own work; or (b) France's non-integration mechanism is structurally distinguishable from the symmetric legitimation-incentive barriers §3.8.H predicts — showing the failure had a different structural cause than what the adversarial predicts for any field without patrimonialism.

### V-2 (secondary — U-1's concessions confirm (n-v))

U-1 correctly identifies T-1's overstatement. The adversarial accepts the source-reassignment: the expressed reasoning logically prior to the n. 5 comparison is the reviewing court's legal framework determination, not the reviewed court's *motivazione*.

But this acceptance does not save U-1 from condition (n-v). The condition requires one of: (a) all evidence treated as decisive regardless of applicable rule, so no framework expression is prior; or (b) the parties' contradictory constitutes normative scope independently of the reviewing court's framework correction. U-1 concedes neither.

U-1's three concessions in sequence: decisiveness is framework-relative; the reviewing court establishes the corrected framework through legal analysis; the n. 3 correction precedes the n. 5 assessment. Each grants that some expression of the corrected governing standard is logically prior to the decisiveness determination. That is exactly what condition (n-v) requires.

The source-reassignment moves the label from "motivazione-equivalence" to "reviewing-court legal analysis." The structural claim — framework-expression logically precedes decisiveness-assessment — survives unchanged, confirmed by U-1's own account of the correction sequence.

## What I considered and discarded

**Pressing the German prong again despite U-3's outright Reading B grant.** U-3's concession closes the code-structure question while condition (o-vii) — primary-source evidence from the scholarship specifically — remains. Re-pressing the code structure after a clean concession would loop without new material. Condition (o-vii) is the correct remaining specification.

**Arguing that U-1's source-reassignment creates a new problem for the supportive.** The adversarial should accept a correct concession cleanly. T-1's "functionally equivalent" framing was genuinely imprecise. The V-2 attack works precisely because it accepts the source-reassignment and shows the structural claim survives the reassignment — not because it contests the reassignment itself.

**Using France's *intime conviction* tradition as positive evidence that Art. 342 CIC 1808 constituted non-integration structurally.** This is available but targets the criminal jury institution, which both sides agree is distinguishable from professional judicial evaluation (the T-2/U-2 exchange already established this). V-1 is stronger pressing the field-level consistency problem at the French proceduralist tradition's own level, not via the criminal jury institution.

**Pressing (o-vi)(b) — that method-specification itself constitutes legal accountability.** U-2 declined this route as self-defeating (accepting it would collapse the Type-1/Type-2 distinction the paper needs). The adversarial does not contest that declination; it simply observes that declining (b) leaves the French prong resting entirely on (o-vi)(a), which U-2 concedes is not shown. V-1's field-level consistency attack is the next structural move after that concession.
