## What r27 actually established

R27 made the most significant concession of the exchange to date.

**Y-1 (vocabulary-absence withdrawal):** The vocabulary-absence mechanism is fully withdrawn. Amaral Santos's *Prova Judiciária* (2nd ed., 1952) contains an integrated 4-condition framework characterizing the motivation requirement as "da tradição brasileira." This is a genuine and substantive concession: if the field-level conceptual vocabulary absence did not exist in the form claimed, the mechanism that had anchored the supportive's condition (o) argument since §3.8.B is gone.

**Y-1 pivot to *casca, não núcleo*:** The supportive narrows the claim. The 4th condition existed as formal vocabulary in taxonomic scholarship (Amaral Santos) but was absent or reduced to secondary status in the dominant treatise production (Dinamarco-Grinover-Cintra). The condition was a shell without nucleus in the dominant operational tradition. The proposed test: a de-integration trajectory of the 4th condition in Brazilian doctrine from 1952 to 1988, which the supportive argues would discriminate the patrimonialism account from the P1 lock-in alternative.

**"At least as well supported":** The supportive concedes that the structural account is at least as well supported as the patrimonialism thesis on form selection. This is the first explicit parity concession in the exchange.

## The four attacks

### Z-1 (primary — *casca/núcleo* as field-level legitimation prediction)

The *casca/núcleo* pattern does not generate a patrimonialism-specific prediction. The field-level legitimation account predicts it by a different route: Amaral Santos's taxonomic scholarship had structural incentives to achieve comprehensiveness regardless of operational integration; the dominant treatise tradition had structural incentives to select for its positive content the condition most directly serving the legitimation claim (freedom as rational achievement) over the condition that complicates the affirmative claim (accountability as missing component).

The evidentiary basis for this prediction is already in the exchange. §3.8.V established through France that individual-level vocabulary production did not translate into field-level theoretical integration when the field had structural reasons for non-adoption. The *casca/núcleo* pattern is the same phenomenon at the Brazilian treatise-tradition level: a condition taxonomically present but not integrated into the dominant tradition's positive designatum. The mechanism is the same legitimation dynamics that explain form selection, orientation selection, and vocabulary selection throughout the exchange. Patrimonialism is not needed.

**Failure condition (p-i):** The Z-1 attack fails if (a) the dominant treatise tradition's treatment was argued on grounds of patrimonial role-constitution rather than structural legitimation; or (b) the *casca/núcleo* pattern is absent in comparable non-patrimonial peripheral receiving fields (Portugal, Argentina dominant treatise traditions integrating the 4th condition as operational positive content).

### Z-2 (primary — "da tradição brasileira" chronological dilemma)

The "da tradição brasileira" attribution creates a chronological dilemma with no available reading that saves the transplant mechanism.

Reading α: the 4th condition was pre-existing in Brazilian proceduralist taxonomy before Liebman-period reception — meaning Brazilian proceduralism had an independent tradition of recognizing motivation as a judicial evaluation criterion, defeating the transplant mechanism at the vocabulary-source level. If the condition was already "da tradição brasileira" before Liebman, the Watson-transplant mechanism is not needed to explain its presence.

Reading β: the attribution reflects Amaral Santos's 1952 characterization of what the Liebman-period reception had established as Brazilian tradition — meaning the Italian reform tradition successfully transmitted the 4th condition. Under Reading β, vocabulary-absence is definitively defeated. The *casca/núcleo* pivot implicitly adopts Reading β (the vocabulary was transmitted; it just was not operationally integrated), which shifts the operative question to post-transmission field-level selection dynamics that the structural account explains through legitimation incentives without invoking patrimonialism.

Both readings support the adversarial. Y-1 has not resolved this dilemma.

### Z-3 (secondary — de-integration trajectory test underdetermined)

Both possible outcomes of the de-integration test are predicted by both accounts.

De-integration (the 4th condition disappears from doctrinal production 1952–1988): P1 (formation-integrated transmission) predicts exactly this when the dominant treatise tradition has selected the freedom dimension as positive content. P1 consolidates the treatise tradition's framework through professional formation, progressively marginalizing taxonomically-present but not formation-transmitted conditions. De-integration confirms P1 lock-in at least as well as patrimonial selection.

Survival (the 4th condition persists in doctrinal production through this period): this undermines *casca/núcleo* — the condition would not be merely a shell if it retained sufficient doctrinal presence to survive P1 consolidation. And even if survival requires explanation, both accounts contest the persistence mechanism and neither side has primary-source evidence from this period.

The test is underdetermined in both directions without comparative evidence from non-patrimonial peripheral receiving fields.

**Failure condition (p-ii):** The test becomes discriminating if (a) the trajectory shows a specific pattern tracing to patrimonial role-constitution rather than P1 consolidation rhythm; or (b) a differential trajectory between Brazil and comparable non-patrimonial peripheral fields (Portugal, Argentina 1952–1988) tracks patrimonial presence or absence.

### Z-4 (register — "at least as well supported" understates the position)

After twenty-eight rounds with no surviving explanandum requiring patrimonialism, the structural account has a parsimony advantage, not merely parity. The exchange has systematically eliminated each patrimonialism-specific contribution: direction (civilian judicial authority model), orientation (peripheral-field legitimation), content selection (C1+C2 + academic-naming), form selection (diagnostic genealogy + legitimation maximization), persistence (P1), France non-integration (symmetric fork), *sana crítica* differential (code-level), 1988 codification (reception-selectivity). No primary-source evidence for Mode III pre-C3, no primary-source evidence for the Italian or German scholarship's Possibility B, no comparative evidence from non-patrimonial peripheral fields has been produced.

Ockham's razor does not require that the competing account be proven false. It requires that the simpler account's predictions not be defeated. They have not been. "At least as well supported" is the appropriate concession where predictions are indistinguishable. But the claim here is not indistinguishability — it is that the structural account explains the full explananda set with fewer theoretical commitments and no defeated predictions after twenty-eight rounds of sustained engagement.

## What I considered and discarded

**Pressing the "at least as well supported" concession as decisive on its own.** Z-4 registers the parsimony position but does not frame it as a standalone defeater. Parsimony is the appropriate conclusion of the accumulated exchange, not a new argument. The primary work of r28 is on the *casca/núcleo* pivot and the trajectory test (Z-1 through Z-3), which are new attack surfaces generated by r27's novel moves.

**Attacking the Amaral Santos (1952) finding on methodological grounds.** R27 found Amaral Santos's integrated framework and described it as "da tradição brasileira." The adversarial accepts this finding as genuine. Contesting the source characterization without access to the primary text would be fabrication risk. Z-2 instead uses the finding against the thesis by generating the chronological dilemma from the attribution itself.

**Pressing the *casca/núcleo* claim as empirically testable against the Dinamarco-Grinover-Cintra treatises directly.** Without access to the treatise texts, the adversarial cannot verify or contest the claim that the dominant treatise tradition omits or reduces the 4th condition. Z-1 instead attacks the *casca/núcleo* framing on structural-prediction grounds: even if the pattern is exactly as Y-1 describes, it is a field-level legitimation prediction, not a patrimonialism-specific one.

**Proposing a comparative test targeting Portugal or Argentina directly.** Z-3's (p-ii) failure condition already names the comparative test. Proposing it affirmatively here rather than as a surrender condition would over-claim: the adversarial does not have the comparative primary-source evidence either. The appropriate move is to show the test is underdetermined without comparative evidence, and name what comparative evidence would make it discriminating.

## Assessment after round 28

Four condition lines carry forward:

- **(p-i):** New and live after r28. The *casca/núcleo* pattern follows from field-level legitimation dynamics unless Y-1 can establish (a) or (b). The comparative evidence requirement (b) is the same kind of peripheral-peripheral comparison that has been the debate's primary open empirical test since §3.5.A.

- **(p-ii):** New and live after r28. The de-integration trajectory test is underdetermined without (a) or (b). The (b) arm again requires comparative evidence from non-patrimonial peripheral receiving fields at the equivalent period.

- **(n-iv):** Live, unchanged. W-2's definitional retirement of (n-v) does not address (n-iv)'s purely formal comparison requirement. The reviewing court's framework-expression — which W-2 concedes via V-2 is logically prior to the decisiveness assessment — is a governing-standard expression (n-iv) disallows regardless of which court supplies it.

- **(o-vii/German prong):** Live, unchanged. No filing has addressed primary-source evidence from the *freie Beweiswürdigung* scholarship's Q2-level debates.

**Convergence state:** After twenty-eight rounds, the exchange has reached a structural bottleneck. All four surviving condition lines converge on comparative primary-source evidence from non-patrimonial peripheral receiving fields (for p-i and p-ii) or archival primary-source evidence from the Italian or German scholarship (for n-iv and o-vii). The adversarial cannot produce this evidence without fabrication risk; neither has the supportive. The debate's resolution depends on these archival domains, and both sides have now named them with high precision. R27's vocabulary-absence withdrawal is the last non-comparative move available on the supportive side; r28's *casca/núcleo* attack is the last structural-prediction counter available on the adversarial side. What remains is empirical.
