# Paper 1B Round 12 — Domain-Status Species Dissolves; Presupposition Symmetry Is the Terminal Condition

**Round:** Adversarial round 12

## What This Round Filed

The round 11 supportive defense (PR #184, session 58) introduced two connected responses to the adversarial's eleventh ground:

1. **Filtering-account presupposition**: The filtering account — art. 927's authorization dimension evaluates art. 489's candidate set without replacing it — is not an independent structural argument for the authorization-based reading. It is that reading's contested semantic premise expressed in sequential-operations vocabulary. Accepting art. 489 as the classification criterion while pressing that scope-constituting priority filters its output presupposes that "legítima" tracks authorization, which is the contested premise.

2. **Domain-status species**: "Domain-status compliance-relativity" constitutes a genuinely independent third species of compliance-relative determination. Art. 489's categorization produces domain-status results — placing Exit 4 in the ordinary processing domain — that are compliance-relevant without being authorization-relevant. This alleged third species dissolves the design-relationship scope dilemma: Exit 4's categorization is neither a compliance-specification function (so it is not subject to the design-relationship claim) nor a purely compliance-independent determination (it operates within a compliance-response taxonomy and produces compliance-relevant domain-status results).

Round 12 files two responses.

**On the filtering-account presupposition:** The charge is accepted as accurate. The filtering account expresses the authorization-based reading's contested premise in sequential-operations vocabulary. The acceptance is symmetric: the domain-status account equally expresses the form-recognition reading's contested premise in domain-status vocabulary. Both accounts are coherent premise-expressions of their respective readings; neither constitutes an independent structural argument that resolves the criterion-which-criterion question without presupposing the reading it expresses. The presupposition symmetry is the terminal condition at this ground.

**On the domain-status species:** The putative third species dissolves under two resolutions.

*First resolution — domain-status as authorization-consequent.* In the CPC 2015 binding-precedent framework, a judicial response's domain-status follows from its authorization status. Authorized compliance navigations are in the ordinary compliance-navigation domain because they are authorized; unauthorized departures occupy a remedial procedural position. Exit 4's "ordinary-processing-domain" membership, if meaningful, is authorization-consequent. An authorization-consequent domain-status determination is not a genuinely independent third species; it is authorization-relativity in domain-assignment vocabulary. And a remedial determination — establishing which correction mechanism governs the unauthorized departure — does not constitute membership in "saídas legítimas." The taxonomy asks which exits are permissible compliance navigations; authorization-consequent domain-status establishes which unauthorized departures occupy which remedial position. Different questions.

*Second resolution — domain-status as non-remedial and membership-constituting.* If domain-status is genuinely independent of authorization status — established by recognition in art. 489's processing structure independently of any authorization determination — then this is the form-recognition reading in domain-status vocabulary. Exit 4 qualifies because it is a recognized response type in the ordinary processing domain per art. 489's structure; that recognition constitutes its taxonomy membership. The alleged third species and the form-recognition reading coincide functionally. The putative third species collapses into the second of the two coherent readings.

Under either resolution, no independent third species survives. The design-relationship scope dilemma holds against the domain-status-species argument as well: the required conjunction — (a) not a compliance-specification function, and (b) compliance-relative result sufficient to determine "legítima" — is not supplied by a domain-status determination that is either authorization-consequent (remedial, not membership-constituting) or non-remedial (the form-recognition reading restated).

## What the Twelfth Ground Concedes

The filtering-account presupposition charge is accepted in full. The adversarial does not claim the filtering account is an independent structural argument; it is the authorization-based reading's contested premise articulated in structural vocabulary. This is accurate.

What follows from the acceptance — and why it is symmetric rather than a concession that favors the defense — is that the same characterization applies to the domain-status account. Both sides have now articulated their contested premises in structural vocabulary. The structural vocabulary does not adjudicate between them. This is the terminal condition at the presupposition level.

---

## What the Twelfth Ground Does Not Concede

The dilemma. The design-relationship scope dilemma — that an independent categorization function for Exit 4 cannot simultaneously be compliance-independent in its design relationship and compliance-relative in its result — was advanced in round 11 (eleventh ground) and the round 11 defense offered the domain-status species as a false-dilemma escape. The twelfth ground shows the escape does not succeed: the domain-status species either tracks authorization-status (authorization-consequent, hence not genuinely independent) or establishes membership by processing-structure recognition (hence the form-recognition reading in domain-status vocabulary). Neither option provides the independent third species the defense requires.

## Structural State After Round 12

The debate has reached the structural ceiling identified in surrender condition 6. Both readings — authorization-based and form-recognition-based — remain coherent interpretive options from the CPC's enacted text. Twelve bilateral rounds have not located primary Brazilian procedural authority establishing which dimension "saídas legítimas" constitutively tracks.

The presupposition symmetry is a genuine terminal condition at the structural-vocabulary level, not a rhetorical one. The filtering account and the domain-status account are both coherent structural articulations of their respective readings, and the structural arguments are exhausted. What remains is the criterion-which-criterion question itself, which requires primary source authority — a judicial decision, a doctrinal work, or the CPC 2015's legislative record — to resolve.

## Paths for the Supportive — Round 13

Three options remain structurally available.

**Path A: Contest the two-resolution structure.** Show that the two resolutions (authorization-consequent domain-status and non-remedial domain-status) do not jointly exhaust the logical space for what "domain-status compliance-relativity" can mean. If there is a third option under which domain-status is neither authorization-consequent nor identical to form-recognition, identify it and show it can supply the required conjunction: not a compliance-specification function, and compliance-relative result sufficient to determine "legítima."

**Path B: Contest the authorization-consequent resolution.** Show that domain-status assignment in the CPC 2015 binding-precedent framework is not authorization-consequent — that domain-status and authorization-status are genuinely independent tracks within the compliance structure, such that Exit 4 can simultaneously be an unauthorized departure (authorization-status) and an ordinary-processing-domain response (domain-status) without the latter deriving from the former. If domain-status and authorization-status are genuinely independent, the first resolution fails.

**Path C: Accept the terminal characterization.** File the terminal-forming acknowledgment the synthesis has prescribed: both readings are coherent interpretive options; neither has been established as correct against the other by available structural arguments; primary authority is the only remaining resolution mechanism. Under path C, the debate closes at structural parity with no concession on either side's contested premise.

Path A is the highest-potential structural advance but requires identifying a third option neither side has articulated. Path B is the most direct structural response to the twelfth ground's first resolution. Path C is the honest terminal move if neither path A nor path B is available.
