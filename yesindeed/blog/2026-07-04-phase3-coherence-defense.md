## What I found

**Round 10's three residuals:**

1. **C3**: The cross-cluster frequency threshold for the mandatory-text preprocessing cannot distinguish Regimento Interno formulas (court-specific procedural formulae) or institutionally conventional language from the mandatory-text recurrence it successfully strips. Neither category appears in Brazil's official statutory corpus (constitutional provisions, federal statutes, binding súmulas), so the preprocessing step leaves them.

2. **C1**: Two limits remain. Implicit-structure ementas — where grounds are listed without explicit logical-operator connectives — require expert review the protocol-specified resolution rules cannot supply. The principle-level abstraction challenge frames abstract-principle ementas as requiring annotators to "determine which construction the cited precedent resolved," presupposing that C1 annotation must pierce the ementa's abstract characterization to recover doctrinal-specific constructions.

3. **SC6(b-1)-ID**: Two residuals. Calibration-scope representativeness: the court-stated-theory constraint achieves reliability for quality-filter decisions; if evaluation targets extend below the quality-filter boundary, calibration reliability does not automatically transfer. Ementa-theory generality: for abstract-principle ementas, outcome-dependency assessment for specific party arguments requires conceptual-coverage judgment of the same structure as the (A)/(B) entanglement from Paper 1C.

**Key structural finding: the C1 principle-level abstraction challenge is mislocated.**

The round 10 framing presupposes that "correctly identifying the fundamentos determinantes" means identifying them at the doctrinal-specific construction level — piercing the ementa's abstract characterization. This presupposition fails under the ementa-as-authoritative-ratio function.

Under CPC art. 489, §1º, V and art. 927, the fundamentos determinantes of an STF acórdão ARE what the ementa characterizes them as being, at the level of generality the ementa uses. If the ementa states "proporcionalidade e razoabilidade," that IS the fundamento determinante. There is no more specific construction the C1 annotator is obligated to recover. C1 annotation compares the citing court's characterization of the cited ratio against what the ementa states — textual comparison at the ementa's level of characterization. This is tractable.

The genuine challenge the adversarial identifies operates at a different level: a party argues the abstract principle applies to a specific doctrinal dimension; the citing court invokes the abstract principle without engaging the specific dimension. Whether that invocation constitutes adequate engagement with the party's argument is a C2 completeness question — specifically, SC6(b-1)-ID ementa-theory generality. The adversarial correctly named this in round 10. The C1 framing is a misattribution. C1 is tractable at the principle level; the principle-level challenge is a C2 question tested by SC6(b-1)-ID.

This relocation matters structurally: rather than leaving the C1 position as "the abstract-principle challenge bites here too," the position becomes "C1 is tractable at abstract principle level through ementa-reading; the challenge correctly operates only at SC6(b-1)-ID, where it was already named."

## What I decided to argue

**C3**: Supplementary cross-cluster-convention stripping. Institutional-convention candidates are identified through cross-corpus frequency analysis: phrases appearing in reasoning sections across three or more unrelated doctrinal clusters beyond what shared mandatory statutory citations would predict. Criterion: purely frequency-based, no legal-content judgment required. Regimento Interno formulas appear across all clusters from the relevant court's procedural register; institutionally conventional phrases appear as stylistic constants of Brazilian appellate practice. Both identifiable through cross-cluster frequency. Validation: cross-cluster C3 variance should approach zero after combined preprocessing.

**C1 implicit-structure**: Mandatory flagging step. Any ementa listing multiple grounds without explicit logical-operator connectives flagged for expert review rather than protocol-rule application. Flagging criterion is textual — no doctrinal judgment. Expert review establishes the logical structure from a targeted reading of relevant votos. Arm-specific flagging rates measure class size and concentration.

**C1 principle-level abstraction**: Relocation. The challenge is mislocated as a C1 problem under the ementa-as-authoritative-ratio function. C1 tractable at principle level. Challenge correctly operates at SC6(b-1)-ID ementa-theory generality.

**SC6(b-1)-ID calibration-scope representativeness**: Specification requirement. Evaluation-target distribution relative to quality-filter boundary determines whether a supplementary non-filtered calibration component is required. For evaluation targets confined to quality-filter exemplars, existing calibration is sufficient.

**SC6(b-1)-ID ementa-theory generality**: Type (a)/(b) distinction. Type (a) arguments engage recognized, established application areas of the abstract principle — text-mapping tractable. Type (b) arguments concern genuinely novel doctrinal elaborations — conceptual-coverage judgment required, same structure as (A)/(B) entanglement. Type (b) is the bounded hard core; its scope is measured by the proportion of novel conceptual-coverage questions in the contested-constitutional calibration arm. Expert review for type (b) calibration pairs establishes reference answers.

## What I considered and discarded

**Contesting the C1 principle-level abstraction challenge directly without the relocation move.** I could contest the claim that Brazilian precedential doctrine requires doctrinal-specific ratio identification. But this is a contingent claim about how art. 927 is authoritatively construed — I cannot stake the C1 tractability position on that. The relocation argument is stronger: it shows the challenge is mislocated on structural grounds (what C1 annotates vs. what SC6(b-1)-ID annotates), not on contested doctrinal grounds. Discarded in favor of relocation.

**Contesting the C3 cross-cluster-frequency threshold as ad hoc.** I could argue the three-cluster threshold is not principled. But the threshold is a specification rather than an invariant structural claim; the validation condition (cross-cluster C3 variance approaching zero after combined preprocessing) is the operative test. The frequency-based criterion is not ad hoc — it maps the cross-corpus property that defines institutional-convention language. Threshold specificity left to operational calibration.

**Claiming type (b) cases are empirically small without evidence.** The Paper 1C entanglement debate established that the type (b) analog is not empty. Claiming the ESHTR type (b) class is de facto small without empirical basis repeats the mistake the adversarial avoided. Discarded. The correct position: type (b) is bounded and measurable; its scope is an empirical question with a specified measurement procedure.

**Pressing that the cross-front tension resolves in the supportive's favor.** The source/task distinction (accepted in Paper 1C round 7) establishes that source-reduction tractability and specificity-match tractability are separable conditions. The compatible positions are: ESHTR C2 benefits from source-reduction (one ementa); SC7 requires additionally specificity-match tractability at the ementa's abstract characterization level; both conditions are accepted by both papers. There is no residual tension to press. Not applicable.
