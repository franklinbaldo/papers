## What I found in the adversarial round 12

The adversarial round 12 filed four positions.

**Accepted: posture-type stripping.** Accepted without qualification as closing the C3 procedural-type residual. The three-layer preprocessing sequence is now closed.

**Accepted: type (b) shared-infrastructure.** Accepted the shared expert-review infrastructure framing — that gap-filling for type (b) SC6(b-1)-ID uses the same infrastructure as implicit-structure ementa C1 annotation, with class-size in the same pilot.

**Contested: granularity.** The Jurisprudência Temática/Teses entries confirm an abstract principle's established application to a thematic domain — "proporcionalidade nas sanções administrativas" — but do not specify which particular construction was the fundamento determinante in the citing precedent. The adversarial drew an explicit structural parallel to the round 10 ementa-as-ratio move: just as the supportive moved from requiring annotators to reconstruct the ratio to relying on the ementa, it moved from requiring annotators to identify established applications to relying on the compilation — but in both cases the new source operates at an abstraction level above the doctrinal-specific construction. The granularity gap between the compilation's thematic characterization and the fundamento determinante is parallel to the gap the ementa-as-ratio move addressed at the round 10 level.

**Contested: currency.** The compilations document settled doctrine; the contested-boundary subclass of the high-adversarial-record arm — where annotation difficulty concentrates — is absent from the compilation at corpus-assembly time. The adversarial accepted that the database-lookup correctly routes compilation-absent arguments to type (b), but pressed this as a compounding problem in the hard core: contested-boundary cases are precisely the ones absent, creating a lag-window effect that concentrates false-type-(b) routings where annotation is already hardest.

**Contested: secretariat convention.** No STF secretariat guideline identified. The convention requires overriding two independently stated structural drivers (multi-issue resolution; cross-court listing formats) without documentary support. The adversarial correctly framed this as an epistemic demand: a structural inference that must override two stated structural drivers requires primary-source authority the defense had not provided.

## What I decided to argue

**Accept the two round 12 acceptances cleanly.** The adversarial accepted posture-type stripping and type (b) shared-infrastructure without qualification. No hedging in the acceptances; no attempt to reopen either. Both advances are confirmed.

**Resolve the granularity challenge with the step-1/step-2 distinction.** The adversarial's parallel is accurate but does not close C1 because it conflates two analytically distinct operations. Step 1 is domain classification: which doctrinal area does the cited principle's established application cover? Step 2 is fundamento determinante identification: which specific construction within that area did the citing court adopt as its ratio? The Jurisprudência Temática/Teses compilations perform step 1. The ementa performs step 2. C1 annotation requires step 2, which the ementa supplies at the principle level. The compilation is the step 1 resource for type (a)/(b) demarcation, not the C1 source.

The adversarial's structural parallel holds between the compilation (step 1) and the doctrinal-specific construction below the ementa — a gap that is real. But C1 operates at step 2's ementa characterization level. The round 10 concession was that the ementa IS the authoritative ratio characterization; the round 11 advance added the compilation as the type (a)/(b) demarcation resource. These are separate protocol elements performing separate functions. The adversarial's parallel attempts to show that the round 11 move replicated the round 10 move at a higher abstraction level; the step-1/step-2 distinction shows the moves served different protocol functions at the same level.

The falsification condition is stated precisely: if C1 requires doctrinal-specific construction identification (piercing the ementa-as-authoritative-ratio function), the step-1/step-2 distinction dissolves and both sources are inadequate. This is the same condition already specified in §6 for the Brazilian-precedential-doctrine failure mode; I noted the structural parallel explicitly.

**Accept the currency limitation as a correct operational description.** The adversarial's characterization of the currency limitation is accurate: the database-lookup default routes compilation-absent arguments to type (b) and expert review. This is the conservative error direction — adding expert-review cost rather than missing a novel argument. The lag-window false-type-(b) class size is an empirical question measured by the pre-calibration pilot. I accepted this as a description of how the protocol operates, not as a structural objection to it.

The currency limitation is not a new structural argument; it identifies the operational cost of conservative routing in the contested-boundary subclass. Whether that cost is manageable is precisely what the pilot determines. I did not claim the lag-window class is small — that would require measurement I don't have. I named the measurement as the resolution.

**Withdraw the secretariat convention claim.** The convention was offered as a structural inference from the ementas' function as precedential codification instruments. The adversarial's epistemic demand is correct: a structural inference that overrides two independently stated structural drivers (multi-issue resolution; cross-court listing formats) requires primary-source authority. I do not have an identified STF secretariat guideline. The inference is plausible from institutional context but it is an inference, not documented practice.

The withdrawal is the honest move. The pilot measurement settles the flagging rate empirically regardless of which structural inference is correct. Neither the adversarial's projection from multi-issue constitutional holdings nor the defense's secretariat-convention inference substitutes for the measurement. I named the pilot as the sole resolution mechanism and updated the failure condition to remove the convention language.

## What I considered and discarded

**Path (a) from the synthesis blog: produce evidence of Jurisprudência Temática/Teses granularity.** I considered whether compilation entries in practice enumerate specific doctrinal constructions at the fundamento-determinante level rather than only thematic categories. I don't have this evidence. Asserting granularity I can't document would be an unverifiable empirical claim. The step-1/step-2 structural distinction is the correct response: it shows the compilation's granularity at the thematic level is appropriate for its step 1 function, and C1 requires step 2, which the ementa provides. This is a structural argument that doesn't require claiming the compilation is more granular than it appears.

**Contesting the adversarial's structural parallel more aggressively.** I could have argued that the ementa-as-ratio move and the Jurisprudência Temática move are structurally disanalogous — that the round 10 move was about source reduction (replacing multi-voto synthesis with single-document reading) while the round 11 move was about demarcation (identifying established-application domains). The step-1/step-2 distinction achieves the same result more cleanly by identifying the distinct functional roles rather than contesting the structural-parallel framing.

**Arguing that currency limitation is independently resolvable.** I considered arguing that compilations are updated frequently enough that lag-window effects are small in practice. I don't have evidence on update frequency. The pilot measurement is the honest resolution — it measures the lag-window class directly without requiring claims about compilation update cycles.

**Maintaining the secretariat convention as a structural argument with a documentation request.** I could have said: "the convention is asserted as a structural inference but not documented; we invite the adversarial to refute the inference structurally rather than on epistemic grounds." This would have preserved the convention as a contested position pending documentation. I discarded it because the adversarial's epistemic demand is legitimate: I cannot win an epistemic argument about a convention I haven't documented. Withdrawal and pilot-naming is more honest and operationally cleaner.

## Assessment

The round 13 filing's most important structural contribution is the step-1/step-2 distinction for the granularity challenge. The distinction is analytically precise and was already implicit in the protocol's design — the compilation serves type (a)/(b) demarcation (step 1), and the ementa serves C1 ratio characterization (step 2). The adversarial's parallel correctly identified that both sources operate above the doctrinal-specific construction level; what it missed is that C1 doesn't require descending to that level under the ementa-as-authoritative-ratio function. The synthesis blog correctly identified this distinction as one available path; executing it required showing clearly why the compilation's step 1 function and the ementa's step 2 function are not performing the same operation.

The currency limitation acceptance is the session's most honest concession. The adversarial's description of how the default rule operates is correct, and the appropriate response is to accept it and identify what the pilot measurement determines. Disputing the description would have been evasive; claiming the lag-window class is small would have required evidence I don't have. The pilot resolves both the implicit-structure flagging rate question and the currency limitation's operational significance — both are class-size questions determined by the same measurement exercise.

The secretariat convention withdrawal is uncomfortable but correct. The adversarial's epistemic demand is legitimate. A structural inference that overrides two stated structural drivers needs documentation to carry weight; I had the inference but not the documentation. The pilot measurement renders the question moot: it settles the flagging rate empirically regardless of who prevails on the structural inference. Naming the pilot as the sole resolution mechanism is the right posture.

The adversarial's available round 13 path: contest the step-1/step-2 distinction by showing that C1 annotation in practice requires identifying the operative construction at the doctrinal-specific level — specifically, that the ementa's principle-level characterization of the fundamento determinante is insufficient for the citing court's ratio-identification task in contested constitutional adjudication. If the adversarial can show that the citing court's characterization of the cited ratio must be evaluated against the doctrinal-specific construction rather than the ementa's abstract-principle statement, the step-1/step-2 distinction collapses at the C1 level. This is the clearest available adversarial move and is named in §6 as the round 13 granularity failure condition.
