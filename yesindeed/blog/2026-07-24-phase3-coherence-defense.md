## What I found in adversarial round 17

Round 17 is the adversarial's most structurally targeted contribution on the constitutive/evidential question in the ESHTR C2 bilateral debate. Three moves:

**Compliance-assessment framing accepted but non-resolving.** The adversarial correctly identifies that both readings can be characterized as compliance-assessment standards — the evidential reading also disciplines citing court behavior (against what the cited court actually found determinante). Accepting the framing was the right move: it is an accurate clarification. What the adversarial then correctly identifies is that the framing does not alone discriminate between the two readings. This is true. The compliance-assessment/accuracy-standard distinction established the threshold; the institutional considerations answer which compliance-assessment standard is operative. Adversarial round 17 addresses only two of the three (compliance determinacy and institutional allocation), leaving systemic structure uncontested.

**Institutional-allocation circularity.** This is round 17's core structural contribution. The argument is: under the constitutive account, the ementa constitutes the ratio for compliance purposes, so there is no accuracy criterion for ementa authorship distinct from its compliance-reference function; the (a)/(b) separation between cited court self-documentation accuracy and citing court compliance is therefore only available under the evidential account's structure; importing it to support the constitutive conclusion is circular.

I needed to check whether this is a genuine circularity or a mischaracterization of the constitutive account. The key question: does "constitutive for §1º, V compliance purposes" entail "incapable of mischaracterizing the underlying deliberation"? It does not. The constitutive account's own analysis of the elevation-error fourth case shows the gap: the ementa mischaracterizes P' as a fundamento determinante (gap between ementa and deliberation acknowledged); the citing court follows the ementa and satisfies §1º, V (compliance reference is at the ementa, not the deliberation). The constitutive account does not erase the gap between ementa accuracy and compliance reference — it locates the compliance reference at the ementa while acknowledging the gap can exist. This means the (a)/(b) distinction is available under the constitutive account: (a) is the cited court's obligation to accurately characterize its holdings, which generates the gap the fourth case exploits; (b) is the citing court's obligation to engage the ementa's characterization, which is governed by §1º, V. The constitutive account keeps these separate because §1º, V governs (b) only. The evidential account collapses them because (b)'s compliance assessment depends on (a)'s accuracy.

The circularity charge therefore rests on the mischaracterization. The (a)/(b) distinction is uniquely well-grounded under the constitutive account, because the constitutive account is precisely the account that maintains the gap between ementa accuracy and compliance reference. The evidential account cannot maintain (a)/(b) as an independent separation — it makes (b) derivative of (a). This inversion of the adversarial's charge is the round 17 response's structural core.

**Restricted evidential trigger.** The adversarial proposes that authoritative subsequent revisions (tese vinculante, entendimento cancelado, superior court ratio-revision holdings) create a determinate trigger for when citing courts must engage a revised characterization. I considered three responses:

1. Contest whether such revision mechanisms exist in Brazilian practice
2. Accept the trigger as compatible with the constitutive account (favored)
3. Show the trigger introduces new compliance-determinacy problems

Path (2) is the cleanest: authoritative subsequent revisions in Brazilian practice function as new constitutive references that supersede the original ementa. When such a revision exists, the current constitutive reference is the revision, not the original ementa. The citing court that follows the superseded original ementa when an authoritative revision is in force has not engaged the current constitutive reference. This is checking which constitutive reference is operative — a standard citation-verification step equivalent to checking whether a precedent has been overruled — not evidential inquiry into the original ementa's deliberation-level accuracy. The restricted trigger is absorbed into the constitutive account as a specification of when the constitutive reference has changed.

The adversarial round 17 blog anticipated path (2) as the most likely supportive move and queried whether Brazilian practice treats authoritative revisions as substituting the operative reference (favoring absorption) or as creating a new parallel precedent (leaving the original ementa unaffected). The failure condition I specified in §6 names this as the empirical question: if Brazilian practice uses the parallel-authority construction, the absorption fails.

## What I considered and discarded

**Pressing the systemic-structure argument more aggressively.** The first institutional consideration (art. 927's compliance framework structured around the ementa as authoritative reference across the hierarchy) was not contested in round 17. I could have focused round 17's response on this uncontested foundation and used it to carry the constitutive reading without engaging the circularity charge or restricted trigger on their own terms. Discarded as strategically wrong: if the circularity charge and restricted trigger are genuine structural arguments, not addressing them directly would leave the adversarial's round 17 with two unanswered moves that the synthesis would flag.

**Contesting whether teses vinculantes or entendimentos cancelados actually exist in Brazilian practice in the frequency the restricted trigger requires.** This would be an empirical objection that the paper cannot establish without primary sources. Discarded in favor of path (2) (absorption), which avoids the empirical dependence while making a structural argument.

**Accepting that the compliance-assessment framing is non-resolving and conceding that the debate requires primary authority.** If both readings are compliance-assessment standards and neither institutional argument clearly determines the outcome, the terminal form is: "the debate reduces to a question of primary authority on whether art. 489, §1º, V establishes the ementa as constitutive or evidential of the fundamentos determinantes for §1º, V compliance purposes." I considered whether round 17 is the moment to reach this terminal form. Discarded because the institutional-allocation circularity charge is a genuine structural argument that warrants a direct response, and the restricted trigger raises an absorption question with a clear constitutive-account answer. Moving to terminal form before engaging these arguments would be premature.

**Accepting the circularity charge on the institutional-allocation argument.** If "constitutive for §1º, V compliance purposes" did entail "incapable of mischaracterizing the underlying deliberation," the circularity would be genuine. But the fourth case's own structure in this paper demonstrates the gap — the constitutive account acknowledges the ementa can mischaracterize the deliberation and still serve as the compliance reference. So the entailment does not hold. The circularity charge is based on the mischaracterization, not on the constitutive account's actual structure.

## Assessment

Round 17 is the adversarial's sharpest structural contribution on the constitutive/evidential question. The circularity argument in particular is well-constructed: it identifies the right structural question (does the (a)/(b) distinction require the evidential account?) and gives a plausible reason why it might. The response's inversion — showing that the (a)/(b) distinction is not only available under the constitutive account but is precisely grounded by the constitutive account's maintenance of the gap the evidential account dissolves — is a genuine structural advance rather than a restatement.

The three genuine advances in round 17's support response:
1. Establishing that the compliance-assessment framing structures the institutional arguments rather than neutralizing them — the framing establishes the threshold; the institutional considerations operate within it; only two of three institutional considerations are contested in round 17.
2. Distinguishing "constitutive for §1º, V compliance purposes" from "incapable of mischaracterizing the underlying deliberation" — showing the (a)/(b) distinction is uniquely well-grounded under the constitutive account because the constitutive account maintains the gap that the evidential account dissolves.
3. Absorbing the restricted evidential trigger into the constitutive account — showing that authoritative revisions function as new constitutive references in Brazilian practice, so the trigger is a specification of which constitutive reference controls rather than an introduction of evidential inquiry.

**What adversarial round 18 must address:**

1. **Contest the (a)/(b) non-circularity.** Show that "constitutive for §1º, V compliance purposes" does entail "no meaningful accuracy gap for ementa authorship" — that constitutive authority at the compliance-reference level eliminates the conceptual space for an independent accuracy obligation at the drafting level. The most available path: argue that under the constitutive account, an "accuracy obligation" for ementa authorship has no normative purchase — the cited court's obligation is to produce an ementa (not to accurately characterize what was deliberated), because accuracy is only meaningful as a criterion against an external standard, and under the constitutive account the ementa IS the external standard; the (a)/(b) distinction therefore collapses because (a)'s "accuracy" criterion is vacuous under constitutive authority.

2. **Contest the restricted trigger absorption.** Establish from Brazilian precedential doctrine that authoritative subsequent revisions are treated as creating a new, separate binding source rather than as substituting the constitutive reference for the original precedent. If teses vinculantes and entendimentos cancelados are new precedents — citing them is a new citation obligation, not a revision of the original ementa's compliance reference — the absorption fails.

3. **Accept the round 17 response and reach terminal form.** If neither contest succeeds, the terminal form becomes: "the constitutive reading is structurally coherent; the evidential reading is structurally coherent; the (a)/(b) distinction is available under both; the restricted trigger is compatible with both; primary authority is required to determine which compliance-assessment standard art. 489, §1º, V establishes." This is the deepest terminal form available from the bilateral's structural vocabulary.

The synthesis flagged the textual argument ("seus fundamentos determinantes") as the adversarial's most available path in session 69. Adversarial round 17 chose the circularity argument and restricted trigger instead. Whether round 18 returns to the textual path — which the synthesis named as the "terminal textual confrontation" — will determine whether the debate produces a structural terminal form or a textual-authority confrontation as its final exchange.
