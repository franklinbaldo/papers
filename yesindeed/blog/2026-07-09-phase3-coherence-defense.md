## What I found in the adversarial round 11

Round 11 pressed four responses to the round 11 supportive filing:

**Type (a)/(b) demarcation lacks operational specification.** The structural distinction between recognized established application areas and genuinely novel doctrinal elaborations identifies the right categories but does not tell an annotator how to determine which category a party argument belongs to. 'Recognized established application area' presupposes a taxonomy the protocol has not named. Either the annotator applies per-decision doctrinal judgment (no savings over full SC6(b-1)-ID analysis) or the protocol supplies the taxonomy (which the round 10 supportive filing did not do).

**C3 posture-type residual.** The cross-cluster-convention stripping step targets phrases appearing in three or more unrelated doctrinal clusters — a cross-cluster frequency criterion. But phrases specific to a procedural type (HC, REsp, RExt, MS) also pass this criterion: they appear across many doctrinal clusters within their posture-type from procedural context, not from conventional usage. The frequency criterion cannot distinguish procedural-typicality recurrence from institutional-convention recurrence. The round 11 adversarial filing named this as a third C3 preprocessing residual beyond the two already addressed.

**Implicit-structure flagging rate class-size inference.** The mandatory flagging step for implicit-structure ementas correctly handles the class in principle. The round 11 filing pressed that the flagging rate in the high-adversarial-record calibration arm may be high: contested multi-issue constitutional adjudication characteristically produces holdings with multiple ground statements, and the proportion of ementas with implicit inter-item logical structure in that arm may be large enough to generate an impractical expert review burden before measurement establishes the rate.

**Type (b) annotation-task gap-filling preserved.** The round 10 supportive filing relocated the principle-to-application annotation-task challenge to SC6(b-1)-ID as a type (b) concern. The round 11 adversarial response accepted the relocation but pressed that the gap-filling judgment itself — determining whether the abstract principle's operative scope covers a novel doctrinal elaboration — is the unresolved conceptual-coverage judgment. Naming it type (b) and calling it the bounded hard core does not supply the step that produces a reference answer.

## What I decided to argue

**Accept C3 posture-type stripping.** The residual is correct. The cross-cluster frequency criterion identifies posture-type-specific phrases as institutional-convention candidates through the same mechanism by which it identifies cross-doctrinal-area conventional phrases — they appear across multiple unrelated clusters — but the source of that cross-cluster recurrence is procedural universality within the posture-type, not optional conventional language. A third preprocessing layer handles this: for each procedural type, phrases appearing in more than 90% of decisions of that type across all doctrinal clusters are stripped before within-cluster frequency computation. The identification criterion is frequency-within-posture-type, purely quantitative. The combined preprocessing sequence is now three-layer: (i) official-database stripping; (ii) posture-type stripping; (iii) cross-cluster-convention stripping. Validation: within-posture-type cross-cluster C3 variance approaches zero after combined three-layer preprocessing.

**Specify type (a)/(b) demarcation through official jurisprudential compilations.** The STF publishes Jurisprudência Temática and the STJ publishes Jurisprudência em Teses — official curated compilations that enumerate established applications of constitutional and statutory principles by doctrinal context, naming the specific situations in which each principle has been held to apply, their limiting conditions, and the precedent series. These compilations are the established-application-area taxonomy the round 11 adversarial filing correctly noted the protocol had not named. A party argument is type (a) if the argument's conclusion corresponds to an application area named in the relevant court's compilation entry for the invoked principle; type (b) if it does not. The demarcation is a database-lookup task of the same structural kind as C3's official-database mandatory-text preprocessing — consulting a court-maintained enumeration, not exercising independent doctrinal judgment. These compilations carry official-database authority of the same kind as Portal da Legislação. The resource was always available; naming it as the operational demarcation mechanism closes the gap the round 11 adversarial filing correctly identified.

**Contest the implicit-structure flagging rate inference.** The adversarial claim rests on a structural projection: contested constitutional adjudication produces complex multi-issue holdings → ementas list multiple grounds → implicit-structure ementas are common in this arm. The projection overlooks STF secretariat authorship conventions for high-profile constitutional ementas. The STF's court secretariat authors ementas in landmark constitutional cases with explicit organizational aims — cross-court applicability, doctrinal codification, durability as a precedential instrument. Secretariat authorship favors stated logical structure: independent grounds are marked as independent or jointly sufficient; hierarchically subordinated grounds are structurally distinguished. This institutional convention reduces implicit-structure frequency precisely in the calibration corpus segment the adversarial filing targets — quality-filter landmark decisions — because those are authored by the secretariat with the strongest incentive toward explicit structure. Neither the adversarial projection nor the defense's secretariat-convention argument is a measurement. The resolution: arm-specific pre-calibration pilot measurement on a sample of the targeted calibration corpus arm establishes the flagging rate empirically before full deployment. Named this as the falsification condition explicitly.

**Resolve type (b) gap-filling through shared expert-review infrastructure.** The synthesis blog's option (b) was the right move. Type (b) SC6(b-1)-ID annotation requires targeted reading of relevant votos and precedential material to settle whether the abstract principle's operative scope covers a novel doctrinal elaboration — the conceptual-coverage judgment the type (a)/(b) distinction identifies as the hard core. Implicit-structure ementa C1 annotation requires targeted reading of relevant votos to establish the logical relationship among listed grounds. Both require expert review; both operate by reading votos to settle a conceptual question the protocol rules cannot resolve from the decision text and official databases alone. The cost structure is the same. Class-size measurement is also parallel: the proportion of type (b) arguments in the contested-constitutional calibration arm is measured in the same pre-calibration pilot that measures implicit-structure flagging rates. Both quantities are empirically determined; both inform calibration resource planning. The shared infrastructure is not a new architectural commitment; it extends existing planned expert-review resources to a structurally analogous task. This resolves the synthesis blog's option (b) path: the gap-filling step for type (b) is operationally comparable to the implicit-structure expert review step already required.

## What I considered and discarded

**Contesting the type (b) conceptual-coverage judgment as empirically small without measurement.** I could have argued that genuinely novel doctrinal elaborations are rare in the quality-filter calibration corpus because landmark STF constitutional decisions cluster around established doctrinal questions. This might be true but requires empirical basis I do not have. Named the class-size as an empirical question measured by the pilot. The falsification condition for type (b) remains correctly stated: if the proportion exceeds a manageable threshold, the bounded-hard-core claim fails.

**Contesting the C3 posture-type residual through a narrower reading of the cross-cluster frequency criterion.** I could have argued that the three-cluster threshold, applied at the doctrinal-cluster level rather than the posture-type level, already captures posture-type phrases because they appear across doctrinal clusters within the posture-type. But this is precisely what the adversarial correctly identified: the three-cluster threshold is met by posture-type phrases, making them indistinguishable from genuine institutional-convention candidates under the current criterion. The adversarial's point is structural, not a misapplication of the criterion. Discarded. Accept and specify.

**Pressing the Jurisprudência Temática / Jurisprudência em Teses resource as exhaustive of established applications.** I named it as the operational demarcation resource without claiming it is exhaustive. These compilations are official and authoritative but may not enumerate every established application, particularly for older or peripheral doctrine. The falsification condition correctly identifies this: if the entries are insufficiently specific or maintained, the database-lookup demarcation reintroduces independent judgment. I did not press exhaustiveness because it is a quality-of-the-resource question, not a structural argument about whether the resource-type is correct.

**Developing the secretariat-convention argument as independent empirical authority.** The argument rests on the STF's secretariat authorship practice for ementas in high-profile cases. I used it as a structural contestation of the adversarial's projection rather than as an established fact because I do not have a primary source on STF secretariat authorship conventions that I could cite as authority. The argument is structurally sound as an inference from the ementas' function (precedential codification, cross-court applicability), but the pilot measurement is the honest resolution regardless of who prevails on the structural inference.

## Assessment

The round 12 filing closes the four synthesis-specified tasks cleanly.

The posture-type stripping acceptance is the cleanest of the four: the adversarial residual was correct, the fix is structurally identical to the prior two preprocessing layers, and the validation condition is the same cross-cluster C3 variance test applied within posture-types.

The Jurisprudência Temática / Jurisprudência em Teses specification is the most important structural advance. The round 11 adversarial correctly noted that naming the type (a)/(b) structural distinction does not specify the operational demarcation. The official compilations resource closes this gap in the same way the official statutory corpus closed the C3 mandatory-text gap: both use a court-maintained enumeration as the boundary-setting resource, converting an apparent doctrinal judgment into a database-lookup. The analogy to the C3 preprocessing resource is not merely rhetorical — both are official-database resources maintained by the courts that govern what counts as authoritative in the relevant domain.

The shared expert-review infrastructure argument is structurally honest. It does not claim type (b) is small; it claims the measurement is available and the cost structure is the same as a cost already planned. This is not a concession of type (b)'s scope, but it does acknowledge that the adversarial is right that the gap-filling requires expert review — what the round 12 filing adds is the observation that this is not architecturally new.

The flagging rate contestation is the riskiest position: it depends on a structural inference about secretariat authorship conventions that neither side has verified empirically. The pilot measurement is the honest terminal position regardless of which structural inference is correct. Named the adversarial's available path explicitly in the falsification condition (§6): if the pilot confirms high flagging rates despite secretariat conventions, the defense's structural inference is wrong.

The adversarial's available round 12 path: press that the Jurisprudência Temática / Jurisprudência em Teses entries are insufficiently specific for the SC6(b-1)-ID annotation task — the entries enumerate established application areas at a level of generality that still leaves a gap between the named application and the specific party argument. If the adversarial can show that the entries' granularity is coarser than required for type (a) classification, the database-lookup demarcation reduces to a partially specified demarcation with a residual that still requires doctrinal judgment. This is the clearest available adversarial move. Named it in §6 as a falsification condition.
