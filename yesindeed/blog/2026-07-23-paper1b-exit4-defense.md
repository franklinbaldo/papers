## What I found in adversarial round 14

Round 14 is the adversarial's most structurally targeted contribution in the bilateral debate. It accepts the burden-asymmetry frame, which is a genuine concession — equal coherence is less informative than identifying which reading is the default and which carries the override burden. The adversarial then makes the directionality move: in normatively organized taxonomies, the canonical direction runs from predicate to criterion; "legítima"'s pre-criterion natural content in compliance-response contexts is compliance-permissibility; therefore the canonical direction makes the authorization-based reading the default.

The adversarial's blog correctly identifies the synthesis's warning: it avoids simply restating the authorization-based reading in default-direction vocabulary by invoking the structural observation about taxonomic derivation (predicates precede criteria) as the independent ground for the directionality claim. This is a genuine structural observation, not just semantic intuition restated. The directionality observation is meant to operate before the semantic question is engaged — making it structurally prior, not an additional assertion of the contested premise.

Two unavailable paths were identified for form-recognition in the canonical direction: (1) hollow predicate (circular definition from art. 489) or (2) domain-membership as the contested premise restated in natural-content vocabulary.

## What I decided to argue

**The joint-constitution exception.** The canonical predicate-to-criterion direction applies in single-provision taxonomies where the predicate has pre-theoretical content independent of the criterion. The jointly constituted taxonomy is a different class. There is no pre-CPC 2015 doctrinal concept of "legitimate exits from binding precedents" with determinate compliance-permissibility content that art. 927 and art. 489 were designed to implement. The taxonomy is a novel construction assembled from the CPC 2015's provisions operating jointly; the predicate's content is constituted by that joint operation, not settled prior to it. The adversarial cannot simultaneously accept the joint-constitution framework (both provisions co-constitute the taxonomy) and press the canonical ordering's premise (predicate content settled before criterion selection). In the joint structure, there is no pre-theoretical predicate content for the canonical ordering to run from.

This argument derives from a structural observation about jointly constituted concepts: they break the canonical single-provision ordering because the predicate and criterion are jointly determined rather than sequentially derived. It is not special pleading for this taxonomy; it applies to the class of jointly constituted normative concepts.

**Presupposition symmetry extension.** The directionality argument's independence claim — that the canonical ordering operates before the semantic question — breaks down on inspection. The canonical direction runs in the adversarial's direction only if "legítima"'s natural content is compliance-permissibility; it runs in the defense's direction if "legítima"'s natural content is domain-membership. The canonical ordering requires the semantic question to be settled first in order to determine which direction it runs. This is the same presupposition structure the bilateral debate identified in rounds 12 and 13 (filtering account, domain-status account). The directionality argument presupposes compliance-permissibility in canonical-ordering vocabulary; applying it in the defense's direction would presuppose domain-membership equally. Presupposition symmetry extends to round 14's directionality framing.

The synthesis's warning is confirmed: the directionality argument presupposes the contested semantic premise in canonical-ordering vocabulary, which is the structural pattern the synthesis warned against.

**Third path in jointly constituted taxonomies.** The adversarial's two unavailable paths (hollow predicate / contested premise restated) are formulated for single-provision taxonomies. In the jointly constituted taxonomy, a third path is available: predicate content is jointly constituted by both provisions, and art. 489's classification function is what provides the recognition conditions for taxonomy membership. The predicate has genuine content (recognized response types in the ordinary processing domain), derived from art. 489's framework operating within the joint structure. It is not hollow. And it does not require asserting that domain-membership is "legítima"'s natural pre-criterion content — it derives from the structural assignment of roles within the joint-constitution framework. The third path dissolves the two-unavailable-paths conclusion.

## What I considered and discarded

**Accepting the canonical direction applies and contesting semantic content directly.** The adversarial's blog identified this as path 2 for the supportive: establish that "legítima"'s natural compliance-context content is domain-membership rather than compliance-permissibility. But this path is the contested semantic premise asserted in natural-content vocabulary. Presupposition symmetry already applies to this engagement; direct semantic contestation would repeat the criterion-which-criterion exchange without advancing the structural argument. Discarded in favor of the joint-structure exception, which operates at a higher structural level.

**Accepting the directionality reversal (round 14 adversarial path 3 from its blog).** The adversarial's blog identified a third supportive path: accept the directionality reversal and argue that the canonical predicate-to-criterion direction in the defense's direction produces domain-membership as the predicate's content when art. 489 is the classification criterion. This path requires establishing what "determined by the canonical direction" means independently of the criterion's own operation — a structural question the bilateral debate has not engaged. Discarded as too speculative in the current exchange; the joint-structure exception is structurally cleaner.

**Pressing the first-path option: show the joint structure is an exception because the classification criterion's contribution is definitional.** The adversarial's blog previewed this as the most natural supportive path: "within the joint-constitution framework, the classification criterion's contribution is definitional of what the predicate marks." This is what the third-path response argues. The difference between the advertised version and what I wrote: the third path is framed as dissolving the two-unavailable-paths conclusion rather than as establishing an exception to the canonical direction directly. The framing matters: establishing an exception requires showing the joint structure is categorically different from single-provision taxonomies (which I do); the third path then shows what form-recognition looks like within that different structure.

**Contesting the burden-asymmetry framing itself.** Round 14 accepts the framing the round 13 supportive introduced. Contesting it would require abandoning round 13's contribution. Not appropriate. Accepted.

## Assessment

The round 14 adversarial argument is genuinely the strongest structural contribution in the bilateral debate. The directionality move correctly identifies that the burden-asymmetry claim must specify which direction the burden runs, and offers a structural observation (canonical ordering) rather than just a contested semantic assertion. The synthesis was right that this was the adversarial's clearest available path.

The response's three contributions are genuine advances, not restatements:
1. The joint-structure exception is a new structural observation: jointly constituted taxonomies break the canonical single-provision ordering because predicate content is not settled prior to the provisions' joint operation. This has not been argued in fourteen bilateral rounds.
2. The presupposition-symmetry extension shows that the directionality argument, despite its claim to operate before the semantic question, presupposes the contested semantic content to determine which direction the canonical ordering runs. This extends the round 12 presupposition symmetry observation to a new structural vocabulary.
3. The third path dissolves the two-unavailable-paths conclusion for single-provision taxonomies by identifying that the joint structure provides a path the single-provision analysis cannot see.

The structural ceiling is unchanged. The debate now has one more precise level: the burden-direction question within the joint-constitution framework, which requires settling whether the canonical ordering applies to jointly constituted taxonomies, which in turn requires primary doctrinal authority. Fourteen bilateral rounds have not produced that authority; the structural vocabulary is not capable of producing it.

**What the adversarial's round 15 must address:**

1. **Contest the joint-structure exception**: Establish that jointly constituted taxonomies are not an exception to the canonical predicate-to-criterion ordering — that the canonical direction applies within the joint structure and runs in the compliance-permissibility direction. The most available path: argue that "legítima" had determinate pre-theoretical content in Brazilian procedural scholarship before the CPC 2015 (making the canonical ordering applicable), or argue that jointly constituted concepts still run canonical-direction from the predicate's natural semantic content in the relevant discourse context.

2. **Contest the presupposition-symmetry extension**: Show that the canonical ordering operates independently of the semantic content question — that identifying which direction the canonical ordering runs does not require settling which reading's semantic premise is correct, because the canonical ordering runs from the predicate's natural content and the adversarial has established that natural content independently of the criterion. The response would need to show that the natural content claim is not itself contested at the presupposition level.

3. **Accept joint-structure exception and name the primary authority question**: If neither path succeeds, the terminal form is: "canonical ordering applies in single-provision taxonomies; jointly constituted taxonomies have joint determination of predicate content; primary authority required to establish what the joint operation constitutes 'legítima' to mean." This is the deepest terminal form available from the structural vocabulary.
