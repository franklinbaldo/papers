**The round 17 shape — two responses.**

### Against O-1: Logical-Structure Testing as the Independent Operationalization Mechanism

O-1's attack: criterion/mechanism distinction holds conceptually, but the good-faith analogy fails at the legal-system actionability level because epistemic sensitivity lacks independent behavioral proxies. Good faith has transaction-level behavioral indicators (commercial custom, course of dealing, reliance patterns) that allow appellate review to assess criterion satisfaction without relying on the party's self-report. Epistemic sensitivity has no analogous proxy in three target domains (mixed evidence, weight-assignment, narrative reconstruction). Separate violability of Art. 116 and Art. 132 is conceded but does not establish independent operationalizability — because the only appellate evidence for Art. 116 criterion satisfaction in these domains may be Art. 132's exposition.

The response makes three moves. First, the disanalogy is real but misdescribed. Good faith uses transaction-level behavioral proxies. Epistemic sensitivity uses **logical-structure testing against the trial record** — whether a judicial conclusion is consistent with the evidence presented, whether the inferential moves track the logical structure of the evidentiary record, whether the judge demonstrably ignores categories of evidence. Italian appellate doctrine recognized exactly this: *errore di giudizio* (error of judgment on the merits) and *omessa valutazione di prove* (omitted evaluation of evidence) are doctrinal categories that test criterion satisfaction against the trial record without treating the *motivazione* document as the primary information source. The trial record constitutes the independent reference point. A judge who produces a conclusion inconsistent with the record can be corrected on Art. 116 grounds even on a formal compliance reading of the *motivazione* obligation; conversely, formally adequate *motivazione* does not immunize Art. 116 failure visible in the logical structure of the conclusion. These are the independent failure modes the two-institution structure was designed to capture.

Second, O-1 misspecifies the criterion for independent operationalizability. The criterion is not that the mechanism be of the behavioral-proxy type — the criterion is that some mechanism for assessing criterion satisfaction independent of the *motivazione* disclosure document exists as the primary information source. Logical-structure testing satisfies this: it uses the trial record as the primary source, with the *motivazione* document as a secondary consistency check, not as the foundational source of information about whether epistemic sensitivity was exercised.

Third, O-1's separate violability argument restates its conclusion rather than establishing it. O-1 claims the trial record does not constitute an independent reference point for Art. 116 criterion-satisfaction assessment in the three target domains. This is exactly what the logical-structure testing argument contests. O-1 must show that in mixed-evidence cases, weight-assignment decisions, and narrative reconstruction, integrative inconsistency with the evidentiary record is not assessable from the record itself. The adversarial has not made that showing.

Revised failure condition (n-ii): the response fails if the adversarial establishes specifically for the three target domains that logical-structure testing against the trial record cannot identify criterion-satisfaction failures independently of the *motivazione* document — either because there is no consistent logical relationship between the evidentiary record and rationally possible conclusions in these domains, or because Italian appellate doctrine under Art. 116 specifically required the *motivazione* document as the primary information source for Art. 116 correction. Primary-source evidence about how Italian courts operated *errore di giudizio* review in these specific evidentiary categories would be determinate.

### Against O-2: Pre-C3 Domestic Proceduralist Scholarly Traditions as the Specific Vocabulary Resource

O-2's attack: accepts the Type-1/Type-2 distinction and the general-level asymmetry (non-patrimonial rational-legal fields do supply Type-2 vocabulary through general institutional formation; patrimonial fields supply only Type-1). But general Type-2 vocabulary does not supply the specific proceduralist-domain constituting vocabulary condition (a) requires. The scholarly framework for treating the evidentiary evaluation concept as a theoretical object with internally specifiable positive content, and for positioning the accountability dimension as a question about that concept's internal structure rather than a separate institutional obligation, develops through field-specific C3 proceduralist engagement — not from adjacent-domain Type-2 formation.

The response identifies a specific vocabulary resource that O-2's argument misses: **domestic proceduralist scholarly traditions in non-patrimonial civilian jurisdictions** that had constituted free-evaluation evidentiary concepts as theoretical objects with internally specifiable rational structure prior to the Italian Liebman tradition's period of Iberian and Latin American transmission.

**French *intime conviction* doctrine.** Article 342 of the 1808 Code d'instruction criminelle established free jury evaluation in criminal procedure. The subsequent French proceduralist scholarly tradition — extending through the nineteenth century and culminating in Gorphe's *De l'Appréciation des Preuves en Justice* (1924) — constituted the *intime conviction* concept as a theoretical object with internally specifiable rational structure: what makes the judge's or jury's conviction *intime* (authentic rather than arbitrary), what evidentiary features constrain it, and what distinguishes conviction that meets the rational standard from mere personal sentiment. The accountability dimension — what makes conviction articulable and assessable rather than merely personal — was internal to this debate from early in the French tradition. This is precisely the proceduralist-domain constituting vocabulary O-2 identifies as the required resource: the scholarship engaged the free-evaluation concept's positive designatum as a theoretical object within the proceduralist tradition, not merely from outside it.

**German *freie Beweiswürdigung*.** Section 286 ZPO (1877) established free judicial evaluation in civil procedure. The post-1877 German proceduralist scholarship engaged *freie Beweiswürdigung* as a theoretical concept with specifiable content — distinguishing it from arbitrary evaluation, specifying what evidentiary engagement it required — within the proceduralist tradition. The accountability dimension (the grounds on which the judge's evaluation could be assessed on *Überzeugungsbildung* grounds) was internal to this engagement, not imported from adjacent administrative law.

**Portugal and Argentina** had access to these traditions through their doctrinal formation. Portuguese proceduralist scholarship engaged French and German doctrine directly; Argentine proceduralist formation included the same comparative-law channels. The pre-C3 proceduralist vocabulary resource O-2 demands was available to these fields' scholars as domain-specific conceptual formation about free evidentiary evaluation concepts as theoretical objects — not merely as general Type-2 orientation from institutional formation.

**Brazil had no equivalent domestic tradition.** The 1939 Brazilian CPC incorporated free judicial evaluation (Art. 118 CPC 1939) as a regime change — replacing the *prova legal* system — but the practice was not theorized within a pre-existing domestic proceduralist scholarly tradition engaging the concept's positive designatum. The Liebman USP engagement from 1940 was Brazil's primary encounter with proceduralist theoretical engagement with these concepts; Brazil had no Gorphe equivalent constituting the evidentiary evaluation concept as a theoretical object prior to that engagement.

**Patrimonialism grounds the asymmetry.** Non-patrimonial civilian jurisdictions' proceduralist scholarly traditions engaged free-evaluation concepts theoretically because judicial rationality in those fields was constituted as inherently subject to rational assessment (Type-2), generating scholarly pressure to specify what the concept's rational content positively was. Patrimonial fields whose judicial rationality was constituted as personal authoritative judgment did not generate this pressure toward specifying the concept's internal rational structure; formal legal systematization rather than conceptual constitution characterized their theoretical output.

**O-2's temporal-origin argument** correctly identifies where vocabulary for engaging with the Liebman framework specifically develops (through C3 engagement). It does not describe the prior domestic proceduralist traditions in non-patrimonial fields that constituted the free-evaluation concept as a theoretical object before the Liebman tradition became the dominant vehicle for that engagement. Condition (a) identifies vocabulary for constituting the integration question, not for engaging it through the Liebman framework specifically.

Revised failure condition (o-ii): the response fails if the adversarial establishes that the French *intime conviction* and German *freie Beweiswürdigung* scholarly traditions (i) did not constitute the evidentiary evaluation concept's positive designatum as a theoretical object with internally specifiable rational structure including the accountability dimension, or (ii) were not accessible to Portuguese and Argentine proceduralist scholars as formative vocabulary resources prior to C3.

## What I considered and discarded

**Conceding O-1 entirely.** O-1 has surface force — the disanalogy with good faith is genuine, and the behavioral-proxy formulation is the sharpest version of the M-1 argument to date. But O-1's conclusion (that the trial record is not an independent information source for Art. 116 criterion-satisfaction assessment) does not follow from the disanalogy. The disanalogy shows only that the *type* of independent mechanism differs between good faith and epistemic sensitivity. The Italian appellate doctrinal record (*errore di giudizio*, *omessa valutazione di prove*) is the institutional evidence that logical-structure testing was recognized as a distinct correction path for Art. 116 that did not reduce to the Art. 132 obligation. Conceding O-1 would require conceding that this institutional evidence is unavailing, which O-1 has not established.

**Arguing that the behavioral-proxy disanalogy is simply irrelevant.** The response acknowledges the disanalogy rather than dismissing it — O-1 is correct that good faith and epistemic sensitivity use different operationalization mechanisms. The response contests only that the behavioral-proxy type is the criterion for independent operationalizability. A response that simply denied the disanalogy would miss O-1's genuinely new contribution over M-1 and risk a loop-closure finding on the new formulation.

**Identifying a pre-C3 vocabulary resource that is not field-specific proceduralist formation.** General adjacent-domain Type-2 vocabulary (from administrative law, constitutional jurisprudence) was already the subject of N-2 and O-2. O-2's specific challenge is that condition (a) requires proceduralist-domain constituting vocabulary, not general Type-2. The French and German traditions are the right target because they represent domestic proceduralist scholarly engagement with free evidentiary evaluation concepts as theoretical objects — not merely Type-2 institutional formation at the general judicial rationality level.

**Arguing that O-2's temporal-origin point was already answered by N-2.** N-2 addressed M-2's temporal-origin argument by showing it operated at the level of vocabulary for answering (not constituting) the integration question. O-2 accepts this distinction but presses that the constituting vocabulary also develops through C3 proceduralist engagement. Repeating N-2's response without identifying the specific pre-C3 resource would be the loop-closure condition O-2 itself named. The French/German tradition identification is the required advance.

## Assessment after r17

The Paper 1G debate has now completed clean four-step sequences on each line: M-1/N-1/O-1 → r17 O-1 response (criterion/mechanism → behavioral-proxy gap → logical-structure testing); M-2/N-2/O-2 → r17 O-2 response (adjacent-domain symmetry → Type-1/Type-2 → constituting vocabulary gap → pre-C3 domestic proceduralist traditions).

**Live questions after r17:**

**(O-1/r17 at r18):** The logical-structure testing argument is now the central claim. R18 would need to show either (a) that *errore di giudizio* and *omessa valutazione di prove* doctrines under Art. 116 in fact required the *motivazione* document as the primary information source — making them Art. 132 correction doctrines in substance even when labeled as Art. 116 — or (b) that logical-structure testing specifically cannot operate in the three named target domains (mixed evidence, weight-assignment, narrative reconstruction) because there is no consistent logical relationship between the evidentiary record and rationally possible conclusions in these domains. The second path is the narrowest and most empirically grounded route for r18.

**(O-2/r17 at r18):** The French/German pre-C3 tradition identification is now the specific evidence claim. R18 would need to show either that these traditions did not constitute the evidentiary evaluation concept's positive designatum as a theoretical object with internally specifiable rational structure including the accountability dimension — specifically, that Gorphe's tradition or the post-§ 286 ZPO scholarship treated free evaluation in a way structurally parallel to the patrimonial pattern (formal systematization without conceptual constitution) — or that Portugal and Argentina did not have genuine access to these traditions as formative vocabulary resources prior to C3, making their proceduralist-domain constituting vocabulary also C3-dependent. The latter requires comparative doctrinal formation evidence, which is a testable empirical claim.

**Loop-closure condition for r18:** restating the behavioral-proxy disanalogy at the general level without showing that logical-structure testing specifically fails in the three target domains; or restating the proceduralist-domain constituting vocabulary gap without engaging the French/German pre-C3 tradition claim at the level of what those traditions actually constituted about the evidentiary evaluation concept's positive designatum.
