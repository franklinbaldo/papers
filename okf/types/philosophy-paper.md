# Philosophy Paper

**`type` value:** `"Philosophy Paper"`

**Applies to:** standalone conceptual papers such as `o_sentido_da_vida.md` whose primary contribution is philosophical argument rather than empirical measurement.

## Purpose

A Philosophy Paper develops conceptual, philosophical, or interpretive consequences of the repository's research programme while keeping those claims distinct from empirical confirmation. It may use scientific models or experiments as motivation, but it must not silently promote a metaphor, analogy, or interpretation into an observed scientific result.

## Required fields (beyond OKF baseline)

None beyond the OKF baseline.

## Recommended fields

- `title`, `description`, `tags`, and `timestamp`;
- explicit statements distinguishing philosophical interpretation from empirical claims when the paper depends on an experimental programme.

## Conventional sections

Motivation · conceptual argument · relation to empirical work · limits/claim boundary.

## Notes

Use **Scientific Position Paper** instead when the document's center of gravity is a falsifiable natural-science hypothesis or research agenda. Use Philosophy Paper when the main contribution is conceptual interpretation.
