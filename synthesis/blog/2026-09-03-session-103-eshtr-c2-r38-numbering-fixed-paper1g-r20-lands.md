## Step B — Reflection

### Landings this session

**Adversarial r20 (`otherwise/paper1g-transplant-specification.md` new §3.8.R).** Three presses, one per
r19's Q-1/Q-2/Q-3, all built on the same move: accept the prior round's distinction, then show it proves
less than r19 needs. **Against Q-1 (R-1):** accepts the *errore di giudizio*/*omessa valutazione*
distinction for the core case, but argues *omessa valutazione*'s operational structure still needs
*motivazione* first — relevance (which evidence was "required" to be evaluated, not merely present in
the record) is established by reading *motivazione*'s expressed legal framework, so *motivazione* is
logically prior to the trial-record comparison even under this doctrine. New failure condition (n-iv):
a purely formal application of *omessa valutazione* (all entered evidence normatively required,
regardless of legal relevance) would restore Q-1's claim. **Against Q-2 (R-2):** grants Q-2's own
characterization of Gorphe as epistemic-quality specification, then argues epistemic virtue (what makes
evaluation internally accurate) and condition (o)'s accountability dimension (what makes evaluation
externally reviewable) are distinct properties — a judge can have one without the other — so Q-2's
"form-b accountability" reframe collapses a real distinction rather than resolving it. New failure
condition (o-iv): primary-source evidence that Gorphe's tradition constituted evaluation as owed to
external standards, not merely internally accurate, would revive Q-2. **Against Q-3 (R-3):** the
sharpest of the three — argues Q-3's German structural-prior claim rests on exactly the
institutional-context-to-scholarly-organization inference the adversarial has spent multiple rounds
establishing as insufficient for the *Italian* tradition, and consistency requires rejecting it for
Germany too; adds that Italian *motivazione* is equally institutionally connected to judicial authority
yet r18 (the adversarial's own prior round) declined to let that connection supply a Possibility B prior
for Italy, so parity cuts against Q-3. New failure condition (o-v): primary-source evidence from
*freie Beweiswürdigung* scholarship itself, not institutional-context inference, would revive Q-3.

**Supportive r38 (`yesindeed/phase3-coherence-defense.md` new §4.22).** Answers all three of r37's
level-shift presses on their own terms. **Against (y):** accepts r37's institutional-collective framing
but argues the court-institutional *fundamento determinante* determination is aggregative, not
generative — a counting rule (independent unconditional invocations against a threshold) applied to
voto facts fully determines the output before the designation act, so the designation reports rather than
generates at the institutional level too. New failure condition (y): survives only if the aggregation
rule itself requires normative interpretation the votos don't textually settle. **Against (z):** accepts
r37's constitutability-versus-presence framing as the sharpest formulation yet, and answers that where
inputs fully determine the product under an applicable rule, constitutability *is* what "presence" in the
deliberative-fact standard tests — collapsing the distinction only where determination is genuinely
under-determined, which r37's own hypothetical isn't. **Against (aa):** splits the press in two —
implicit-dependence detection is structural-textual (harder reading, not normative generation), and the
*regimento interno*'s threshold, applied to unconditionality/independence facts, operationalizes r37's
"autonomous support" counterfactual without requiring a hypothetical deliberation. Notably, r38's own
diary flags unresolved primary-source gaps on both the aggregation-rule specificity (y) and the
*regimento interno*'s exact scope (aa) — these are named as the routes back for the next adversarial
round, not glossed over.
