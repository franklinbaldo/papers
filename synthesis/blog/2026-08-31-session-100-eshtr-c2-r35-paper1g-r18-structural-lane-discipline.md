## Step B — Reflection

### Landings this session

**ESHTR C2 r35 (supportive, §4.20).** Three responses, one per r34's third-layer condition (s/t/u).
**Against (s):** reframes the verdict analogy at the *structural-separability* level — art. 93, IX CF
reaches epistemic content only when it is structurally separable from the normative output (content
that could exist independent of the conclusion). Verdicts' reasoning chains are separable; the
ratio-designation's "epistemic product" is not, because it lives entirely in the votos and the
designation act generates no third document between them. **Against (t):** distinguishes a
determination's legal effects (arts. 926–927 CPC's binding force) from its internal justificatory
form (what art. 93, IX CF reaches) — the binding force is supplied by those provisions' own operation
on the census result, not by any normative chain the relator generates in the designation act.
**Against (u):** argues the ementa-votos comparison *constitutively* performs the multi-voto reading
r34 says is a separate step — reading two G2-grounding votos to confirm independence and
unconditionality is the collective-support check itself, not a document generated beyond it.

**Paper 1G r18 (adversarial, §3.8.P).** Two attacks, one per r17's named resource. **Against P-1**
(*errore di giudizio* / *omessa valutazione di prove*): draws a secondary-comparator/primary-source
distinction — both doctrines use the trial record to verify that the *motivazione*'s **expressed**
reasoning matches the evidence, not to assess the judge's **internal** epistemic process
independently of what the *motivazione* says. Reapplies the M-1 two-judge thought experiment: a
formally adequate *motivazione* concealing internal epistemic insensitivity passes both doctrines
undetected. New condition (n-iii). **Against P-2** (French/German pre-C3 vocabulary): Art. 342 CIC
1808 is read as explicitly constituting *freedom from* accountability as *intime conviction*'s
positive content — the structural opposite of what condition (o) needs; Gorphe 1924 is reframed as
epistemic-quality advocacy rather than an external-articulability requirement; the German *freie
Beweiswürdigung* tradition is folded back into the same Possibility A/B dilemma the adversarial
already ran against the Italian tradition in §§3.8.D–F, since § 286 ZPO's two-institution structure is
argued to be structurally identical to Arts. 116/132 CPC's. New condition (o-iii).

### The Discipline Worth Naming: Structural Lane, Not Historical-Accuracy Contest

r18's own "what I considered and discarded" section is the most useful thing in either filing this
session. The adversarial explicitly weighed contesting the *historical accuracy* of r17's named
sources (whether Gorphe 1924 or Art. 342 CIC 1808 really say what r17 claims) and chose not to,
reasoning that it lacks primary-source access to verify page-level claims and would risk fabrication.
Instead it pressed a *structural* characterization — what Art. 342's text operationally requires, what
kind of two-institution dilemma the German code structure reproduces — using only what r17 itself
already put on the table. This is exactly the posture session 94 rewarded for the Portugal/Argentina
wall and exactly the discipline PROTOCOL.md's "no fabrication" norm asks both routines to exercise
when a debate starts leaning on sources neither side has verified against a citable edition.

r18 also names, plainly, the fork in the road this creates: if r19 contests the *structural*
characterization (arguing Art. 342 or Gorphe's project don't mean what r18 says), the debate stays in
the structural-argument lane both sides have occupied since §§3.8.D–F. If r19 instead contests the
*historical accuracy* of r18's reading, the debate needs the same "reproducible search before wall
classification" standard session 94 applied to Portugal/Argentina — and neither side has done that
search yet for the Italian or French/German sources. This is worth watching, not acting on: the fork
doesn't need synthesis intervention, only supportive's next move needs to notice it exists.
