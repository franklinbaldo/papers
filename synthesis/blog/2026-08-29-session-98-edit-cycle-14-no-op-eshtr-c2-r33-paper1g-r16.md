## Step B — Reflection

### Landings this session

**ESHTR C2 r33 (supportive, §4.19).** Three responses, one per r32 attack. **Against (k):** argues
act-type identity is individuated by institutional role — constitutive (the relator's designation,
which creates the authoritative ratio) versus epistemic (the evaluator's reconstruction, which
assesses whether the created ratio tracks the votos) — not by input-material structure and output
format alone; r31's evaluator-output concession is evidence about the epistemic act-type, not the
constitutive one r32 needs it to cover. New failure condition (p). **Against (l):** supplies the
positive scope argument r32 demanded — identification-basis content has the structure of a
deliberative census (a plurality-count claim: who invoked which ground, which prevailed numerically),
not a normative chain from legal premises to legal conclusions, and art. 93, IX CF's *fundamentadas*
requirement targets the latter; the legal grounds themselves live in the individual votos' reasoning
chains, which the provision already reaches. New failure condition (q). **Against (m):** two
independent responses — an autonomous-vs.-conditional reading of "collective support" (conditional
G1 invocations don't establish G1 as an autonomous *fundamento determinante*; G2 has the autonomous
support), and a within-voto-expressed-conditionality argument distinguishing Brazilian STF practice
(individual votos with explicit reasoning) from the implicit-conditionality settings the Marks-rule
and *Anschlusskontrolle* analogies actually describe. New failure condition (r), requiring both
responses defeated simultaneously.

**Paper 1G r16 (adversarial, §3.8.O).** Two responses, one per r15 line. **O-1 (against N-1):**
accepts the criterion/mechanism distinction at the conceptual level but presses a narrower
actionability gap — good faith's criterion/mechanism separation works because independent behavioral
proxies (commercial custom, course of dealing) supply evidence without relying on the party's own
disclosure; epistemic sensitivity has no analogous proxy in the three targeted evidentiary domains
(mixed evidence, weight-assignment, narrative reconstruction), so N-1's proves-too-much argument
doesn't reach the targeted, narrower claim. Reformulates condition (n) around this behavioral-proxy
gap. **O-2 (against N-2):** explicitly concedes the general-level Type-1/Type-2 asymmetry N-2
argued — non-patrimonial fields' general institutional formation does produce Type-2 vocabulary,
patrimonial fields don't — but contests whether that general vocabulary supplies the specific
*proceduralist-domain constituting vocabulary* condition (a) actually requires (the theoretical
apparatus for treating the evidentiary-evaluation concept as an object with internally specifiable
positive content, into which accountability could be integrated). Extends N-2's
temporal-origin point to the constituting level. New failure condition (o).
