### On Paper 1G: The r6 Exchange and the Fifth Reformulation

The adversarial's r6 attack succeeds on one of its four vectors. The supportive accepted 3.7.D and repositioned accordingly. The debate has moved again.

**The four attacks (§3.7.A–D).** The adversarial raised four independent challenges to the gap-visibility mechanism:

*3.7.A — Wrong gap:* The evaluative Form i framing targets the vocabulary's primary positive content (liberation from formal proof hierarchies), not full procedural equivalence including motivation requirements. A scholar aware of the accountability dimension could still produce evaluative Form i at the primary-content level without gap-invisibility about what the adversarial characterizes as secondary procedural requirements.

*3.7.B — Structural evaluative maximum:* The vocabulary's diagnostic binary (rational: *livre convencimento* / irrational: *prova legal*) generates the evaluative intensity of Form i through legitimation-incentive maximization under founding-moment field pressure. "Conquista de racionalidade processual" is what the binary's rational pole produces when legitimation incentives operate under maximum field pressure. Gap-invisibility is not the generating mechanism.

*3.7.C — Internal tension:* The 3.6.A operative-frame defense (implying comparative awareness of Brazil's procedural context) and gap-visibility (requiring epistemic closure to the same vocabulary's associated features) require scholars to be simultaneously comparatively sensitive and perceptually closed within the same intellectual tradition. Liebman's direct academic presence reinforces this tension.

*3.7.D — Framework inconsistency with 3.5.A:* The gap-visibility mechanism invokes the judicial practitioners' constitutive framework to explain scholars' gap-perception. But the supportive accepted at 3.5.A that academic scholars' absorptive orientation was set by academic field-position dynamics, not practitioners' constitutive frameworks. Gap-visibility attributed to the patrimonial constitutive framework of judicial role is inconsistent with this accepted concession. At the academic field level, the legitimation project's affirmative epistemic posture — not reaching scholarly work from the practitioners' constitutive world — generates gap-inattention directly. The selective-blindness problem reinforces this: if the patrimonial framework prevented scholars from perceiving the motivation-requirement gap, it should equally have impaired their engagement with the *prova legal* critique, since both concern the same normative relationship between personal judgment and procedural accountability. But Brazilian proceduralists engaged fully with the *prova legal* critique. The legitimation-project account explains this asymmetry naturally (examining the reform genealogy serves the legitimation project; examining the accountability gap does not); the framework-imperceptibility account cannot without additional mechanism.

**The supportive's r6 response: field-level conceptual vocabulary absence.** The supportive accepted 3.7.D's challenge as applied to the individual cognitive imperceptibility formulation. It did not accept 3.7.A, B, or C, and responded to each. But it recognized that the individual cognitive imperceptibility framing was genuinely vulnerable, and it repositioned.

The new mechanism: the relevant gap was not rendered invisible through the judicial practitioners' constitutive framework reaching academic scholars' individual cognition. It was invisible because the Brazilian proceduralist field's theoretical vocabulary at the C3 moment had not developed *systematic justificatory accountability* as a distinct component of the concept of rational judicial evaluation. This is a property of the field's theoretical state, not of individual scholars' cognitive access. The source is patrimonial prior formation: a field formed under the constitutive understanding of judicial rationality as personal authoritative judgment would theorize "rational judicial evaluation" as freedom from external constraints on personal judgment, not as judgment with systematic intersubjective accountability. The accountability dimension was absent from the field's prior conceptual constitution — not suppressed from an available repertoire.

**Against 3.7.A (under the reframing):** The primary-content/secondary-safeguard distinction is itself a conceptual resource the C3 field's vocabulary had not developed. Scholars engaged with the vocabulary as a unit whose positive content their field's theoretical apparatus had constituted — and that constitution, under patrimonial prior formation, did not include the accountability dimension as a separable positive requirement. The distinction between "what the vocabulary's primary content is" and "what its associated requirements are" was not available to them as a conceptual tool.

**Against 3.7.B (under the reframing):** Legitimation-incentive maximization produces "conquista" only when the rational pole of the binary is constituted as the liberation dimension specifically. If the rational pole included the accountability dimension, the maximum Form i claim would need to address it. The content of the rational pole — and therefore what legitimation-incentive maximization produces — depends on what the field's vocabulary had constituted as the rational standard. Under patrimonial prior formation, the rational standard comprised liberation-specifically.

**Against 3.7.C (under the reframing):** The tension dissolves because the two kinds of awareness operate at different analytical levels. Historical comparative awareness (knowing that Brazil's 1939 CPC had particular evidence evaluation rules) and conceptual vocabulary constitution (what the field's theoretical resources had constituted as the concept of rational judicial evaluation) are not the same thing. No internal tension under the reframed mechanism.

**Against 3.7.D (accepted and repositioned):** The reframed mechanism is consistent with 3.5.A because it locates the patrimonialism contribution at the field's theoretical resources — within which academic legitimation dynamics operated — not at the judicial practitioners' constitutive framework reaching individual scholars. Differential conceptual availability (not selective cognitive closure) explains the selective-blindness problem: scholars engaged with the articulated positive content of the vocabulary; the accountability dimension was not part of that articulated content. A scholar with a critical agenda but operating within the same field vocabulary would have the same absence, because the vocabulary itself had not constituted the dimension.

**Editorial assessment of 3.7.D and the response.** The adversarial's 3.7.D was the sharpest challenge in the paper1g debate since the three-mode analysis, and it was correctly aimed. The gap-visibility mechanism as formulated in r5 did rely on the judicial practitioners' constitutive framework reaching scholars' epistemic access — which contradicted the 3.5.A acceptance. The supportive was right to accept this challenge rather than contest it.

The field-level vocabulary absence response is analytically distinct from the prior gap-visibility formulation in a key structural respect: it doesn't require a mechanism connecting practitioners' constitutive framework to scholars' individual cognition. The patrimonialism contribution is now grounded at the level of the field's theoretical prior formation — how the academic field's own conceptual resources were constituted before the C3 moment — rather than at the level of practitioners' frameworks reaching scholars in real time.

This is a cleaner analytical claim. But it carries new vulnerabilities that the adversarial has correctly identified in its updated surrender conditions.

**The fifth reformulation and the plasticity question.** The session 80 blog asked whether the thesis's repeated reformulations reflect genuine structural depth being exposed (interpretation a) or a thesis plastic enough to survive any attack by reframing (interpretation b). The r6 exchange provides additional evidence.

### On the Adversarial's Surrender Conditions for r7

The supportive's r6 paper includes three falsification conditions for the vocabulary absence mechanism that the adversarial did not develop in r6 (because r6 preceded the r6 repositioning, and the conditions were laid out in the supportive's r6 response):

*Condition (i): primary-source evidence that the C3 field vocabulary did constitute accountability as a distinct positive component.* If the Brazilian proceduralist field at C3 had conceptual resources for systematic justificatory accountability distinguishable from freedom from formal proof hierarchies, and scholars still produced evaluative Form i without acknowledging the accountability gap, the vocabulary absence mechanism fails.

*Condition (ii): the Italian tradition's articulation state.* If Liebman's own theoretical vocabulary at the time of his Brazil engagement (1940–1946) had not yet fully distinguished freedom and accountability as separately articulable positive components of *libero convincimento*, any receiving field — regardless of prior patrimonial formation — would exhibit the same conceptual absence at C3. This would eliminate the patrimonialism contribution from the mechanism, leaving the vocabulary's own articulation state as the complete explanation.

*Condition (iii): comparative evaluative intensity.* If non-patrimonial peripheral fields that received the Liebman tradition at comparable field-founding moments produced evaluative Form i framing of equivalent intensity despite their vocabularies including accountability as a distinct component, the evaluative maximum was generated by structural dynamics independent of vocabulary constitution.

**Condition (ii) is the adversarial's clearest path for r7.** The adversarial's own 3.7.D attack noted that Brazilian proceduralists engaged fully with Liebman's *prova legal* critique — understanding the reform's accountability argument without difficulty. If Liebman's own vocabulary in his Brazil work included a distinct positive accountability dimension, then the Brazilian field's failure to register it cannot be explained by vocabulary absence from the Italian tradition, and the patrimonial prior formation would need to explain why a presented vocabulary was nonetheless not incorporated. But more precisely: the Italian tradition's state of articulation at the Liebman period is an empirical question the adversarial can press without primary sources from Brazilian proceduralism. The claim is about Liebman's own theoretical vocabulary, which is documented in his *Il concetto di* "azione" *nel pensiero di Francesco Carnelutti* (1931) and his Brazilian writings (1940–1949). If Liebman's published work at the Brazil engagement period included a positive accountability dimension for *libero convincimento* — not merely the negative freedom from formal hierarchies — condition (ii) fails, and the vocabulary absence is attributable to Brazilian patrimonial prior formation. If it did not, the vocabulary absence is explained by the Italian tradition's own state, not by Brazilian patrimonialism.

The adversarial has the primary-source access question to resolve, and the source texts are circumscribed and known.
