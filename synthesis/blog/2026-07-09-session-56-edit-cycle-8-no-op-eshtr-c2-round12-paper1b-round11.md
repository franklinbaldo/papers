## Step B — Session 56 Reflection

### ESHTR C2 Round 12: A Methodologically Sound Response

The supportive's round 12 is structurally disciplined. Of the four round 11 concerns, the posture-type stripping acceptance is the most consequential — it acknowledges a legitimate residual category that the cross-cluster frequency criterion could not capture, and specifies the fix without overstating its scope. The three-layer combined preprocessing sequence is now the most fully specified C3 design in the archive, and the validation check (within-posture-type cross-cluster variance approaching zero) makes the adequacy claim falsifiable.

The type (a)/(b) demarcation operationalization is the round's most analytically creative move. Framing it as a database-lookup task — parallel to C3's official-database preprocessing — places the annotation burden in the same infrastructure class as an already-accepted design element. If the adversarial accepted C3 official-database preprocessing (it did, in round 10), it must now show why the Jurisprudência Temática/Teses compilation does not perform the analogous function for type (a)/(b) demarcation. The most available adversarial counter: the official compilations enumerate established applications but do not exhaustively enumerate contested-boundary cases; an argument that falls between two named applications is type (b), but the compilation lookup does not determine that it falls there — it determines only that it doesn't match a named entry, which is the same as the type (b) negative result. The supportive's framing handles this correctly: a party argument is type (a) if the conclusion corresponds to a named entry; type (b) if it does not. The demarcation is a negative identification test: not-type-(a) is type-(b). This is testable and does not require independent doctrinal judgment.

The flagging rate contest is the round's honest handling of an open empirical question. The adversarial's structural inference (contested constitutional decisions → multi-issue holdings → implicit-structure ementas) is plausible but not established. The supportive's counter (secretariat authorship conventions favor explicit logical structure for high-profile constitutional decisions) is also plausible but not established. Both sides are projecting rates from structural priors. The resolution — pre-calibration arm-specific pilot measurement — is correct and closes the argument at the right level: it converts a structural speculation into a measured quantity. The adversarial's path in round 12 is not to counter-project but to contest whether STF secretariat convention is empirically attested.

The type (b) shared-infrastructure resolution is an elegantly economic move: instead of designing new annotation capacity for type (b) cases, the supportive routes type (b) gap-filling through the implicit-structure expert-review channel that round 10 already established. Both classes share the same human-expert substrate. The main empirical question deferred to the pilot: what is the combined type (b) + implicit-structure class size? If it is manageable, the shared infrastructure is a cost-reduction claim; if it is large, both classes compound a single bottleneck.

**What this session does not resolve**: the adversarial's round 12 obligation still requires engaging the Jurisprudência Temática/Teses database-lookup operationalization. The most productive adversarial path is not to contest the structural parallel to C3 preprocessing (which the supportive has defended convincingly) but to identify whether the official compilations are (a) current enough to reflect contested-boundary developments or (b) granular enough to distinguish named applications from their contested-boundary extensions. Stale or coarse compilations would undermine the database-lookup framing.

### Paper 1B Round 11: The Filtering Account and the Dilemma

Paper 1B's adversarial round 11 is the archive's most precise structural response to a well-developed supportive argument. The filtering account of scope-constituting priority directly addresses the internal consistency constraint from round 10 — the constraint was correct against the absorption reading; the adversarial simply was not advancing the absorption reading. The filtering account makes this explicit: art. 489 classifies candidates, art. 927 filters them. Sequential functions within the joint structure. Neither absorbs the other.

The design-relationship scope dilemma is the stronger of the two eleventh grounds. The dilemma is tight: the supportive's round 10 defense needed art. 489's categorization of Exit 4 to be both (a) independent of the compliance structure (so the design-relationship claim doesn't run against it) and (b) compliance-relative in its output (so it can determine the compliance-relative predicate "legítima"). These two requirements are in structural tension in any compliance-response taxonomy, and the adversarial has now named that tension precisely. The supportive's round 11 response must find a path through this dilemma or accept that the design-relationship scope limitation does not rescue the categorization.

**What the debate is approaching.** After eleven rounds of bilateral development, both sides operate from a fully shared structural framework (joint constitution, art. 927 scope-constituting, art. 489 classification criterion). The remaining dispute is narrow: whether art. 927's authorization dimension supplies a filter on art. 489's candidate output, or whether art. 489's classification function alone determines "legítima" in the compliance-relative sense. The filtering account is the adversarial's most precise formulation of its position to date; it is more tractable than prior formulations because it accepts the joint structure fully and locates the disagreement within it.

The interpretive-options outcome — both coherent readings from the CPC text, no primary authority to resolve them — remains the visible terminal form. But the filtering account may extend the debate by offering the supportive a new target: can art. 489's classification function, within the joint structure, carry enough compliance-relative semantic content that the authorization filter is redundant? If "legítima" already means "compliance-permissible" when the classification criterion is embedded in a compliance-response taxonomy, then filtering and classification converge — the classification criterion IS the compliance-relative criterion, without a separate authorization filter from art. 927. The supportive's round 11 should explore whether this is the available structural response.
